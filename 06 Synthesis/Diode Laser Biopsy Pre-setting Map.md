# Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** Built only from the vault's currently processed oral soft-tissue biopsy literature and its existing evidence/synthesis notes: [[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield|Angiero et al. 2011]], [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data|Romeo et al. 2014]], [[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data|Azevedo et al. 2016]], [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]], [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]], [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]]. Two sources are cited as supporting-only, non-biopsy background where directly relevant (tip-initiation, CW/PW): [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth|Romanos et al. 2022]] and [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters|Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. 2020]] — neither is an oral soft-tissue biopsy study and neither is included as a row in the mapping table below. Capodiferro et al. 2008 and Suter et al. 2010 remain unprocessed (no full-text literature note in `02 Literature/`) and are excluded. No new literature was searched for this note.

**No "optimal setting" is claimed anywhere in this note.** Per vault instruction, only "evidence-supported range" is used, and only where the evidence directly supports it — see [[#9. Whether any evidence-supported pre-setting range can currently be proposed]].

## 1. Preset ranges associated with acceptable biopsy quality

- FACT: The three sources with a quantified diagnostic-yield outcome at or near 100% — [[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield|Angiero et al. 2011]] (100% at ≥4 mm), [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data|Romeo et al. 2014]] (100%, diode arm 2 W CW), and [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]] (100%, 2 W CW) — all used **continuous-wave mode**, an 808 or 980 nm diode, a fiber diameter of 300–320 μm, and a set power within **1.6–2.7 W**.
- INTERPRETATION: This vault's evidence is consistent with an evidence-supported CW set-power range of roughly **1.6–2.7 W** (808/980 nm, 300–320 μm fiber) being compatible with acceptable-to-100% diagnostic yield, *provided* specimen size/margin was ≥4–5 mm in the same studies. This is not evidence that the power range itself is the cause of acceptable quality — see [[04 Evidence/Biopsy Specimen Quality]], which already concludes that specimen size/margin, not power, is the variable most consistently associated with diagnostic outcome across these same three sources.
- FACT: [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]] used a higher set power (3 W, both CW and PW) and reports only a qualitative statement of correct diagnosis, not a quantified percentage — this cannot extend the 1.6–2.7 W range above with the same evidentiary strength.
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: No source in this table varies power alone while holding specimen size/margin, wavelength, and fiber diameter constant, so the 1.6–2.7 W range above should be read as "the range in which acceptable outcomes were observed," not as a range validated as causally sufficient or necessary for acceptable quality.
- Ex vivo sources ([[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data|Azevedo et al. 2016]], [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]]) render no diagnosis at all and therefore cannot contribute to a "diagnostic quality"-anchored range; their power values (3.5 W PW; 2–4 W CW/PW) are noted separately below as thermal-damage data only, not diagnostic-quality data.

## 2. Parameters associated with greater thermal artifact

- FACT: Within [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters|Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. 2020]] (non-biopsy background source), lost-tissue area, thermal-damage area, and necrosis area were all higher at 2.0 W set power than at 0.5 W, regardless of wavelength.
- FACT: Within [[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data|Azevedo et al. 2016]], the higher-intensity "Boost" PW setting produced greater ETTD (626.82 μm) than the standard PW setting at the same nominal 3.5 W label (456.15 μm) — a within-device gradient with increasing pulsed-setting intensity.
- FACT: Within [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]], raw thermal-damage minima/maxima increased with power (epithelium 0.62 mm at 2 W CW up to 3.12 mm at 4 W PW), but the source's own formal statistical test of power level alone (pooling CW/PW, 2/3/4 W) found **no significant difference** in either epithelial (p=0.765) or connective-tissue (p=0.652) damage.
- INTERPRETATION: The association between higher power/intensity setting and greater thermal-damage magnitude is weak and inconsistently supported — raw values trend upward with power in two sources, but the only source that formally tested power across three levels with a significance test found no significant effect. This vault cannot state a general power → thermal-damage-severity curve for biopsy-relevant diode settings.
- CONFIRMED within-source conflict (preserved, not resolved): in Azevedo et al., the setting with *more* ETTD (Boost, 626.82 μm) had *lower* carbonization (1.80) than the setting with *less* ETTD (standard PW, 456.15 μm, carbonization 2.50) — greater linear thermal-damage extent did not track with greater carbonization within this device.
- INTERPRETATION: Mode (CW vs. PW) cannot currently be listed as a parameter reliably "associated with greater thermal artifact" in either direction — see [[#4. CW vs PW patterns]] below for the unresolved directional conflict.

## 3. Parameters associated with better margin readability

- FACT: Specimen size/excision margin ≥4–5 mm is the parameter most consistently associated with margin/diagnostic readability across independent sources: [[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield|Angiero et al. 2011]] (empirically derived, size-graded), [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]] (a priori 5 mm, 100% success), and [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]] (independently recommends ≥5 mm at ≤3 W).
- FACT: [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]] reports better pathologist-graded slide quality for PW than CW at matched 3 W, in a real human biopsy pathway — the only source in this table that directly scores "slide quality" (as distinct from diagnosis or thermal-damage width) by mode.
- FACT: Fiber diameter is 300–320 μm in every source that reports it (Angiero et al., Romeo et al., Gambino et al., Pergolini et al.); Azevedo et al. and Gundlapalle et al. do not report it. No source tests a different fiber diameter as a comparator, so fiber diameter's association with margin readability cannot be evaluated — it is invariant, not tested.
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: Tip-initiation status is UNKNOWN in five of the six core biopsy sources (only Pergolini et al. reports it, as "initiated," with no non-initiated comparator within that same study). No biopsy source in this vault can currently support a claim linking tip-initiation status to margin readability.

## 4. CW vs PW patterns

- CONFIRMED CONTRADICTION (full detail in [[06 Synthesis/Contradictions in Diode Laser Biopsy Evidence]]): [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]] (real human biopsy, matched 3 W) found PW causes less thermal damage than CW (208.766 μm vs. 279.84 μm) and better slide quality; [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]] (ex vivo, pooled 2/3/4 W) found the opposite, non-significant trend (PW numerically greater than CW for both tissue compartments).
- FACT: [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data|Romeo et al. 2014]]'s diode-CW-vs-KTP-PW comparison and [[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data|Azevedo et al. 2016]]'s PW-vs-Boost-PW comparison are **not usable** as CW-vs-PW evidence — the former confounds mode with wavelength/laser type, the latter has no CW arm at all.
- INTERPRETATION: This vault cannot currently state a general direction of effect for CW vs. PW mode on thermal artifact or margin quality in dental-diode oral biopsy. The two genuine within-mode comparisons (Gundlapalle et al., Pergolini et al.) disagree, and neither reports a quantified diagnostic-yield percentage, so neither mode can be linked to diagnostic outcome directly.

## 5. Fiber/tip-related patterns

- FACT: Tip-initiation status is UNKNOWN in five of six core biopsy sources (Angiero et al., Romeo et al., Azevedo et al., Gambino et al., Gundlapalle et al.); only [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]] reports it ("initiated"), without a non-initiated comparator in the same study.
- FACT: Contact mode is reported as "contact" in Azevedo et al., Gambino et al., and Pergolini et al.; UNKNOWN in Angiero et al., Romeo et al., and Gundlapalle et al.
- FACT (supporting, non-biopsy source, reused for context only): [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth|Romanos et al. 2022]] directly tested initiated vs. non-initiated tips and found a within-source inconsistency — its Abstract states initiated tips showed *lower* penetration depth than non-initiated, while its own Table 1 and Discussion show the opposite wherever a significant difference was found. This is preserved, not resolved, and cannot be used to fill the tip-initiation gap in the biopsy literature itself.
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: No processed oral soft-tissue biopsy source in this vault directly compares initiated vs. non-initiated tips against a biopsy-relevant outcome (thermal damage, margin readability, diagnostic yield). This is a confirmed reporting gap, not a resolved-but-unfavorable finding.
- INTERPRETATION: Fiber diameter (300–320 μm where reported) is uniform enough across sources that it cannot explain any of the disagreements identified in this note — it is not a candidate variable for the CW/PW or margin-recommendation conflicts, since it does not vary meaningfully between the disagreeing sources.

## 6. Specimen-size effects

- FACT: Diagnostic failure is sharply size-graded in [[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield|Angiero et al. 2011]]: 63.63% unreadable at 2 mm, 37.71% at 3 mm, 0% unreadable at ≥4 mm (n=608).
- FACT: [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data|Romeo et al. 2014]] (5–10 mm lesions) and [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]] (standardized 5 mm margin) both report 100% diagnostic success, independently converging with Angiero et al.'s ≥4–5 mm threshold.
- INTERPRETATION: Specimen size/margin (≥4–5 mm) is the single strongest and most consistent parameter-to-outcome relationship anywhere in this table — stronger and more consistent than any power, mode, or fiber-related pattern identified above. This reproduces the standing conclusion of [[04 Evidence/Biopsy Specimen Quality]] and [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Quality]].
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: No source varies specimen size and power/mode independently in the same design, so the relative contribution of size vs. power/mode to diagnostic outcome remains untested.

## 7. Where studies disagree

- CW-vs-PW thermal-damage direction: [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]] (PW < CW) vs. [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]] (PW > CW, not significant) — unresolved, full detail in [[06 Synthesis/Contradictions in Diode Laser Biopsy Evidence]].
- Recommended safety margin: ≥5 mm ([[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield|Angiero et al. 2011]], [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]], [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]]) vs. ≥2 mm ([[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]], ex vivo, no diagnosis rendered) — genuinely different recommendations from different evidentiary bases, not treated as interchangeable.
- Within-source conflict: [[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data|Azevedo et al. 2016]]'s ETTD and carbonization score move in opposite directions between its two pulsed settings.
- NEEDS VERIFICATION (preserved from source data extraction): [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]]'s own narrative text ("t=1.94, p<0.01") and its own Table 2 (p=0.084) disagree for the Group A vs. Group B (2 W CW vs. 2 W PW) epithelial-damage comparison; this vault does not resolve the discrepancy.

## 8. Which parameters are too poorly reported to evaluate

- **Incision speed** — UNKNOWN in all six core biopsy sources. No source reports it at all; it cannot be evaluated as a parameter in this vault under any circumstance currently.
- **Tip-initiation status** — UNKNOWN in five of six core sources; only Pergolini et al. reports a value, and without a within-study comparator.
- **Pulse duration and duty cycle** — UNKNOWN in every source that uses a pulsed setting (Azevedo et al., Gundlapalle et al., Pergolini et al.); only Pergolini et al. reports frequency (10 kHz).
- **Coagulation zone** — not scored or measured as an isolated variable in any of the six core sources; only qualitative, non-numeric descriptions exist (Angiero et al.: vascular/thrombosis descriptors; Gambino et al.: "hemocoagulative phenomena"; Gundlapalle et al.: physics-mechanism description only). Capodiferro et al. 2008 remains the only identified candidate that scores coagulation specifically, and it is unprocessed.
- **Calibration method** — UNKNOWN in all six core sources; no source describes a laser power-meter calibration protocol.
- **Measured/delivered power** — unavailable ("No") in all six core sources, confirmed in the mapping table above; see [[#Set power vs delivered power caveat]] below.
- **Contact/non-contact mode** and **fiber diameter** — reported in roughly half the sources each; not missing entirely, but too inconsistently reported across sources to support a cross-study comparison.

## 9. Whether any evidence-supported pre-setting range can currently be proposed

- INTERPRETATION: A narrow, heavily caveated evidence-supported range can be stated for **CW mode only**: **1.6–2.7 W set power, 808 or 980 nm diode, 300–320 μm fiber**, was the range used by the three sources achieving acceptable-to-100% quantified diagnostic yield in this vault ([[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield|Angiero et al. 2011]], [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data|Romeo et al. 2014]], [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]]) — but this is contingent on specimen size/margin ≥4–5 mm in the same studies, not isolated to power, and none of the three independently measured delivered power. This is an **evidence-supported range**, not an optimal setting.
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: This range should not be extended to PW mode. No PW-mode source in this vault reports a quantified diagnostic-yield percentage; the two PW-relevant sources (Gundlapalle et al., Pergolini et al.) disagree on direction of thermal effect and use different power levels (3 W matched vs. 2–4 W crossed).
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: This range should not be extended to devices, wavelengths, or fiber diameters outside those directly tested (808/980 nm, 300–320 μm) — no source in this table tests a fiber diameter outside this narrow band, so its independent contribution is untested, not confirmed favorable.
- What prevents a broader or firmer range from being proposed: (1) specimen size/margin, not power, is the variable most consistently associated with diagnostic outcome, so the power range above is correlational, not isolated as causal; (2) no source independently measures delivered power, so "1.6–2.7 W" refers only to console settings, which the vault's separate power-output evidence shows can deviate from actual tissue-incident power by a device-dependent and currently unknown amount for every device in this table (see [[04 Evidence/Power Output and Tissue Effect]], [[04 Evidence/Set Power vs Measured Output]]); (3) tip-initiation status, incision speed, pulse duration, duty cycle, and calibration method are UNKNOWN across nearly all sources, so their interaction with the proposed range cannot be ruled in or out; (4) the CW-vs-PW and margin-recommendation contradictions mean no single range can currently cover both modes.
- This synthesis draws no clinical recommendation. The evidence-supported range stated above describes what has been observed in this vault's processed literature, not a recommended clinical setting.

## Set power vs delivered power caveat

- FACT: All values in the "Set/preset power" column above are console/panel values as reported by each source; none is independently verified by a calibrated power meter at the point of tissue contact. This distinction is preserved throughout this note per [[04 Evidence/Set Power vs Measured Output]] and [[04 Evidence/Power Loss and Deviation Across Dental Diode Devices]], which show that measured/delivered output deviates from set power in a device-dependent, non-uniform way (both magnitude and direction vary by device) for every device independently tested in this vault — none of which overlaps with any device in the mapping table above. See [[06 Synthesis/Contradictions in Diode Laser Biopsy Evidence]] for the full argument.

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- บันทึกนี้เป็น **PROVISIONAL** (ผลชั่วคราว) สร้างจากวรรณกรรมเรื่อง biopsy เนื้อเยื่ออ่อนในช่องปากที่ประมวลผลแล้วในคลังนี้เท่านั้น 6 ฉบับ **ไม่มีการอ้างว่ามี "ค่าตั้งที่เหมาะสมที่สุด (optimal setting)" ในบันทึกนี้** — ใช้คำว่า "evidence-supported range" แทนตลอดทั้งบันทึก
- รูปแบบที่พบซ้ำมากที่สุดคือ: การศึกษา 3 ฉบับที่วินิจฉัยได้เกือบหรือครบ 100% (Angiero, Romeo, Gambino) ล้วนใช้โหมดต่อเนื่อง (CW) เลเซอร์ไดโอด 808/980 nm เส้นใย 300–320 ไมครอน และกำลังที่ตั้ง 1.6–2.7 W — แต่ทั้งสามการศึกษานี้ยังใช้ขอบตัด/ขนาดชิ้นเนื้อ ≥4–5 มม. ร่วมด้วยเสมอ ทำให้ไม่สามารถแยกผลของ "กำลังไฟ" ออกจากผลของ "ขนาดชิ้นเนื้อ" ได้อย่างชัดเจน
- ช่วงกำลังไฟ 1.6–2.7 W (CW) จึงเป็นเพียง **evidence-supported range** ไม่ใช่ค่าที่เหมาะสมที่สุด และไม่ควรขยายไปใช้กับโหมดพัลส์ (PW) เนื่องจากยังมีความขัดแย้งเรื่องทิศทางผลของ PW เทียบ CW ที่ยังไม่คลี่คลาย
- พารามิเตอร์ที่รายงานไม่เพียงพอที่จะประเมินได้เลย ได้แก่ ความเร็วในการตัด (incision speed) — ไม่มีการรายงานในทุกการศึกษา, สถานะปลายไฟเบอร์ (initiated/non-initiated) — ไม่ทราบใน 5 จาก 6 การศึกษา, และ coagulation zone — ไม่มีการวัดแยกเป็นตัวแปรของตัวเองในการศึกษาใดเลย
- ทุกการศึกษารายงานเฉพาะค่ากำลังที่ตั้งเครื่อง (set power) เท่านั้น ไม่มีการวัดกำลังขาออกจริงด้วยเพาเวอร์มิเตอร์ในการศึกษาใดเลย และไม่มีอุปกรณ์ใดในตารางนี้ที่ตรงกับอุปกรณ์ที่มีข้อมูลกำลังขาออกจริงในคลังนี้ ดังนั้นช่วงค่าที่เสนอจึงอ้างอิงเฉพาะค่าตั้งเครื่อง ไม่ใช่กำลังที่ส่งถึงเนื้อเยื่อจริง
- บันทึกนี้ไม่มีข้อเสนอแนะทางคลินิกใด ๆ

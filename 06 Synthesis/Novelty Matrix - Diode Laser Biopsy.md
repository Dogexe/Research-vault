# Novelty Matrix — Diode Laser Biopsy

**Status: PROVISIONAL. Reframed 2026-09-13** to match [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]]'s new ex vivo experimental framing. This matrix cross-tabulates, at full-text level, the four closest-prior-art candidates processed in this vault's last batch — [[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data|Hanke et al. 2021]], [[07 Data/Strakas et al 2023 - 940nm Diode Cutting Efficiency and Thermal Damage Data|Strakas et al. 2023]], [[07 Data/Isola et al 2018 - Diode Laser Pyogenic Granuloma Excision Speed Data|Isola et al. 2018]], and [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2024 - Dual-Wavelength Diode vs Er,Cr YSGG Biopsy Margin Histology Data|Al-Ani et al. 2024]] — against this vault's strongest existing sources, to test how close any of them comes to the current proposed design:

> independently measured delivered power + controlled incision speed + biopsy-oriented histopathologic specimen-quality assessment, in an **ex vivo oral soft-tissue model**

`Diagnostic biopsy outcome` is retained as a column for context but is **no longer the decisive novelty column** — clinical diagnostic outcome is background/clinical-relevance information now, not this project's primary experimental endpoint. Per instruction: set power is never equated with delivered power; incision speed is never inferred from procedure time; companion-paper data are kept as separate rows; dental/oral diode evidence is kept separate from CO2/non-diode/background methodology; no "first ever" or absolute-novelty claim is made; `UNKNOWN` is used where data are missing.

## 1. Closest experimental/technical precedent

**[[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data|Hanke et al. 2021]] and [[07 Data/Strakas et al 2023 - 940nm Diode Cutting Efficiency and Thermal Damage Data|Strakas et al. 2023]] are direct prior art for this project's power-measurement and speed-control design**, not merely supporting evidence. Both independently verify delivered power with a calibrated power meter checked before every single incision (≥5% deviation triggering fiber-tip replacement), control incision speed mechanically at 2 mm/s, and quantify histologic thermal effect in oral (porcine gingival) tissue — the most rigorous measured-power protocol found anywhere in this vault's diode literature. Neither, however, uses a biopsy-oriented outcome framework: both quantify cutting efficiency (cut depth/width, total interaction zone, efficiency factor γz) for surgical-protocol optimization, not specimen-quality/margin-readability/architecture-preservation. See the full side-by-side in [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy#Hanke and Strakas as direct prior art]].

## 2. Closest ex vivo biopsy-oriented precedent

**[[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2024 - Dual-Wavelength Diode vs Er,Cr YSGG Biopsy Margin Histology Data|Al-Ani et al. 2024]]** is the closest existing approximation of a biopsy-oriented ex vivo histologic outcome framework in this batch: ordinal epithelial/connective-tissue damage scores (0–3, covering nuclear/cytoplasmic changes, carbonization, desiccation, vascular changes) plus a lateral thermal damage extent measurement (μm), a confirmed controlled incision speed (0.75 mm/s, stated in its own text), an initiated tip, and contact mode — all in oral (sheep tongue) tissue. It still lacks independently measured delivered power (confirmed from its own text to be a setup-check only) and does not excise a lesion or specimen in the sense a diagnostic biopsy would. **[[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]]** is a secondary partial match on the same axis (crossed power×mode design, initiated tip, quantified compartment-level damage) but with a coarser (continuous mm, not ordinal-scored) outcome measure and no reported speed.

## 3. Clinical relevance context (not direct experimental matches)

[[07 Data/Palaia et al 2021 - 445 nm Diode Laser In Vivo Biopsy Thermal Effect Data|Palaia et al. 2021]], [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]], and [[07 Data/Isola et al 2018 - Diode Laser Pyogenic Granuloma Excision Speed Data|Isola et al. 2018]] are real human in vivo diagnostic-biopsy studies. They are retained as **clinical relevance / outcome context** — showing which histologic alterations matter clinically, when thermal artifact does or does not compromise interpretation, and (for Gundlapalle et al.) what a formal specimen-quality scoring instrument can look like — but they are not ex vivo and are not treated as direct experimental matches to the proposed design. **Gundlapalle et al. 2022's pathologist-graded slide-quality scale (good/average/poor/non-diagnostic) is this vault's strongest existing clinical analog for a specimen-quality outcome instrument**, and is retained specifically for that methodological reason.

## 4. What remains unlinked

- No ex vivo, oral, diode-laser source in this vault combines independently measured delivered power, controlled incision speed, and a biopsy-oriented specimen-quality outcome framework (margin readability, tissue architecture preservation) in one design. Hanke 2021/Strakas 2023 close the first two links but use a cutting-efficiency, not specimen-quality, outcome set. Al-Ani 2024/Pergolini 2025 approximate a biopsy-oriented outcome set but lack independently measured power.
- No source defines a formal margin-readability or tissue-architecture-preservation scoring instrument for an ex vivo, non-lesional oral specimen. Gundlapalle et al. 2022's slide-quality scale is the closest instrument found, but it is in vivo and set-power-only, so it cannot be directly imported without validation in an ex vivo setting.
- No source varies incision speed as an independently manipulated experimental variable against a specimen-quality outcome — every numeric speed value found (10, 2, 1, 0.75 mm/s across different sources) is a single fixed protocol value, not a tested variable.

## 5. POTENTIAL NOVELTY — REQUIRES SYSTEMATIC VERIFICATION

> The combination of independently measured delivered power, controlled incision speed, and histologic tissue-effect assessment has precedent in prior diode-laser studies (Hanke et al. 2021; Strakas et al. 2023). What remains to be established is whether this methodology has been applied to an ex vivo oral soft-tissue model using biopsy-oriented specimen-quality endpoints such as margin readability, tissue architecture preservation, and peri-incisional thermal artifact.

This is narrower than the prior framing (measured power + speed + diagnostic biopsy outcome), because Hanke 2021 and Strakas 2023 already demonstrate that combination — novelty cannot be claimed from those three elements alone. The narrower remaining distinction rests on: the oral soft-tissue model specifically (already partly satisfied by Hanke/Strakas, whose tissue is gingiva); biopsy-oriented specimen-quality outcomes as the outcome framework (not yet demonstrated together with measured power + speed by any source found); margin readability / tissue architecture as explicit endpoints (approximated only partially, by Al-Ani 2024's ordinal scoring and Gundlapalle 2022's clinical slide-quality grade); the specific parameter combinations not already jointly covered; and the integration of all of this into a single study design. This conclusion rests on three independent verification passes on this vault ([[06 Synthesis/Novelty Check - Prior Systematic Review]], [[90 Agent/Search Reports/Novelty Verification - Measured Power and Incision Speed/Novelty Verification Report]], and this matrix), not silence alone, and is not a full systematic search — see the linked verification report for stated limitations. **No claim is made that no such study exists, or that this would be the first ever such study.**

## 6. สรุปภาษาไทย

- ตารางนี้ปรับกรอบใหม่ตามคำถามวิจัย ex vivo ของโครงการ — คอลัมน์ "ผลการวินิจฉัยทางคลินิก (diagnostic biopsy outcome)" **ไม่ใช่คอลัมน์ชี้ขาดความใหม่อีกต่อไป** เป็นเพียงข้อมูลบริบท
- **Hanke et al. 2021 และ Strakas et al. 2023 คือต้นแบบโดยตรง (direct prior art)** ของการออกแบบวัดกำลัง+ความเร็ว — มีกำลังที่วัดได้จริง (ตรวจสอบก่อนตัดทุกครั้ง) + ความเร็วควบคุม + ผลจุลพยาธิวิทยา ในเนื้อเยื่อช่องปาก (เหงือกหมู) แล้ว แต่ใช้กรอบผลลัพธ์แบบประสิทธิภาพการตัด ไม่ใช่กรอบคุณภาพชิ้นเนื้อแบบ biopsy
- **Al-Ani et al. 2024** เป็นต้นแบบที่ใกล้เคียงที่สุดในด้านกรอบผลลัพธ์แบบ biopsy-oriented (คะแนนความเสียหายของเนื้อเยื่อบุผิว/เนื้อเยื่อเกี่ยวพัน) แต่ไม่มีการวัดกำลังจริง
- **Gundlapalle et al. 2022** (ในมนุษย์) มีเครื่องมือให้คะแนนคุณภาพชิ้นเนื้อโดยพยาธิแพทย์ที่ใกล้เคียงที่สุดในคลังนี้ แต่ไม่ใช่ ex vivo
- ความใหม่ที่เหลืออยู่ (ระมัดระวัง): การผนวกกำลังที่วัดได้จริง + ความเร็วควบคุม เข้ากับกรอบประเมินคุณภาพชิ้นเนื้อแบบ biopsy-oriented (ความอ่านง่ายของขอบ, การรักษาโครงสร้างเนื้อเยื่อ) ในแบบจำลอง ex vivo เนื้อเยื่อช่องปาก — ยังไม่มีแหล่งข้อมูลใดทำครบ
- ไม่มีการอ้าง "ไม่มีการศึกษาใดเลย" หรือ "เป็นครั้งแรกในโลก" ในบันทึกนี้

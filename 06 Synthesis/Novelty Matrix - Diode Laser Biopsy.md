# Novelty Matrix — Diode Laser Biopsy

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** This matrix cross-tabulates, at full-text level, the four newly processed closest-prior-art candidates — [[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data|Hanke et al. 2021]], [[07 Data/Strakas et al 2023 - 940nm Diode Cutting Efficiency and Thermal Damage Data|Strakas et al. 2023]], [[07 Data/Isola et al 2018 - Diode Laser Pyogenic Granuloma Excision Speed Data|Isola et al. 2018]], and [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2024 - Dual-Wavelength Diode vs Er,Cr YSGG Biopsy Margin Histology Data|Al-Ani et al. 2024]] — against this vault's strongest existing sources, to test whether the proposed novelty chain is weakened or closed by any of them:

> independently measured delivered power + controlled/measured incision speed + histologic/diagnostic biopsy outcome

Per instruction: set power is never equated with delivered power; incision speed is never inferred from procedure time; companion-paper data are kept as separate rows, not merged into one experiment; dental/oral diode evidence is kept separate from CO2/non-diode/background methodology; no "first ever" or absolute-novelty claim is made; `UNKNOWN` is used where data are missing.

## 1. Exact matches

**None.** No paper found — among the four newly processed candidates, or among the vault's existing strongest sources re-checked here — reports all three links of the proposed chain (independently measured delivered power + numeric incision speed + histologic/diagnostic biopsy outcome) together in one study.

## 2. Closest partial matches

Two distinct clusters emerge, each closing a different subset of the chain, and neither overlaps with the other:

- **Power + speed + histology cluster (missing: diagnostic biopsy outcome):** [[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data|Hanke et al. 2021]] and [[07 Data/Strakas et al 2023 - 940nm Diode Cutting Efficiency and Thermal Damage Data|Strakas et al. 2023]] (both diode, dental-relevant porcine gingiva) plus [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data|Goharkhay et al. 1999]] (diode, pig oral mucosa) and [[07 Data/Wilder-Smith et al 1995 - CO2 Laser Incision and Thermal Damage Data|Wilder-Smith et al. 1995]] (CO2, not diode) all pair a genuinely measured or verified power value with a controlled/measured numeric incision speed and a histologic thermal-damage outcome — but **none of the four excises a lesion or renders a diagnosis.** Hanke 2021 and Strakas 2023 are the closest of this cluster on the *diode* axis specifically, and their power-verification protocol (calibrated power meter, checked before every single incision, ≥5% deviation triggering fiber-tip replacement) is more rigorous than any measured-power protocol previously catalogued in this vault's dental-diode literature — though the exact measured wattage is still not tabulated as a value distinct from the nominal set power in either paper.
- **Diagnostic biopsy + partial-data cluster (missing: measured power, and mostly missing: speed):** [[07 Data/Isola et al 2018 - Diode Laser Pyogenic Granuloma Excision Speed Data|Isola et al. 2018]] (human, real PG excision, measured speed but zero power data), [[07 Data/Palaia et al 2021 - 445 nm Diode Laser In Vivo Biopsy Thermal Effect Data|Palaia et al. 2021]] (human, 100% diagnostic yield, set power only, no speed), and [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]] (human, qualitative diagnosis, set power only, no speed) all render a real or near-real diagnostic outcome, but none independently measures power, and only Isola 2018 reports a numeric speed.

No single source bridges the two clusters. [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2024 - Dual-Wavelength Diode vs Er,Cr YSGG Biopsy Margin Histology Data|Al-Ani et al. 2024]] comes closest to a bridge on the ex vivo side — it has both a confirmed numeric speed (0.75 mm/s) and rich quantified histology — but it lacks measured power and, being ex vivo sheep tongue with no lesion, cannot render a diagnostic outcome by design.

## 3. What remains unlinked

- No dental-diode source — across all eleven papers in this matrix, including the four newly processed ones — pairs an independently measured/verified delivered power value with a diagnostic biopsy outcome (human or otherwise) in the same study. The two sources that come closest to independent power measurement (Hanke 2021, Strakas 2023) are not biopsy studies at all; the sources with real diagnostic outcomes (Palaia 2021, Gundlapalle 2022, Isola 2018) all use set/console power only, or report no power value at all.
- No source pairs independently measured power with numeric incision speed in a real diagnostic biopsy pathway. Hanke 2021 and Strakas 2023 close measured-power + speed together, but only in non-diagnostic ex vivo tissue blocks.
- No source pairs numeric incision speed with a quantified (not merely narrative) diagnostic outcome. Isola 2018 is the closest attempt (measured speed + real human biopsy) but its own diagnostic outcome is reported narratively, not as a formal diagnosis-affected/yield metric.
- The single strongest measured-power protocol in the whole vault (Hanke 2021 / Strakas 2023's calibrated per-incision power-meter verification) has never been applied, by any source located to date, to a lesion-excision or diagnostic-biopsy design — this remains the specific, still-open engineering/design gap this project's own logic chain identifies.

## 4. POTENTIAL NOVELTY — REQUIRES SYSTEMATIC VERIFICATION

**Survives — narrowed further, not closed, by this batch.** None of the four newly processed papers, nor any of the six existing strongest sources re-checked against them here, demonstrates the full connected chain: set power → independently measured delivered power → controlled/measured incision speed → histologic thermal effect → histopathologic/diagnostic biopsy quality. This conclusion is now supported by three independent search/verification passes on this vault ([[06 Synthesis/Novelty Check - Prior Systematic Review]], [[90 Agent/Search Reports/Novelty Verification - Measured Power and Incision Speed/Novelty Verification Report]], and this full-text matrix), not silence alone.

The margins have narrowed in one specific respect: Hanke et al. 2021 and Strakas et al. 2023, read at full text, show a substantially more rigorous power-verification protocol (calibrated power meter checked before every single incision) than this vault had previously catalogued anywhere in its dental-diode literature — closer to "independently measured power" than the single-setup-check pattern seen in the Al-Ani 2023/2024 pair. This narrows, but does not close, the gap: neither paper renders a diagnosis, and neither tabulates the actual measured wattage as a value distinct from the nominal set power. As with all prior passes, this is not a full systematic search (see individual source notes and the linked verification report for stated limitations), and a dedicated instrumentation/metrology-focused review could still exist outside what has been searched to date.

## 5. สรุปภาษาไทย

- ตารางนี้เปรียบเทียบ 4 บทความที่เพิ่งประมวลผล (Hanke 2021, Strakas 2023, Isola 2018, Al-Ani 2024) กับแหล่งข้อมูลหลักเดิมของคลังนี้ เพื่อทดสอบว่าห่วงโซ่ความใหม่ที่อ้างไว้ — **กำลังที่วัดได้จริงอย่างเป็นอิสระ + ความเร็วตัดที่ควบคุม/วัดได้ + ผลชิ้นเนื้อทางจุลพยาธิวิทยา/การวินิจฉัย** — ถูกปิดหรือลดทอนความใหม่ลงหรือไม่
- **ไม่พบการจับคู่ที่ตรงทั้งหมด (exact match)** ในบทความใดเลย
- พบสองกลุ่มที่ใกล้เคียงที่สุด: (1) Hanke 2021, Strakas 2023, Goharkhay 1999 — ปิดได้ 3 จาก 4 จุด (กำลังวัดได้ + ความเร็ว + ผลจุลพยาธิวิทยา) แต่ไม่มีการตัดชิ้นเนื้อจากรอยโรคจริงหรือการวินิจฉัย; (2) Isola 2018, Palaia 2021, Gundlapalle 2022 — มีเส้นทางการวินิจฉัยจริงในมนุษย์ แต่ไม่มีการวัดกำลังที่เป็นอิสระ และส่วนใหญ่ไม่มีความเร็วตัดเป็นตัวเลข
- **ยืนยันเป็นพิเศษ (ตามที่ผู้ใช้ขอ):** Al-Ani et al. 2024 รายงานความเร็วตัด 0.75 มม./วินาที ไว้เองในเนื้อหาบทความ ไม่ได้ยืมมาจากบทความคู่แฝดปี 2023 และเพาเวอร์มิเตอร์ (PINTUDY) ยังคงใช้เพียงเพื่อตั้งค่ากำลังเท่านั้น ไม่ใช่การวัดค่าที่ส่งจริงแยกต่างหาก
- **ความใหม่ของโครงการยังคง "อยู่รอด" (survives)** จากการตรวจสอบรอบนี้ แต่ขอบเขตแคบลง — Hanke 2021 และ Strakas 2023 มีโปรโตคอลการยืนยันกำลังที่เข้มงวดกว่าที่เคยพบในคลังนี้ แต่ทั้งสองบทความไม่ใช่การศึกษา biopsy และไม่มีการวินิจฉัยใด ๆ
- ไม่มีการอ้าง "ครั้งแรกในโลก" หรือความใหม่แบบสมบูรณ์ในบันทึกนี้

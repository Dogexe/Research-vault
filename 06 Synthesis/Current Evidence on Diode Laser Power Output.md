# Current Evidence on Diode Laser Power Output

**Status: PROVISIONAL** — this synthesis reflects only the six processed vault sources listed below. It will change as more sources are processed.

## Research question

- How does measured/delivered power output from dental diode lasers relate to preset/set power, what power loss or deviation has been reported, and what operating parameters are documented as relevant to that relationship?

## Scope

- Direct power-deviation evidence: [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters|Parker et al. 2022]], [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output|Xue et al. 2022]], [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage|Zegaib et al. 2011]], [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers|Kim et al. 2020]].
- Supporting operating-parameter evidence only (neither source reports an independently measured output value distinct from its set value, so neither contributes power-deviation data): [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters|Al-Ani et al. 2023]], [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters|Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. 2020]].

## 1. Current consistent findings

- FACT: Every direct-measurement source that reports both a set value and an independently measured value found a nonzero difference between them, at every device × setting combination tested. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- FACT: Parker et al. found statistically significant variation in percentage power deviation both between devices and between emission-power levels, across 38 units of 20 device types. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]]
- FACT: Kim et al., measuring three separate devices under one shared power meter, fiber tip, and protocol, found the deviation magnitude and direction differed by device (K2 mobile +11.9% to +18.1%; Epic 10 TM −23.7% to −27.3%; Saeshin −14.0% to −18.3%). [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- FACT: Deviation is therefore reported as device-dependent by more than one source, using two different study designs (cross-device survey in Parker et al.; single-protocol multi-device bench study in Kim et al.). [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- FACT: Zegaib et al. found output loss increased with repeated fiber-tip tissue contact (mean 9% before contact, rising to a mean 40.26% by the fourth contact application in the study group), while the non-contact control group showed only 0.14–1.58% change across the same sequence. [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]
- FACT (operating-parameter support only): Al-Ani et al. 2023 and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. 2020 both used a power meter at the fiber tip only to standardize/set the delivery power before their experiments, and neither reports an independently measured output value distinct from the set value — this reporting pattern is consistent between the two supporting sources. [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- FACT (operating-parameter support only): Both supporting sources treat fiber-tip initiation status as a deliberately controlled variable — Al-Ani et al. used an explicitly initiated (pre-charred) tip; Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. used an explicitly non-initiated bare fiber to isolate tissue interaction from added thermal effect. [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]

## 2. Conflicting findings

- FACT: Parker et al. mainly found post-fibre output below control-panel settings. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]]
- FACT: Xue et al. found signed deviations in both directions depending on device and mode — continuous-wave: +21%, +22%, −8% (445/970/810 nm); pulsed: +37%, +26%, −3% (445/970/810 nm). [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- FACT: Zegaib et al.'s single studied device (810 nm, Biolase) showed output only below the panel reference in every measured condition, directionally matching Parker et al. rather than Xue et al.'s mixed pattern; this adds one more below-only device without resolving the Parker/Xue directional conflict. [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- FACT: Kim et al. reproduced the same above/below mixed-direction conflict within one single study using one shared measurement protocol (one device above panel setting, two devices below). [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- FACT: Zegaib et al.'s own Results/Conclusions state a "mean PO loss of 54%" between panel setting and fourth-application tip output, which is arithmetically inconsistent with the study's own Table 1 value (0.54 W of a 1.0 W reference ≈ 46% loss). This vault preserves the source's stated figure verbatim; the inconsistency is unresolved. NEEDS VERIFICATION. [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]
- Magnitudes are not directly comparable across the four sources: Parker et al. reports only device-specific Table 1 values (no single study-wide figure); Xue et al. reports mode-dependent mean percentages; Zegaib et al. reports a contact-application-dependent progression; Kim et al. reports per-device single-session percentages. No pooled or averaged figure is computed here, consistent with vault policy. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]

## 3. Methodological reasons for disagreement

- INTERPRETATION: The four power-deviation sources use no overlapping device models, so the Parker/Xue/Zegaib/Kim directional disagreement cannot be attributed to a shared hardware fault or a shared measurement artifact by inspection alone. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- INTERPRETATION: Kim et al.'s mixed-direction result, obtained within one study under one shared power meter and fiber-tip type, indicates that at least part of the direction-of-deviation pattern is a device-level property rather than solely an artifact of differing measurement protocols across separate studies — this is the source's own stated interpretation, reproduced here. [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- INTERPRETATION: Operating mode differs across studies in ways that could independently affect measured deviation: Xue et al. report different deviation magnitudes for the same wavelength between continuous-wave and pulsed mode on the same device; Kim et al. and Zegaib et al. used continuous-wave only; Parker et al. does not report mode. This is an association documented within Xue et al.'s own data, not a cross-study causal claim. [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]]
- INTERPRETATION: Zegaib et al.'s own framing attributes its large progressive loss (up to 40.26%) to fiber-tip fouling from repeated tissue contact rather than intrinsic fiber transmission loss, since the non-contact control group showed only 0.14–1.58% change under the same sequence. This is the source's own attribution, not independently re-derived here. [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]
- INTERPRETATION: Measurement instrumentation and calibration reporting depth differ across the four sources (Parker et al.: calibrated meter, six measurements averaged per emission, 15-minute warm-up; Xue et al.: two named meters with temperature and wavelength correction; Zegaib et al.: named meter, no calibration protocol described; Kim et al.: named calibrated meter, protocol detail beyond "calibrated" not described), which limits direct comparison of magnitude across studies. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- INTERPRETATION (operating-parameter support only): Al-Ani et al.'s explicit tip-initiation protocol and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.'s explicit non-initiation protocol show that tip-initiation status is treated in the literature as a variable relevant to laser-tissue interaction, but because neither source publishes a measured-output value, this vault cannot use either source to quantify how tip-initiation status affects power loss or deviation. [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- Study scale and design differ sharply: Parker et al. (38 units, 20 device types, cross-sectional survey), Xue et al. (2 devices, bench protocol, CW and pulsed), Zegaib et al. (1 device, secondary analysis of a clinical trial, periodontal curettage), Kim et al. (3 devices, in vitro titanium-disc irradiation). Sample scale differences limit how far any single source's pattern generalizes to the others. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]

## 4. Research gaps

- NEEDS VERIFICATION — no device model is shared between any two of the four power-deviation sources, so no direct head-to-head confirmation of a specific device's deviation direction/magnitude exists in this vault.
- NEEDS VERIFICATION — Zegaib et al.'s stated "54%" panel-to-fourth-application loss figure versus its own Table 1 arithmetic (~46%) remains unresolved. [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]
- NEEDS VERIFICATION — beyond Kim et al.'s 3-device, 3-setting design, no larger standardized multi-device comparison using one shared power meter and protocol exists in this vault.
- Gap: Al-Ani et al. and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. both performed a fiber-tip power-meter measurement but neither published the resulting numeric value, so no vault source currently links tip-initiation status (initiated vs. non-initiated) quantitatively to power-loss or deviation magnitude. [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- Gap: No source in this vault directly combines a quantified power-deviation measurement (as in Parker et al., Xue et al., Zegaib et al., or Kim et al.) with a controlled tip-initiation comparison (as in Al-Ani et al. or Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.) in the same device/protocol.

## 5. Provisional interpretation

- INTERPRETATION: Across the processed vault sources, the difference between preset/set power and measured/delivered output is a real, reproducible, device-dependent phenomenon — it is not zero for any tested device, and its magnitude varies significantly by device and by emission-power level within at least one source (Parker et al.). [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]]
- INTERPRETATION: The direction of that deviation (above vs. below the set value) is not consistent across devices; Kim et al.'s within-study result indicates this inconsistency is at least partly attributable to device-level differences rather than solely to differing measurement protocols across separate studies. [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- INTERPRETATION: Operating parameters such as contact vs. non-contact use, repeated fiber-tip tissue contact, and operating mode (CW vs. pulsed) are documented as associated with output deviation within individual sources (Zegaib et al. for contact/fouling; Xue et al. for mode), but this vault does not have evidence establishing any of these as a general causal mechanism across devices — the association is source-specific. [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- HYPOTHESIS: Tip-initiation status (initiated vs. non-initiated) may interact with measured power loss/deviation, based on its documented relevance to laser-tissue thermal interaction in Al-Ani et al. and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.; this is untested within the current vault sources and requires a study that both quantifies output and controls tip-initiation status. NEEDS VERIFICATION. [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- This synthesis draws no clinical recommendation and treats every conclusion above as provisional pending additional processed sources.

## 6. สรุปภาษาไทย

- การสังเคราะห์นี้เป็น **PROVISIONAL** (ผลชั่วคราว) อ้างอิงเฉพาะแหล่งข้อมูล 6 รายการที่ประมวลผลแล้วในคลังข้อมูลนี้เท่านั้น
- ทุกแหล่งข้อมูลที่วัดทั้งค่าตั้ง (set power) และค่าที่วัดได้จริง (measured output) พบว่าค่าทั้งสองต่างกันเสมอ ในทุกอุปกรณ์และทุกระดับกำลังที่ทดสอบ
- ทิศทางของส่วนต่างไม่สอดคล้องกันในทุกแหล่งข้อมูล — Parker et al. และ Zegaib et al. พบว่ากำลังขาออกต่ำกว่าค่าตั้งเป็นส่วนใหญ่ ขณะที่ Xue et al. และ Kim et al. พบทั้งค่าที่สูงกว่าและต่ำกว่าค่าตั้ง ขึ้นอยู่กับอุปกรณ์และโหมดการทำงาน
- ผล Kim et al. ซึ่งใช้โพรโทคอลและเครื่องวัดเดียวกันสำหรับ 3 อุปกรณ์ บ่งชี้ว่าความขัดแย้งด้านทิศทางนี้เป็นคุณสมบัติเฉพาะของอุปกรณ์แต่ละเครื่อง ไม่ใช่ผลจากความต่างของวิธีวัดระหว่างงานวิจัยเพียงอย่างเดียว (INTERPRETATION)
- Al-Ani et al. และ Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. ใช้เป็นหลักฐานสนับสนุนด้านพารามิเตอร์การทำงาน (เช่น สถานะการ initiate ปลายไฟเบอร์) เท่านั้น ไม่ใช่หลักฐานส่วนต่างกำลังโดยตรง เนื่องจากทั้งสองแหล่งไม่ได้รายงานค่าที่วัดได้จริงแยกจากค่าตั้ง
- ยังไม่มีแหล่งข้อมูลใดในคลังนี้ที่เชื่อมโยงสถานะการ initiate ปลายไฟเบอร์กับปริมาณการสูญเสียกำลังเชิงตัวเลขได้โดยตรง — ถือเป็นช่องว่างของงานวิจัย (research gap) ที่ต้องการการยืนยันเพิ่มเติม (NEEDS VERIFICATION)
- การสังเคราะห์นี้ไม่มีข้อเสนอแนะทางคลินิก และไม่ได้สรุปความสัมพันธ์เชิงเหตุ-ผล (causation) จากข้อมูลที่เป็นเพียงความสัมพันธ์ร่วม (association)

# Research Gaps in Dental Diode Laser Output

**Status: PROVISIONAL** — built only from the current core evidence ([[04 Evidence/Set Power vs Measured Output]], [[04 Evidence/Power Loss and Deviation Across Dental Diode Devices]]) and supporting/background evidence ([[04 Evidence/Output Accuracy and Repeatability - Supporting Evidence]], [[04 Evidence/Tip Initiation and Fiber Condition - Supporting Evidence]]) in this vault, and the underlying 07 Data extractions those four notes cite. It is not a systematic review of the wider literature; absence of a study in this vault is not by itself treated as proof the study doesn't exist elsewhere — each gap below states the specific basis for calling it CONFIRMED or LIKELY rather than resting on vault-absence alone.

## Scope

- Core (direct power-deviation) sources: [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters|Parker et al. 2022]], [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output|Xue et al. 2022]], [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage|Zegaib et al. 2011]], [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers|Kim et al. 2020]].
- Supporting/background sources: [[07 Data/Davies et al 2020 - EVLA Fiber Tip Power Output and Degradation|Davies et al. 2020]] (non-dental, EVLA), [[07 Data/Girasol et al 2022 - Photobiomodulation Device Power and Beam Accuracy|Girasol et al. 2022]] (non-dental, photobiomodulation), [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth|Romanos et al. 2022]] (dental-adjacent, ex vivo, tip-initiation comparison), [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters|Al-Ani et al. 2023]] (dental-adjacent, ex vivo, initiated tip), [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters|Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. 2020]] (dental-adjacent, in vitro, non-initiated tip), [[07 Data/Prado et al 2022 - Micro vs Super Pulsed Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Prado et al. 2022]] (dental, ex vivo, initiated tip, measured power with numeric values reported — narrows but does not close the tip-initiation gap below).

## 1. Confirmed gaps

A gap is listed here only where cross-tabulating the variables actually extracted from the current evidence base shows the specific combination is absent — this is a demonstrable property of the sources processed, independent of whether the wider literature has been exhaustively searched.

- **CONFIRMED GAP (narrowed 2026-09-14) — no source directly compares initiated vs. non-initiated tip status under otherwise controlled conditions while independently measuring delivered output.** This gap was previously stated more broadly as "no source pairs controlled tip-initiation status with an independently measured power-meter output value" — that broader claim is now stale: [[07 Data/Prado et al 2022 - Micro vs Super Pulsed Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Prado et al. 2022]] uses an explicitly initiated tip *and* independently measures delivered power with numeric values reported (PM600 power meter, e.g. 1.2 W display → 1.0 W measured). Prado et al. 2022 does **not**, however, close the gap stated here — it uses an initiated tip throughout with no non-initiated comparison arm, so it demonstrates "initiated tip + measured power" but not a controlled initiated-vs-non-initiated *comparison* with measured output. Romanos et al. 2022 controls initiated vs. non-initiated tips but measures histological penetration depth, not power [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]]. Al-Ani et al. 2023 uses an explicitly initiated tip but only uses its power meter to set/standardize power, publishing no independently measured output value [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]]. Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. 2020 uses an explicitly non-initiated tip and performs a fiber-tip power-meter check before each incision but does not publish the resulting numeric value [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]. None of the four sources that do publish a measured-output value (Parker, Xue, Zegaib, Kim) reports tip-initiation status as initiated/non-initiated, except Xue et al., which reports its tip as uninitiated only (no initiated-tip comparison arm) [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]. This exact gap (narrowed the same way) is also independently stated in [[06 Synthesis/Current Evidence on Diode Laser Power Output]] §4 and in [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]].
- **CONFIRMED GAP — no same-device independent replication exists among the sources that measure power deviation.** Cross-tabulating device/manufacturer fields across all four direct power-deviation sources shows zero shared device models: Parker et al. spans 38 units of 20 unnamed device types; Xue et al. uses SIROLaser Blue (Dentsply Sirona) and Picasso (AMD Lasers); Zegaib et al. uses one named Biolase 810 nm unit; Kim et al. uses K2 mobile (Hulaser), Epic 10 TM (Biolase), and Saeshin (Saeshin) [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]. The one apparent device overlap in the whole vault — Romanos et al. 2022 also uses SIROLaser Blue, the same model as Xue et al. — does not close this gap, because Romanos et al. measures penetration depth, not power, so it cannot independently confirm or contest Xue et al.'s measured-output values for that device [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]].
- **CONFIRMED GAP — no shared or standardized calibration/measurement protocol is used across the dental-diode power-deviation sources.** Reported calibration detail differs sharply: Parker et al. describes a "calibrated" meter, six averaged readings per emission, and a 15-minute device warm-up, without further protocol detail; Xue et al. names two specific meters (PowerMax Pro, LabMax-Pro) with stated temperature/wavelength correction, the most complete calibration description in the core set; Zegaib et al. names a meter (Molectron) with no calibration protocol described; Kim et al. describes a "calibrated" meter (NovaII, Ophir Photonics) with no protocol detail beyond the label [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]. By contrast, the non-dental Girasol et al. 2022 documents a full calibration protocol (manufacturer calibration within 12 months, stated 1%–1.5% sensor error rate) [[07 Data/Girasol et al 2022 - Photobiomodulation Device Power and Beam Accuracy]], showing this level of documentation is achievable but is not what the dental-diode sources in this vault provide.
- **CONFIRMED GAP — no dental-diode source tests fiber degradation from repeated non-contact firing at a scale approaching the manufacturer-rated fiber lifetime.** Zegaib et al. 2011's non-contact control group is the only dental-relevant repeated-firing data point in the vault: across 28 irradiations (4 per tooth × 7 teeth-equivalent sequence position), output drifted only 0.14%–1.58% from the first firing, distinct from the much larger contact-fouling-driven loss (up to 40.26%) seen in the study group [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]. This is a small-scale (4-application-sequence) comparison. No dental-diode source in this vault tests degradation over a large number of firings or a cumulative energy delivery approaching a stated fiber lifetime limit, the way Davies et al. 2020 does for EVLA fibers (three fibers, ~10,000 J cumulative, no degradation found) [[07 Data/Davies et al 2020 - EVLA Fiber Tip Power Output and Degradation]].

## 2. Likely gaps

These are plausible absences supported by analogy or partial/indirect evidence within the current sources, but are not established as cleanly as the confirmed gaps above — each is flagged with the specific reason it falls short of "confirmed," and each was checked against a targeted PubMed search this session (not a systematic review) that returned no matching dental-diode study.

- **LIKELY GAP — a "first-firing"/warm-up power deficit, as documented for a non-dental diode laser, may also occur in dental diode lasers but is untested there.** Davies et al. 2020 found the first firing of a fiber produced significantly lower output than the second and third firings on the same 1470 nm EVLA console (~15% lower, P = .0004 and P < .0001), a pattern the authors attribute to the diode itself rather than console warm-up, since pre-warming the console for 1 hour without firing did not remove the effect (P = .293) [[07 Data/Davies et al 2020 - EVLA Fiber Tip Power Output and Degradation]]. None of the four dental-diode power-deviation sources (Parker, Xue, Zegaib, Kim) reports firing-order or first-vs-subsequent-firing comparisons within a session — but this is a different laser (1470 nm EVLA vs. dental 445–1064 nm devices) and a different measurement method (calorimetry vs. optical power meter), so extrapolating the mechanism to dental diode lasers is INTERPRETATION, not direct evidence. NEEDS VERIFICATION. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- **LIKELY GAP — whether tip-initiation status changes measured power output (as opposed to tissue-effect proxies) is untested, and even the tissue-effect direction is unsettled.** Romanos et al. 2022 is the only source that manipulates tip-initiation status directly, but it measures penetration depth, not power, and its own Abstract and its own Table 1/Discussion report opposite directions for the initiated-vs-non-initiated tissue effect — the Abstract states initiated tips "tended to display a lower penetration depth," while Table 1 and the Discussion show initiated tips producing greater or equal penetration depth in every significant comparison [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]]. Because this internal inconsistency is unresolved in the source itself, this vault cannot even state with confidence what direction tip-initiation shifts the tissue-effect proxy, let alone extend that to a power-output claim. NEEDS VERIFICATION.
- **LIKELY GAP — the regulatory display-accuracy tolerance Davies et al. cite for EVLA consoles (IEC 60601-2-22, ±20%) may or maynot apply, or apply identically, to dental Class IV diode laser consoles.** Davies et al. cite this standard as background, not as their own finding, and this vault has not independently verified it or confirmed whether an equivalent tolerance is specified for the dental devices in Parker et al., Xue et al., Zegaib et al., or Kim et al. [[07 Data/Davies et al 2020 - EVLA Fiber Tip Power Output and Degradation]]. Several core-evidence deviations exceed ±20% (e.g., Xue et al.'s +37% pulsed 445 nm; Kim et al.'s −27.3% Epic 10 TM), which would be out-of-tolerance if the same standard applied, but this vault does not have a source confirming which standard, if any, governs the specific devices in the core evidence set. NEEDS VERIFICATION.

## 3. Methodological limitations causing uncertainty

- Outcome measures differ by source in ways that block pooling: Parker et al., Xue et al., Zegaib et al., and Kim et al. measure power directly with an optical power meter; Davies et al. measures power indirectly via water-bath calorimetry; Girasol et al. measures power with an optical sensor but on non-fiber-delivered probe devices; Romanos et al., Al-Ani et al., and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. measure a tissue-effect or temperature proxy instead of power. No single outcome measure spans all sources. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]] [[07 Data/Davies et al 2020 - EVLA Fiber Tip Power Output and Degradation]] [[07 Data/Girasol et al 2022 - Photobiomodulation Device Power and Beam Accuracy]] [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- Calibration reporting depth is shallow or absent for most dental-diode sources (see Confirmed Gaps §1), which limits confidence in comparing magnitudes even among the four sources that do measure power directly.
- Two internal source-level inconsistencies remain unresolved and are preserved rather than silently corrected, per vault policy: Zegaib et al.'s stated "mean PO loss of 54%" does not arithmetically match its own Table 1 value (~46% loss) [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]; Romanos et al.'s Abstract and Table 1/Discussion report opposite directions for the initiated-vs-non-initiated tissue-effect comparison [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]]. Both weaken confidence in directly quoting either source's headline percentage/direction without checking the underlying table.
- Sample scale is small and heterogeneous across sources: Kim et al. and Xue et al. test 2–3 devices each; Zegaib et al., Romanos et al., Al-Ani et al., and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. each test a single device or device pairing; only Parker et al. (38 units, 20 types) and Girasol et al. (24 units) approach a survey scale, and neither is a controlled single-protocol comparison. This limits how far any single source's pattern can be generalized. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Girasol et al 2022 - Photobiomodulation Device Power and Beam Accuracy]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- Girasol et al. found no significant correlation between device age or time-since-maintenance and measured-power accuracy (all reported correlations non-significant, p > .2) [[07 Data/Girasol et al 2022 - Photobiomodulation Device Power and Beam Accuracy]]. This is non-dental evidence, but if it generalizes, it would undercut a simple "older/less-maintained devices drift more" explanation for the core dental-diode deviations — the actual cause of device-level deviation in the core evidence set remains unexplained by any source in this vault.
- Two of the five supporting/background sources (Davies et al., Girasol et al.) are non-dental; extrapolating their findings to dental diode lasers is INTERPRETATION, not direct evidence, throughout this note.

## 4. Highest-priority future research questions

These are phrased as open research questions arising from the gaps above, not as clinical recommendations.

1. Does a dental diode laser, tested with a calibrated fiber-tip power meter across several successive same-session firings of the same fiber, show a first-firing power deficit analogous to the one Davies et al. 2020 reported for an EVLA console? (addresses the first-firing/warm-up gap)
2. On a single dental diode device model, does fiber-tip initiation status (initiated vs. non-initiated) produce a measurable difference in calibrated power-meter output — independent of, and in addition to, any tissue-effect proxy such as penetration depth or temperature rise? (addresses the tip-initiation + direct power-meter gap, and would also help resolve the unexplained abstract-vs-table direction conflict in Romanos et al. 2022)
3. Can any single dental diode device model already reported in the literature (e.g., SIROLaser Blue, used by both Xue et al. 2022 and Romanos et al. 2022) be independently re-measured for power deviation by a second study, to test whether device-level deviation direction and magnitude replicate? (addresses the same-device independent replication gap)
4. Does repeated non-contact firing of a dental diode fiber, at a cumulative energy approaching the manufacturer-rated fiber lifetime, produce measurable power degradation distinct from the contact-fouling effect already documented by Zegaib et al. 2011? (addresses the repeated-use fiber degradation gap)
5. Would adopting one fully specified, shared calibration and measurement protocol (named calibrated power meter, calibration interval and traceability, device warm-up time, number of averaged readings, temperature/wavelength correction) across multiple dental diode devices change — or confirm — the currently device-dependent, bidirectional pattern of set-vs-measured deviation reported in [[04 Evidence/Power Loss and Deviation Across Dental Diode Devices]]? (addresses the standardized calibration/measurement-protocol gap)

This synthesis draws no clinical recommendation and treats every conclusion above as provisional pending additional processed sources.

## 5. สรุปภาษาไทย

- หมายเหตุนี้เป็นการสังเคราะห์ **ช่องว่างงานวิจัย (research gaps)** โดยอ้างอิงเฉพาะหลักฐานหลักและหลักฐานสนับสนุน/ภูมิหลังที่มีอยู่ในคลังข้อมูลนี้เท่านั้น ไม่ใช่การทบทวนวรรณกรรมอย่างเป็นระบบ
- **ช่องว่างที่ยืนยันแล้ว (CONFIRMED GAP)** 4 ข้อ ได้แก่ (1) **[ปรับปรุง 2026-09-14]** ไม่มีแหล่งข้อมูลใดเปรียบเทียบสถานะปลายไฟเบอร์ initiated กับ non-initiated โดยตรงภายใต้เงื่อนไขควบคุม พร้อมกับวัดกำลังขาออกจริง — ข้อความเดิมที่ว่า "ไม่มีแหล่งข้อมูลใดวัดกำลังขาออกจริงคู่กับสถานะ initiated เลย" ล้าสมัยแล้ว เนื่องจาก Prado et al. 2022 มีทั้งปลาย initiated และค่ากำลังที่วัดได้จริงเป็นตัวเลข แต่ Prado ใช้ปลาย initiated ตลอดโดยไม่มีกลุ่มเปรียบเทียบ non-initiated จึงยังไม่ปิดช่องว่างการเปรียบเทียบนี้ (2) ไม่มีการวัดซ้ำโดยอิสระในอุปกรณ์รุ่นเดียวกันระหว่างงานวิจัยที่วัดส่วนต่างกำลัง (3) ไม่มีโพรโทคอลการสอบเทียบ/การวัดที่เป็นมาตรฐานเดียวกันใช้ร่วมกันในทุกแหล่งข้อมูล และ (4) ยังไม่มีการทดสอบการเสื่อมสภาพของไฟเบอร์จากการยิงซ้ำแบบไม่สัมผัสเนื้อเยื่อในระดับใกล้เคียงอายุการใช้งานที่ผู้ผลิตกำหนด
- **ช่องว่างที่เป็นไปได้ (LIKELY GAP)** 3 ข้อ ได้แก่ (1) ผล "การยิงครั้งแรก/การอุ่นเครื่อง" ที่พบในเลเซอร์ที่ไม่ใช่ทางทันตกรรม (Davies et al. 2020) อาจเกิดขึ้นในเลเซอร์ไดโอดทางทันตกรรมด้วยแต่ยังไม่ได้รับการทดสอบ (2) ผลของสถานะ initiated/non-initiated ต่อกำลังขาออกจริงยังไม่มีการทดสอบ และทิศทางของผลต่อเนื้อเยื่อเองก็ยังขัดแย้งกันภายในงานของ Romanos et al. เอง (3) มาตรฐานความคลาดเคลื่อนของหน้าปัดที่ Davies et al. อ้างอิง (IEC 60601-2-22, ±20%) ยังไม่ทราบว่าใช้กับเลเซอร์ไดโอดทางทันตกรรมเหมือนกันหรือไม่
- ข้อจำกัดเชิงระเบียบวิธีสำคัญ ได้แก่ วิธีวัดผลลัพธ์ที่ต่างกันในแต่ละแหล่งข้อมูล (เพาเวอร์มิเตอร์ เทียบกับ แคลอรีมิเตอร์ เทียบกับ ตัวชี้วัดผลต่อเนื้อเยื่อ) การรายงานโพรโทคอลการสอบเทียบที่ไม่ครบถ้วน และข้อขัดแย้งภายในแหล่งข้อมูลเอง 2 กรณี (Zegaib et al. และ Romanos et al.) ที่ยังไม่ได้รับการแก้ไข
- คำถามวิจัยที่มีความสำคัญสูงสุด 5 ข้อ มุ่งเน้นไปที่: การทดสอบผลการยิงครั้งแรกในเลเซอร์ไดโอดทางทันตกรรมโดยตรง, การวัดกำลังขาออกจริงคู่กับสถานะปลายไฟเบอร์บนอุปกรณ์เดียวกัน, การวัดซ้ำโดยอิสระในอุปกรณ์รุ่นเดียวกัน, การทดสอบการเสื่อมสภาพของไฟเบอร์ในระดับอายุการใช้งาน และการใช้โพรโทคอลการสอบเทียบมาตรฐานเดียวกันในหลายอุปกรณ์
- การสังเคราะห์นี้ไม่มีข้อเสนอแนะทางคลินิกใด ๆ และถือว่าทุกข้อสรุปเป็นผลชั่วคราว (PROVISIONAL) รอการประมวลผลแหล่งข้อมูลเพิ่มเติม

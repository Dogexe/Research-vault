# Novelty Check — Prior Systematic Review

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** Compares [[07 Data/Lopes-Santos et al 2023 - High-Power Laser Oral Biopsy Histology Systematic Review Data|Lopes-Santos et al. 2023]] (a general oral-soft-tissue high-power-laser-vs-scalpel systematic review) against this vault's own diode-biopsy synthesis: [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Quality]], [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map]], [[06 Synthesis/Contradictions in Diode Laser Biopsy Evidence]], [[06 Synthesis/Research Gaps in Dental Diode Laser Output]], and [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]]. This note draws no clinical recommendation. Per vault instruction, **absence of a topic in the review is not treated as proof of novelty by itself** — every gap below is labeled by its actual source (the review's own stated limitations vs. this vault's own cross-tabulation of processed primary sources).

**Update (2026-09-13):** Five more literature notes were processed since this note was last updated — [[07 Data/Shnawa et al 2025 - Diode Laser vs Scalpel Oral Fibroma Excision Data|Shnawa et al. 2025]] (CORE), [[07 Data/Merigo et al 2012 - Five-Wavelength Ex Vivo Thermal and Histologic Comparison Data|Merigo et al. 2012]] and [[07 Data/Spille et al 2026 - Diode Laser Learning-Curve and Target-Based Accuracy Data|Spille et al. 2026]] (SUPPORTING), and [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2013 - Diode Laser Soft-Tissue Surgery Review Data|Romanos et al. 2013]] and [[07 Data/Lu et al 2026 - Dual-Wavelength 980-1470 nm Liver Cutting Parameters Data (Abstract-Level)|Lu et al. 2026]] (BACKGROUND). None overlaps with any of the review's 4 diode studies (Amaral 2015, Bhatsange 2016, Monteiro 2019, Gill 2021), all published 2025/2026, after the review's own search cutoff. Sections below are updated only where directly relevant.

## 1. What the review already covers

- FACT: A formal PRISMA-registered (PROSPERO CRD42022347471) systematic review of high-power laser (diode, CO2, Nd:YAG, Er:YAG) vs. cold scalpel for oral soft-tissue biopsy, comparing **histological artifacts** — epithelial changes, connective-tissue changes, vascular alterations, nuclear/cytoplasmic changes, an incision-damage-morphology score (0–4), and thermal damage — across 7 included studies (399 patients), 4 of which used a diode laser (Amaral et al. 2015, Bhatsange et al. 2016, Monteiro et al. 2019, Gill et al. 2021).
- FACT: It formally assesses risk of bias (Cochrane RoB) and certainty of evidence (GRADE, rated **low** overall) across the whole evidence base, and explicitly declines a meta-analysis due to heterogeneity.
- FACT: It reaches a general, laser-type-agnostic conclusion that high-power lasers (including diode) produce more epithelial/connective-tissue/vascular/nuclear artifacts than cold scalpel, and less precise incision margins (score 1.4–2.64 vs. 3.25–4.0 for scalpel), while — in 4 of the 7 studies, including 2 of the 4 diode studies (Amaral, Monteiro) — not interfering with the histopathological diagnosis itself.
- FACT: It reports set/console power, wavelength, and CW/PW mode for most (not all) included studies, and explicitly calls out one diode-adjacent study (Monteiro et al. 2019) by name for not reporting wave mode — the review's single most specific parameter-reporting critique.

## 2. What it does NOT cover

- FACT: The review's eligibility criteria **required a cold-scalpel control arm** in every included study. This is a scope decision, not a quality judgment — it means diode-only or non-scalpel-comparator designs (the kind that make up most of this vault's own core biopsy evidence: [[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield|Angiero et al. 2011]], [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]], [[07 Data/Palaia et al 2021 - 445 nm Diode Laser In Vivo Biopsy Thermal Effect Data|Palaia et al. 2021]], [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]], [[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data|Azevedo et al. 2016]]) were structurally out of scope for this review regardless of their evidentiary strength.
- FACT: The review never raises **set vs. measured/delivered power** as a variable anywhere in its Methods, Results, Discussion, or Limitations. It extracts a single "strength (W)" value per study and treats it uniformly as the operating parameter, with no discussion of power-meter verification or calibration.
- FACT: The review never mentions **incision speed**, **tip-initiation status (initiated/non-initiated)**, **contact vs. non-contact mode**, or **calibration method**, for any of its 7 included studies.
- FACT: The review does not report or discuss **specimen size or excision margin distance** as a variable at all — its diagnostic-quality outcome is a binary "diagnosis affected: Yes/No/N/A" judgment, not a size-graded or margin-graded diagnostic-yield percentage.
- FACT: Pulse duration and duty cycle are reported for none of the 4 diode studies; the only pulsed-diode descriptor in the whole review (Gill et al.'s 980 nm arm, "pulsed mode at 50 ms intervals") is not resolved by the review itself into pulse width vs. period.

## 3. Overlap with our current project

- REAL OVERLAP: The review's own diode-specific findings are consistent in direction with this vault's already-processed evidence — diode lasers produce measurable epithelial/thermal/vascular artifacts relative to scalpel, and (in the 2 diode studies that checked) do not necessarily impair diagnosis. This corroborates, from an independent author group and independent study set, this vault's own standing conclusion in [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Quality]] that thermal artifact and diagnostic failure are not the same thing.
- REAL OVERLAP: The review's Discussion cites **[[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]]** (already processed in this vault) as external support for "pulsed wave mode... reduce[s] damage to oral tissue." This is not one of the review's own 7 included/analyzed studies (it postdates the review's July 2022 search cutoff) — it is cited only in Discussion, not synthesized in Table 2. So the review and this vault are drawing on the same underlying source for the PW < CW direction, not independently confirming it twice.
- PARTIAL, CONFOUNDED OVERLAP: The review's own within-review diode CW-vs-PW comparison is Gill et al.'s two arms — but these confound wavelength (808 vs. 980 nm) and power (2.5 vs. 5 W) with mode simultaneously, exactly the same confound structure this vault already identified and excluded as unusable evidence in [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data|Romeo et al. 2014]]'s diode-CW-vs-KTP-PW comparison (see [[06 Synthesis/Contradictions in Diode Laser Biopsy Evidence]]). The review does not flag this confound itself. Net effect: the review adds no clean, usable CW-vs-PW diode data point to this vault's existing three-way discordant picture (Gundlapalle, Pergolini, Goharkhay).
- NO OVERLAP in primary sources: none of the review's 4 diode studies (Amaral 2015, Bhatsange 2016, Monteiro 2019, Gill 2021) is already present anywhere in this vault's processed literature — confirmed by name-checking against every diode-biopsy source in [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]] and [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map]]. This is a genuinely new, non-duplicative set of primary evidence, now recorded in [[07 Data/Lopes-Santos et al 2023 - High-Power Laser Oral Biopsy Histology Systematic Review Data]].
- OVERLAP IN CRITICAL STANCE: The review's low-GRADE-certainty rating and explicit refusal to meta-analyze due to heterogeneity is broadly consistent with this vault's own repeated NEEDS VERIFICATION / preserved-conflict stance toward its biopsy evidence base — an independent research group reached a similarly cautious conclusion about this literature using a different, more formal methodology (GRADE) than this vault uses.
- NEW REAL OVERLAP (2026-09-13): [[07 Data/Shnawa et al 2025 - Diode Laser vs Scalpel Oral Fibroma Excision Data|Shnawa et al. 2025]] (processed after this review, not one of its 4 diode studies) independently reproduces the review's general diode-vs-scalpel finding pattern — diode excision produced qualitatively better-preserved histologic architecture than scalpel despite noted "minor thermal artifacts" — from a fifth, independent author group and study design not analyzed by the review. This is corroboration of direction only; Shnawa et al. 2025 does not report the review's own artifact-category taxonomy (epithelial/connective/vascular/nuclear scores) or its 0–4 incision-morphology score, so it cannot be slotted into the review's own comparison table.

## 4. Gaps confirmed by the review — REVIEW-IDENTIFIED GAP

These are the review's **own** stated limitations or explicit critiques, in its own words or close paraphrase (see [[07 Data/Lopes-Santos et al 2023 - High-Power Laser Oral Biopsy Histology Systematic Review Data]] for full verbatim quotes):

- REVIEW-IDENTIFIED GAP: "the methodological heterogeneity of the selected studies, the diversity of the high-power laser devices, and the many possibilities of different clinical protocols" prevented pooling/meta-analysis.
- REVIEW-IDENTIFIED GAP: the restriction to studies with a cold-scalpel control arm "drastically restricted" the eligible sample.
- REVIEW-IDENTIFIED GAP: overall certainty of evidence is low, "due to the risk of bias and imprecision of the articles evaluated."
- REVIEW-IDENTIFIED GAP: "It was extremely difficult to compare the wavelength and power of different lasers in this review, mainly because the various studies discussed different types of high-power laser devices" — i.e., device/protocol diversity itself is named as a barrier to establishing standard protocols.
- REVIEW-IDENTIFIED GAP (narrow, single-instance): one specific included study (Monteiro et al. 2019) is explicitly named as not reporting wave mode — the review's only instance of flagging a specific missing operating parameter in a specific study, rather than describing heterogeneity in general terms.

## 5. Gaps not addressed by the review — VAULT-IDENTIFIED GAP

These are gaps this vault has independently established by directly cross-tabulating its own processed primary sources (see [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map]] §8 and [[06 Synthesis/Research Gaps in Dental Diode Laser Output]]). The review neither confirms nor contradicts them — it simply never engages with this axis at all, because its own outcome framework (histological-artifact categories vs. scalpel) does not require it:

- VAULT-IDENTIFIED GAP: no dental-diode biopsy source (in this vault, or among the review's 4 diode studies) pairs an independently measured, calibrated power-meter output value with a histologically assessed biopsy outcome. The review does not raise measured-vs-set power as a concept at all, so it neither closes nor contradicts this gap — it is simply silent on the axis.
- VAULT-IDENTIFIED GAP: no source (in this vault, or among the review's 4 diode studies) reports incision speed against a histologic-quality outcome. The review's own text contains zero mentions of incision speed. UPDATE (2026-09-13): [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2013 - Diode Laser Soft-Tissue Surgery Review Data|Romanos et al. 2013]] (a CE review, not a primary study) adds the vault's most explicit mechanistic statement that collateral damage depends on cutting speed, and [[07 Data/Lu et al 2026 - Dual-Wavelength 980-1470 nm Liver Cutting Parameters Data (Abstract-Level)|Lu et al. 2026]] adds a numeric speed value (10 mm/s) paired with quantified thermal-injury depth — but for rabbit liver, not oral tissue, and not a diode-only device. Neither closes this gap for oral-biopsy histologic quality.
- VAULT-IDENTIFIED GAP: no source (in this vault, or among the review's 4 diode studies) reports tip-initiation status. The review does not mention tip initiation, fiber condition, or non-initiated/initiated tips anywhere. UPDATE (2026-09-13): [[07 Data/Shnawa et al 2025 - Diode Laser vs Scalpel Oral Fibroma Excision Data|Shnawa et al. 2025]] is now the second CORE-tier source in this vault (after Pergolini et al. 2025) to report tip-initiation status as a real value ("initiated," via articulating paper) — narrowing, not closing, this gap, and still with no non-initiated comparator in any core source.
- VAULT-IDENTIFIED GAP: no source (in this vault, or among the review's 4 diode studies) reports a standardized calibration/measurement protocol. The review does not discuss calibration at all.
- VAULT-IDENTIFIED GAP: this vault's ≥4–5 mm specimen-size/margin threshold (independently converged on by [[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield|Angiero et al. 2011]], [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]], and [[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]]) is not addressed by the review at all — it does not report specimen size or margin distance as a variable, and its "diagnosis affected" outcome is a cruder binary judgment, not a size-graded yield percentage. The review therefore neither strengthens nor weakens this vault's threshold finding; it is simply a different, coarser outcome measure.
- VAULT-IDENTIFIED GAP: this vault's three-way discordant CW-vs-PW thermal-damage-direction conflict ([[07 Data/Gundlapalle et al 2022 - Diode Laser CW vs PW Biopsy Margin Data|Gundlapalle et al. 2022]] PW<CW; [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Pergolini et al. 2025]] PW>CW non-significant; [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data|Goharkhay et al. 1999]] no difference) is not resolved or even engaged by the review — its own within-review diode CW-vs-PW comparison (Gill et al.) is confounded by wavelength and power, and its cited external support (Gundlapalle) is only one side of this vault's existing three-way conflict, not new information.

## 6. Potential novelty that remains

**Label: POTENTIAL NOVELTY — REQUIRES SYSTEMATIC VERIFICATION.** Not treated as proven; this label is retained until a dedicated systematic search specifically targets the measured-power + incision-speed + histology chain.

Per instruction, novelty is claimed here only where this vault has **independent, targeted confirmation** (its own processed-source cross-tabulation, not merely the review's silence) that a gap is real, and the review's independent silence is noted only as a weak, secondary corroborating signal — not as the basis for the claim itself.

- The core angle of [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]] — tracing **device preset power → independently measured/delivered output → operating parameters → tissue interaction → biopsy diagnostic quality** as a single connected evidentiary chain — remains undemonstrated by any source this vault has processed, including all 4 diode studies newly extracted from this review, and including the five sources processed 2026-09-13 (Shnawa et al. 2025, Merigo et al. 2012, Spille et al. 2026, Romanos et al. 2013, Lu et al. 2026). The closest partial contributions to date remain isolated to single links of the chain: Merigo et al. 2012 measures power (pooled, not diode-isolated) but renders no diagnosis; Shnawa et al. 2025 renders a diagnosis with an initiated tip but does not measure power or speed; Romanos et al. 2013 and Lu et al. 2026 speak to speed but not to an oral diagnostic-biopsy pathway. This claim rests on this vault's own direct cross-tabulation of every processed diode-biopsy source (documented across [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map]] and [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Quality]]), not on the review's silence alone. The review's independent silence on set-vs-measured power as a concept is consistent with this being a genuinely under-examined axis in the wider oral-biopsy laser literature, but a single general-scope review not engaging with a metrology question is weak, not conclusive, corroboration — a dedicated instrumentation/metrology-focused review could still exist outside what either this vault or Lopes-Santos et al. 2023 has searched.
- The specific parameter-completeness cross-tabulation this vault maintains (incision speed, tip-initiation status, contact mode, pulse duration/duty cycle, calibration method — each checked against every core diode-biopsy source and marked UNKNOWN where absent, per [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map]] §8) is more granular and more systematically tabulated across more sources (11 core + supporting diode-biopsy sources) than anything in Lopes-Santos et al. 2023, whose only comparable statement is the single "one article did not report wave mode" callout. This granularity itself — not merely the topic — is a candidate point of methodological novelty for this project, distinct from a claim that the underlying gap is undiscovered elsewhere.
- The review's scope requirement (mandatory scalpel comparator) structurally excludes the kind of fixed-setting, quantified-diagnostic-yield, non-comparator diode studies this vault relies on most heavily (Angiero, Gambino, Palaia, Pergolini). This vault's synthesis of that specific evidence subset — and its resulting evidence-supported CW range (1.6–2.7 W, narrowing to 2.0–2.5 W for single-fixed-setting sources; [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map]] §9) — sits in evidentiary territory the review's own eligibility criteria could not reach, independent of whether the review would have agreed with it.
- What is **not** claimed as novel here: that diode lasers cause thermal artifact, that PW vs. CW effects are inconsistent across studies, or that device/protocol diversity impairs comparison — the review already states versions of all three (see §4 above), so none of these can be claimed as this project's original contribution.

## 7. สรุปภาษาไทย

- บันทึกนี้เปรียบเทียบ **Lopes-Santos et al. 2023** (systematic review ทั่วไปเรื่องเลเซอร์กำลังสูงเทียบมีดผ่าตัดในเนื้อเยื่ออ่อนช่องปาก) กับงานสังเคราะห์เดิมของคลังนี้เรื่องเลเซอร์ไดโอดโดยเฉพาะ **หลักการสำคัญ: การที่ review ไม่กล่าวถึงหัวข้อใดหัวข้อหนึ่ง ไม่ถือเป็นหลักฐานความใหม่ (novelty) โดยอัตโนมัติ**
- **ครอบคลุมแล้วโดย review:** ผลกระทบทางจุลพยาธิวิทยาของเลเซอร์กำลังสูง (รวมไดโอด) เทียบมีดผ่าตัด ในบทความ 4 ฉบับที่ใช้ไดโอด (Amaral, Bhatsange, Monteiro, Gill) — ไม่มีบทความใดซ้ำกับแหล่งข้อมูลที่ประมวลผลแล้วในคลังนี้เลย
- **ไม่ครอบคลุมโดย review:** การแยกกำลังที่ตั้ง (set) ออกจากกำลังที่วัดได้จริง (measured/delivered), ความเร็วในการตัด, สถานะปลายไฟเบอร์ initiated/non-initiated, โหมดสัมผัส/ไม่สัมผัส, โพรโทคอลการสอบเทียบ, และเกณฑ์ขนาดชิ้นเนื้อ/ขอบตัดแบบละเอียด — ไม่มีข้อใดถูกกล่าวถึงเลยใน review นี้
- **REVIEW-IDENTIFIED GAP** (ที่ review ระบุเอง): ความหลากหลายของอุปกรณ์/โพรโทคอลทำให้เปรียบเทียบยาก, กลุ่มตัวอย่างถูกจำกัดด้วยเกณฑ์ต้องมีกลุ่มควบคุมมีดผ่าตัด, ความน่าเชื่อถือของหลักฐานโดยรวมต่ำ, และมีบทความหนึ่งฉบับ (Monteiro) ที่ไม่รายงานโหมดคลื่น
- **VAULT-IDENTIFIED GAP** (ที่คลังนี้ค้นพบเองจากการวิเคราะห์แหล่งข้อมูลที่ประมวลผลแล้ว ไม่ใช่จากความเงียบของ review): กำลังที่วัดได้จริงคู่กับผลชิ้นเนื้อ, ความเร็วตัด, สถานะปลายไฟเบอร์, โพรโทคอลการสอบเทียบ, และเกณฑ์ขนาดชิ้นเนื้อ ≥4–5 มม. — ยังคงเป็นช่องว่างเดิมของคลังนี้ ไม่ได้รับการยืนยันหรือคัดค้านจาก review
- ความใหม่ที่อาจยังคงเหลืออยู่ (อย่างระมัดระวัง): ห่วงโซ่ "ค่าตั้งเครื่อง → กำลังที่วัดได้จริง → พารามิเตอร์ → ผลเนื้อเยื่อ → คุณภาพการวินิจฉัย" ยังไม่มีแหล่งข้อมูลใดเชื่อมโยงครบทุกจุด ทั้งในคลังนี้และใน 4 บทความไดโอดของ review — ข้อสรุปนี้อ้างอิงจากการวิเคราะห์ตรงของคลังนี้เอง ความเงียบของ review เป็นเพียงหลักฐานสนับสนุนรองที่อ่อน ไม่ใช่หลักฐานยืนยัน
- **อัปเดต 2026-09-13:** ประมวลผลบทความใหม่ 5 ฉบับ (Shnawa et al. 2025 — CORE; Merigo et al. 2012 และ Spille et al. 2026 — SUPPORTING; Romanos et al. 2013 และ Lu et al. 2026 — BACKGROUND) ไม่มีฉบับใดซ้ำกับ 4 บทความไดโอดของ review และไม่มีฉบับใดปิดห่วงโซ่หลักข้างต้นได้ครบ — Shnawa et al. 2025 เป็นแหล่งข้อมูล CORE รายที่สองที่รายงานสถานะปลายไฟเบอร์ initiated จริง (รองจาก Pergolini et al. 2025)
- สิ่งที่ **ไม่ถือเป็นความใหม่**: การที่เลเซอร์ไดโอดก่อความเสียหายจากความร้อน, ความไม่สอดคล้องกันของผล CW เทียบ PW, หรือความหลากหลายของอุปกรณ์ที่ทำให้เปรียบเทียบยาก — เพราะ review ได้ระบุสิ่งเหล่านี้ไว้แล้วด้วยตนเอง
- บันทึกนี้ไม่มีข้อเสนอแนะทางคลินิกใด ๆ และถือเป็น PROVISIONAL

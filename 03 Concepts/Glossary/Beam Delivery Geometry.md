# Beam Delivery Geometry

## Definition
The overall physical arrangement by which laser energy is delivered to tissue — encompassing fiber diameter/spot size, contact vs. non-contact positioning, and fiber tip condition (initiated vs. non-initiated). These variables jointly determine effective irradiance and absorption pathway even when set power is unchanged.

## Unit / equation
Not applicable — umbrella concept grouping several separately measured variables.

## In this project
This vault tracks beam delivery geometry as a modifier of the primary experimental chain (set power → measured power → incision speed → tissue interaction), not as an independent outcome. A given measured power can produce different tissue effects depending on fiber diameter, contact status, and tip initiation — so cross-study comparisons of power or speed alone, without checking delivery geometry, risk comparing non-equivalent exposures.

## Common pitfalls
Do not assume two studies at the same wavelength and power are comparable without checking fiber diameter, contact/non-contact status, and tip initiation — each independently changes the effective irradiance or absorption mechanism at the tissue surface.

# Contact Mode

## Definition

A delivery technique in which the fiber tip physically touches the target tissue during laser application, as opposed to being held at a distance (see Non-contact Mode). Contact mode is commonly paired with an initiated tip in soft-tissue surgical dental laser use, since direct contact facilitates the cutting/ablation mechanism, but the two are conceptually independent variables (see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Initiated Tip]] — Common confusion).

## นิยามภาษาไทย

โหมดสัมผัส (Contact Mode) คือเทคนิคการใช้งานที่ปลายไฟเบอร์สัมผัสกับเนื้อเยื่อเป้าหมายโดยตรงระหว่างการฉายเลเซอร์ ตรงข้ามกับการถือห่างจากเป้าหมาย (ดู Non-contact Mode) โหมดสัมผัสมักใช้คู่กับปลายไฟเบอร์แบบ initiated ในการผ่าตัดเนื้อเยื่ออ่อนทางทันตกรรม เนื่องจากการสัมผัสโดยตรงช่วยกลไกการตัด/สลายเนื้อเยื่อ แต่ทั้งสองแนวคิดเป็นตัวแปรที่แยกจากกันได้

## In this vault

[[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] explicitly compares contact mode (study group, fiber inserted into periodontal pocket) against non-contact mode (control group) using the same fiber type, finding much greater progressive power loss in the contact group (up to 40.26%) than the non-contact group (0.14%–1.58%) over repeated applications — attributed by the source to contact-induced tip fouling rather than intrinsic fiber loss. [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]] also uses contact mode (fiber tip in vertical contact with a titanium disc). [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] uses contact mode for both its diode and Er,Cr:YSGG comparator arms. [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] uses contact mode with the fiber inserted parallel to the tooth axis.

## Common confusion

Contact mode is a delivery/geometry variable, not a synonym for "initiated tip" or for CW operating mode — a laser can be used in contact mode with either an initiated or non-initiated tip, and in either CW or pulsed mode. This vault's sources happen to pair contact mode with initiated tips more often, but that is a co-occurrence in the reviewed literature, not a fixed rule.

## Source basis

**Classification:** FIELD-USAGE TERM

No ISO/IEC laser vocabulary standard, ANSI Z136 edition, or PBM dosimetry consensus reference reviewed for this vault defines "contact mode" as a formal term — those standards specify irradiance/fluence/exposure geometry but do not name a contact/non-contact category. "Contact mode" is used operationally in the peer-reviewed dental laser literature cited in this vault (see In this vault above, e.g. Zegaib et al. 2011, Kim et al. 2020).

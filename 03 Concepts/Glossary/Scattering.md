# Scattering

## Definition
The redirection of light as it travels through tissue without being absorbed, caused by refractive-index inhomogeneities in tissue microstructure. Scattering spreads the effective beam and reduces the fraction of incident light that reaches deeper structures along a collimated path.

## Unit / equation
Not applicable in this vault's usage.

## In this project
Scattering is mentioned here only as foundational background — no source in this vault quantifies scattering for oral soft tissue at any studied wavelength. It is relevant conceptually to why thermal/tissue-effect zones are not perfectly sharp-edged.

## Common pitfalls
Do not assume all unabsorbed light is transmitted straight through tissue — a substantial fraction is scattered, not transmitted in a simple straight-line sense (see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Transmission]]).

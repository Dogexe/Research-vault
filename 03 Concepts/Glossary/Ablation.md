# Ablation

## Definition
The removal of tissue material by laser energy, encompassing vaporization and any mechanical/photomechanical tissue ejection. In continuous-wave diode soft-tissue surgery, ablation occurs primarily via the vaporization mechanism rather than the plasma-mediated or photomechanical ablation seen with some other laser types (e.g., Er:YAG).

## In this project
This vault's cutting-efficiency sources ([[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data]], [[07 Data/Strakas et al 2023 - 940nm Diode Cutting Efficiency and Thermal Damage Data]]) measure cut depth/width as the outcome of this ablation process but do not separately name or score "ablation" as a distinct variable from cut geometry itself.

## Common pitfalls
Do not assume "ablation" implies a specific mechanism across all laser types — the dominant ablation mechanism for a CW diode (thermal vaporization) differs from that of an Er:YAG or Er,Cr:YSGG laser (explosive water-mediated ablation), which this vault's [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2024 - Dual-Wavelength Diode vs Er,Cr YSGG Biopsy Margin Histology Data|Al-Ani et al. 2024]] compares directly.

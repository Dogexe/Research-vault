# Specimen Margin

## Definition

The cut edge (periphery) of an excised tissue specimen, which for a biopsy must retain sufficient intact, non-thermally-altered architecture for a pathologist to assess margin status and render a diagnosis. According to PubMed, Angiero et al. found diode-laser-excised specimens smaller than 3 mm had significantly more frequent epithelial/stromal change, with diagnosis "not achievable in 46.15%" of such small specimens, and recommend an in vivo specimen diameter of at least 5 mm for reliable histologic reading (Angiero F, Parma L, Crippa R, Benedicenti S. *Lasers Med Sci.* 2011;27(2):383-8. DOI: [10.1007/s10103-011-0900-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10103-011-0900-7), PMID: 21387158). Romeo et al. similarly measured margin alterations (epithelial/connective damage, in mm) per lesion type and suggested enlarging laser biopsies by approximately 0.5 mm to compensate for thermal alteration at the margin (Romeo U, et al. *ScientificWorldJournal.* 2014;2014:761704. DOI: [10.1155/2014/761704](https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/761704), PMID: 25405233).

## นิยามภาษาไทย

Specimen margin (ขอบชิ้นเนื้อ) คือขอบรอยตัดของชิ้นเนื้อที่ตัดออกมา ซึ่งสำหรับการตรวจชิ้นเนื้อ (biopsy) จำเป็นต้องคงโครงสร้างที่สมบูรณ์เพียงพอ ไม่ถูกเปลี่ยนแปลงจากความร้อน เพื่อให้พยาธิแพทย์ประเมินสถานะขอบชิ้นเนื้อและวินิจฉัยได้

## In this vault

Four dental-diode biopsy sources are now processed with full text: [[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield]] (n=608, 808 nm diode, 1.6–2.7 W CW; margin thermal-damage width 260.7–321.4 μm, mean 282.8 μm; recommends ≥5 mm in vivo specimen diameter); [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data]] (n=17, 808 nm diode 2 W CW vs. 532 nm KTP 1.5 W PW, margin damage 0.149–0.473 mm by lesion type, with per-patient diode-only figures now isolated); [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data]] (n=30, 980 nm diode 2 W CW, standardized 5 mm circumferential margin; epithelium/lamina propria thickness significantly greater than healthy control, p=0.021/0.034, yet 100% diagnostic success). [[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data]] adds an ex vivo margin-damage measurement (ETTD 456.15–626.82 μm for a 980 nm diode, plus 80% irregular-incision rate at both settings tested) but is not a diagnostic-biopsy specimen. NEEDS EVIDENCE remains for quantifying specimen-margin condition against a calibrated/measured (as opposed to set) dental diode output value. [[04 Evidence/Tip Initiation and Fiber Condition - Supporting Evidence]] separately documents Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.'s related finding that thermal-damage/necrosis area at peri-incisional margins increased with set power (0.5→2.0 W) regardless of wavelength, in an in vitro porcine model.

## Common confusion

"Margin" here means the physical cut edge of the specimen (a histology/pathology concept), not a laser optical/beam parameter — do not confuse with "spot size" or fiber-tip diameter (see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Fiber Diameter]]).

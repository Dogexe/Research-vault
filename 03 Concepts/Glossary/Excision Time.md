# Excision Time

## Definition
The duration specifically required to complete the excision (cutting) of a specimen — narrower in scope than [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Procedure Time]] (which includes non-cutting steps), but still a total/elapsed duration rather than an instantaneous rate like incision speed.

## Unit / equation
s. Not convertible to a speed without assuming a constant rate across the whole excision, which this vault does not assume unless the source states it.

## In this project
Like procedure time, excision time must not be used to derive incision speed unless a source itself explicitly reports the derived speed value — a given excision time is consistent with many different combinations of incision length and speed, including variable speed during the cut or multiple passes.

## Common pitfalls
Do not treat excision time as interchangeable with incision speed, or assume a constant speed across an excision when only a total excision time is reported.

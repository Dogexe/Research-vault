# Incision Depth

## Definition
How deep a laser incision cuts into tissue, measured histologically from the tissue surface to the deepest point of the cut itself — distinct from [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Thermal Damage]] (the width/depth of thermally altered but not physically cut tissue surrounding the incision). This vault treats "incision depth" and "cutting depth" as one canonical concept, aliased together, since sources use both terms for the same measurement without a stated technical distinction.

## Unit / equation
μm (ex vivo bench studies) or mm (larger-scale comparisons, e.g. Romanos et al. 2013's illustrative figures).

## In this project
A core outcome in several of this project's closest technical precedents: [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data|Goharkhay et al. 1999]] (217.5–647.5 μm across 0.5–4.5 W, 400 μm tip; strongly power-dependent), [[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data|Hanke et al. 2021]] (max 820 μm at 445 nm, 344 μm at 810 nm; the numerator of [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Cutting Efficiency|γz]]), and [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2013 - Diode Laser Soft-Tissue Surgery Review Data|Romanos et al. 2013]] (single illustrative comparison, 0.85 mm fixed-power diode vs. 2.3 mm for a proprietary automatic-power-control system — not a replicated study). This vault's project note lists incision depth/width among its secondary, mechanistic candidate outcomes, distinct from the primary biopsy-oriented specimen-quality outcomes (margin readability, tissue architecture preservation).

## Common pitfalls
Do not equate incision depth with thermal damage depth — a deep cut and a wide thermal-damage zone are related but separately measured quantities, and [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data|Goharkhay et al. 1999]] itself found incision depth correlated with power while collateral thermal-damage width did not. Do not pool incision-depth values across studies with different fiber diameters, tissue models, or tip-initiation status without checking [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Beam Delivery Geometry]].

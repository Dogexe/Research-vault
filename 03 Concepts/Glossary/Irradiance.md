# Irradiance

## Definition

Radiant power incident per unit area on a surface, also called power density in much of the clinical laser literature. Irradiance is an instantaneous/power-based quantity (W/cm²), distinct from fluence, which is a time-integrated, energy-based quantity. Jenkins PA, Carroll JD ("How to report low-level laser therapy (LLLT)/photomedicine dose and beam parameters in clinical and laboratory studies." *Photomed Laser Surg.* 2011;29(12):785-7. DOI: [10.1089/pho.2011.9895](https://doi.org/10.1089/pho.2011.9895)) list "beam area" and "power" among the essential parameters from which irradiance is derived, and caution against conflating aperture size with the actual beam area at the target surface.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

ความเข้มการฉายรังสี (Irradiance) คือกำลังงานที่ตกกระทบต่อหนึ่งหน่วยพื้นที่บนพื้นผิว หรือเรียกว่า power density ในวรรณกรรมทางคลินิกจำนวนมาก เป็นปริมาณเชิงกำลัง/ขณะเวลา (W/cm²) ซึ่งต่างจาก fluence ที่เป็นปริมาณเชิงพลังงานสะสมตามเวลา

## Formula

Irradiance (E) = Power (P) / Area (A).

## Unit

W/cm² (also W/mm² in some dental sources).

## In this vault

[[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]] calculates "power density" (its term for irradiance) from measured power and spot size for all three devices at all three settings (e.g., K2 mobile: 969.2–2715 W/cm²). [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] similarly calculates "power density" for both the diode and Er,Cr:YSGG systems (e.g., diode 1153.8–1923 W/cm²).

## Common confusion

"Power density" (as used by Kim et al. and Al-Ani et al.) is the same physical quantity as irradiance (W/cm²) despite the different name — do not confuse it with "energy density," which this vault's sources use for fluence (J/cm² or kJ/cm²; see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Fluence]]). This W/cm² vs. J/cm² naming inconsistency across "power density"/"energy density" and "irradiance"/"fluence" is common in the clinical laser literature generally, not unique to this vault. NEEDS VERIFICATION for whether all vault sources compute beam area (the denominator) the same way (e.g., at the fiber core vs. an assumed spot size at a working distance).

## Source basis

**Classification:** STANDARD TERM

Irradiance (W/cm²) is explicitly defined in ANSI Z136.1 (American National Standard for Safe Use of Lasers) as the surface exposure dose rate, and is likewise a standard radiometric quantity in ISO 11145 (*Optics and photonics — Lasers and laser-related equipment — Vocabulary and symbols*).

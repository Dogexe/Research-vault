# Photothermal Interaction

## Definition
The overall process by which absorbed laser energy is converted to heat within tissue, driving the sequence of thermal effects (coagulation, vaporization, carbonization) depending on the temperature reached and how quickly it is reached.

## Unit / equation
Not applicable — umbrella mechanism, not itself a measured quantity.

## In this project
This is the umbrella mechanism connecting wavelength/absorption/power/exposure-time to the tissue-interaction outcomes this project measures (thermal damage, coagulation, carbonization). This vault does not model photothermal interaction quantitatively (no heat-transfer simulation); it treats the resulting histologic effects as the observable outcome.

## Common pitfalls
Do not treat "photothermal effect" as synonymous with any one specific outcome — coagulation, vaporization, ablation, and carbonization are distinct effects along a severity/mechanism spectrum, not interchangeable terms (see each individually).

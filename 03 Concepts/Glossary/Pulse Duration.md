# Pulse Duration

## Definition

The length of time the laser is emitting ("on") during a single pulse, in a pulsed operating mode. Also called pulse width. Not applicable to CW mode.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

ระยะเวลาพัลส์ (Pulse Duration หรือ Pulse Width) คือระยะเวลาที่เลเซอร์ปล่อยแสง ("เปิด") ในหนึ่งพัลส์ ระหว่างการทำงานแบบพัลส์ ไม่สามารถใช้กับโหมด CW ได้

## Formula

Duty Cycle = Pulse Duration × Frequency (see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Duty Cycle]]).

## Unit

Typically μs, ms, or s, depending on the device.

## In this vault

Reported pulse durations vary by orders of magnitude across sources: [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] reports 29–50 ms; [[07 Data/Mazzoni et al 2026 - Neonatal Frenulum Laser Parameters]] reports 0.05 ms (50 μs); [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] reports a range of 10 μs–0.99 s for its chopped-pulse mode — the widest single-source range in this vault.

## Common confusion

Do not confuse pulse duration (the on-time of one pulse) with exposure duration (the total irradiation time across possibly many pulses, e.g., Romanos et al.'s 30 s buccal + 30 s lingual exposure, which contains many individual 10 μs–0.99 s pulses).

## Source basis

**Classification:** STANDARD TERM

Pulse duration (pulse width) is explicitly defined in ISO 11145 (*Optics and photonics — Lasers and laser-related equipment — Vocabulary and symbols*) as the time interval between specified fractional-peak-power points on a pulse's leading and trailing edges, and is used identically in ANSI Z136.1 pulsed-laser classification.

# Exposure Time

## Definition
The total duration during which the laser is actively irradiating a given tissue location, whether as a single static application or accumulated across a moving incision. Distinct from pulse duration (the on-time of a single pulse within a pulsed exposure).

## Unit / equation
s (or ms). Fluence = Irradiance × Exposure Time, for constant irradiance.

## In this project
Exposure time is not the same as procedure time or incision speed — for a moving incision, the exposure time at any one tissue point is governed by spot size and incision speed together (a smaller spot or faster speed reduces per-point exposure time even at unchanged power). This vault does not infer exposure time from incision speed unless a source explicitly states or derives it.

## Common pitfalls
Do not confuse exposure time (irradiation duration at a tissue point) with [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Procedure Time]] (total clinical procedure duration, including non-irradiating steps) or [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Pulse Duration]] (on-time of one pulse within a pulsed exposure that may contain many pulses).

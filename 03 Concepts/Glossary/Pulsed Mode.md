# Pulsed Mode

## Definition

An operating mode in which the laser emits light in discrete, repeating on/off cycles (pulses) rather than continuously. Pulsed mode is characterized by pulse duration (on-time), frequency (repetition rate), and duty cycle (fraction of time on), and — where clinically relevant — by peak power (during the on-time) as distinct from average power (time-averaged over on- and off-time). Some sources use "chopped" or "super pulsed" as sub-variants of pulsed mode.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

โหมดพัลส์ (Pulsed Mode) คือโหมดการทำงานที่เลเซอร์ปล่อยแสงเป็นช่วง ๆ (พัลส์) สลับเปิด-ปิดซ้ำ ๆ แทนที่จะปล่อยต่อเนื่อง มีลักษณะเฉพาะคือ pulse duration (ระยะเวลาเปิด) ความถี่ (frequency) และ duty cycle (สัดส่วนเวลาที่เปิด) และในบางกรณีมีกำลังสูงสุดระหว่างช่วงเปิดที่แตกต่างจากกำลังเฉลี่ย บางแหล่งข้อมูลใช้คำว่า "chopped" หรือ "super pulsed" เป็นรูปแบบย่อยของโหมดพัลส์

## In this vault

[[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] uses a "chopped-pulse" mode at 35 Hz, 50% duty cycle, pulse duration 10 μs–0.99 s, alongside CW, on the same device and wavelengths. [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] reports pulsed-mode parameters (pulse duration, frequency, duty cycle) for three devices/wavelengths alongside CW measurements of the same devices. [[07 Data/Mazzoni et al 2026 - Neonatal Frenulum Laser Parameters]] uses a "super pulsed" mode (0.05 ms pulse duration, 20 Hz, 100 W peak power). [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]]'s Er,Cr:YSGG comparator (non-diode) uses "pulsed wave" (PW) terminology with pulse energy (mJ/pulse) rather than pulse duration/frequency as its primary pulse descriptor.

## Common confusion

"Pulsed," "chopped," and "super pulsed" are not used identically across this vault's sources — Romanos et al. uses "chopped-pulse," Mazzoni et al. uses "super pulsed," and neither source defines precisely where the boundary between these sub-terms lies. NEEDS VERIFICATION for a single authoritative distinction between "pulsed," "chopped," and "super pulsed" as applied specifically to dental diode lasers.

## Source basis

**Classification:** STANDARD TERM (base concept); sub-variant labels are FIELD-USAGE

"Pulsed" operation (as opposed to CW) is a standard laser operating-mode category in both ISO 11145 and ANSI Z136.1. The sub-variant labels "chopped" and "super pulsed" used by this vault's sources (see In this vault and Common confusion above) are not defined by either standard and have no single authoritative boundary between them, consistent with this note's existing NEEDS VERIFICATION flag.

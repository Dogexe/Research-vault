# Diagnostic Quality

## Definition

Whether an excised specimen retains enough architecturally intact tissue for a pathologist to render a confident histopathological diagnosis, as opposed to producing an indeterminate or non-diagnostic result due to thermal or processing artifact. According to PubMed, Angiero et al. report diagnosis was "not achievable" in 46.15% of diode-laser-excised specimens smaller than 3 mm, versus reliable diagnosis in larger specimens, directly tying specimen size and thermal damage to diagnostic yield (Angiero F, Parma L, Crippa R, Benedicenti S. *Lasers Med Sci.* 2011;27(2):383-8. DOI: [10.1007/s10103-011-0900-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10103-011-0900-7), PMID: 21387158). Gambino et al. report that, despite measurable laser-induced thermal alteration (mean 288.9 μm epithelial, 430.3 μm connective tissue) at 980 nm diode-laser excision margins, "laser-induced thermal effects did not impair definitive histopathological diagnosis in any specimen" in their series (Gambino A, Magliano A, Haddad GE, Bezzi M, Cafaro A, Karimi D, Broccoletti R, Arduino PG. "Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) Evaluation of Thermal Tissue Alterations After Diode Laser Excision of Oral Leukoplakia (OL)." *Dent J (Basel).* 2026;14(3):168. DOI: [10.3390/dj14030168](https://doi.org/10.3390/dj14030168), PMID: 41892776).

## นิยามภาษาไทย

Diagnostic quality (คุณภาพในการวินิจฉัย) คือความสามารถของชิ้นเนื้อที่ตัดออกมาในการคงเนื้อเยื่อที่มีโครงสร้างสมบูรณ์เพียงพอให้พยาธิแพทย์วินิจฉัยได้อย่างมั่นใจ ตรงข้ามกับผลที่ไม่สามารถวินิจฉัยได้ (indeterminate/non-diagnostic) เนื่องจากสิ่งแปลกปลอมจากความร้อนหรือกระบวนการเตรียมชิ้นเนื้อ

## In this vault

Both sources above are identified, unprocessed candidates that report specimen-size-dependent diagnostic-yield results in apparently different directions (Angiero et al.: diagnosis fails below ~3 mm; Gambino et al.: diagnosis preserved at a standardized 5 mm margin) and should be read together as scoping the same underlying variable (specimen size relative to thermal-alteration depth), not averaged or treated as conflicting. ## Common confusion

Diagnostic quality is an outcome (can the pathologist diagnose it), distinct from [[Thermal Damage]] or [[Histological Artifact]], which are mechanisms/findings that may or may not be severe enough to compromise diagnostic quality in a given specimen.

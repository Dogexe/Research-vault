# Initiated Tip

## Definition

A fiber-optic tip that has been deliberately pre-treated — typically by applying a dark/absorbing material (e.g., articulating paper, ink, cork char) to its end and briefly activating the laser — to create a small carbonized/charred layer at the tip before clinical use. Initiation is intended to increase the tip's absorption of the laser wavelength at the point of contact, which proponents describe as improving the tip's soft-tissue cutting/ablation efficiency, particularly at wavelengths otherwise poorly absorbed by non-pigmented soft tissue. Authoritative dental-laser source: Romanos GE, Sacks D, Montanaro N, Delgado-Ruiz R, Calvo-Guirado JL, Javed F. "Effect of Initiators on Thermal Changes in Soft Tissues Using a Diode Laser." *Photomed Laser Surg.* 2018;36(7):386-390. DOI: [10.1089/pho.2017.4428](https://doi.org/10.1089/pho.2017.4428) — this study experimentally compared several initiator materials (red/blue articulating paper, cork, SureStep) against a non-initiated control on diode-laser soft-tissue incisions. NEEDS VERIFICATION: no ISO/IEC standard definition of "tip initiation" was identified; it is a dental/clinical-laser practice term rather than a formal optics/radiometry term, and sources vary in the specific initiator material and protocol used.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

ปลายไฟเบอร์แบบ Initiated (ผ่านการเตรียมปลาย) คือปลายไฟเบอร์นำแสงที่ถูกเตรียมล่วงหน้าโดยเจตนา มักโดยการนำวัสดุดูดกลืนแสงสีเข้ม (เช่น กระดาษคาร์บอน หมึก ไม้ก๊อกไหม้) มาสัมผัสปลายไฟเบอร์แล้วยิงเลเซอร์สั้น ๆ เพื่อสร้างชั้นไหม้/คาร์บอนบาง ๆ ที่ปลายก่อนใช้งานจริง วัตถุประสงค์คือเพิ่มการดูดกลืนพลังงานเลเซอร์ที่ปลายไฟเบอร์ ซึ่งผู้สนับสนุนอ้างว่าช่วยเพิ่มประสิทธิภาพการตัด/สลายเนื้อเยื่ออ่อน โดยเฉพาะที่ความยาวคลื่นซึ่งเนื้อเยื่อที่ไม่มีเม็ดสีดูดกลืนได้น้อย คำนี้ยังไม่มีนิยามมาตรฐานสากลเดียว (NEEDS VERIFICATION)

## In this vault

Three vault sources use an explicitly initiated tip, each with a different initiator material/protocol: [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] (laser set to 0.5 W, fiber applied to articulating paper until perforation/char formed) and [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] (dark blue articulating paper, Accufilm, applied for 5 sec). [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]], by contrast, explicitly uses an uninitiated/new tip for its power-deviation measurements. Romanos et al. 2022's own Abstract and its own Table 1/Discussion give opposite directional claims about whether initiated tips produce a greater or lesser tissue-penetration depth than non-initiated tips — this unresolved internal inconsistency is preserved in [[04 Evidence/Tip Initiation and Fiber Condition - Supporting Evidence]] and [[06 Synthesis/Research Gaps in Dental Diode Laser Output]] rather than corrected.

## Common confusion

"Initiated" describes the tip's physical/optical condition (a carbonized end), not the operating mode (CW/pulsed) or the contact status (contact/non-contact) — a tip can be initiated and still used in either CW or pulsed mode, and in either contact or non-contact mode, though initiated tips are most often paired with contact mode in this vault's sources. Do not confuse "initiated" with simply "activated" (turning the laser on) — initiation specifically refers to the pre-charring preparation step.

## Source basis

**Classification:** FIELD-USAGE TERM (non-standardized)

No ISO/IEC laser vocabulary standard or ANSI Z136 edition defines "tip initiation" or "initiated tip" — these standards cover laser radiometric/safety terminology, not fiber-tip conditioning technique. The term is established in peer-reviewed dental laser literature (Romanos et al. 2018, cited above) and dental-laser manufacturer practice, consistent with this note's own existing NEEDS VERIFICATION flag naming it a clinical-practice term rather than a formal optics/radiometry term. Kept marked non-standardized per vault policy.

# Transmission

## Definition
The fraction of incident laser light that passes through the tissue (or a given tissue thickness) without being absorbed or scattered out of the beam path.

## Unit / equation
Not applicable in this vault's usage.

## In this project
Relevant conceptually to why laser effect diminishes with depth and why very thin tissue sections can show effects at their far surface. No vault source measures tissue transmission directly.

## Common pitfalls
Do not confuse optical transmission through tissue with "transmission" in a fiber-optic delivery sense (how much power reaches the fiber tip from the console) — [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Power Loss]] covers the latter; this note covers only the tissue-optic sense.

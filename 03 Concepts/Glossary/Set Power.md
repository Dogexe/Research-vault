# Set Power

## Definition

The power value selected or displayed on a laser device's control panel/console before or during use — also called "preset," "panel setting," or "displayed power." It reflects the device's internal target/command value, not an independent measurement of what is actually emitted.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

กำลังที่ตั้งไว้ (Set Power) คือค่ากำลังที่เลือกหรือแสดงบนหน้าปัด/คอนโซลของเครื่องเลเซอร์ก่อนหรือระหว่างการใช้งาน หรือเรียกอีกอย่างว่า preset, panel setting, displayed power เป็นค่าเป้าหมายภายในของเครื่อง ไม่ใช่ค่าที่วัดได้อย่างอิสระว่าเครื่องปล่อยแสงออกมาจริงเท่าใด

## Unit

W (or mW for lower-power devices).

## In this vault

Every core source records set power as the control-panel value chosen by the researchers: Parker et al. 2022's 100 mW/500 mW/1.0 W/2.0 W panel values; Kim et al. 2020's "Output Power" column (its label, not this vault's — see Common confusion). [[03 Concepts/Laser Presetting]] documents this vault's standing convention of recording set power strictly separately from measured power.

## Common confusion

Kim et al. 2020's own column header is literally "Output Power" for what this vault classifies as Set Power — a direct example of why this vault does not treat a source's own label as sufficient on its own; the underlying value (dial-set vs. independently measured) must be checked. Do not assume set power reflects actual emission — see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Power Loss]].

## Source basis

**Classification:** FIELD-USAGE TERM

No ISO/IEC laser vocabulary standard or ANSI Z136 edition defines "set power" — those standards define the measured/emitted physical quantity ("power"), not a device's internal command/display value. "Set power" (and its synonyms "preset," "panel setting," "displayed power") is a clinical/device-operational term used throughout the dental laser literature cited in this vault (see In this vault above, e.g. Kim et al. 2020, Parker et al. 2022).

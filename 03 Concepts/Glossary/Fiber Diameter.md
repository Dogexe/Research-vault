# Fiber Diameter

## Definition

The physical diameter of the optical fiber core (or, where specified, the full fiber including cladding) used to deliver laser energy from the console to the treatment site, typically measured in micrometers (μm). Fiber diameter, together with any focusing/divergence at the tip, determines the beam's spot size at the target and therefore its irradiance for a given power.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

เส้นผ่านศูนย์กลางไฟเบอร์ (Fiber Diameter) คือขนาดเส้นผ่านศูนย์กลางทางกายภาพของแกนไฟเบอร์นำแสง (หรือรวมส่วนหุ้มถ้าระบุไว้) ที่ใช้นำพลังงานเลเซอร์จากเครื่องไปยังตำแหน่งรักษา มักวัดเป็นไมโครเมตร (μm) ขนาดไฟเบอร์ร่วมกับลักษณะการโฟกัส/กระจายแสงที่ปลาย จะกำหนดขนาดจุดลำแสงที่เป้าหมาย และดังนั้นจึงกำหนดความเข้มการฉายรังสี (irradiance) ที่กำลังใดกำลังหนึ่ง

## Unit

μm (micrometers).

## In this vault

Reported fiber diameters range from 300 μm ([[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]; [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]'s 300 μm fiber with 280 μm core) to 800 μm ([[07 Data/Fahlstedt et al 2021 - Laser Operating Parameters]]). [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] uses two different diameters for its two devices (SIROLaser Blue 320 μm; Picasso 300 μm); [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]] uses one shared 400 μm tip across all three devices to control for this variable; [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] and [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] use 320 μm and 400 μm, respectively.

## Common confusion

Fiber (core) diameter is not the same as the beam spot size at the target, which can differ due to divergence, working distance (non-contact mode), or tip shaping. [[07 Data/Girasol et al 2022 - Photobiomodulation Device Power and Beam Accuracy]] measured actual beam diameter directly (for non-fiber-delivered PBM probe devices) and found it differed from the manufacturer's nominal value by 38%–543% — a reminder that a stated dimension cannot be assumed accurate even when reported.

## Source basis

**Classification:** FIELD-USAGE TERM

Fiber core diameter is a physical specification governed by fiber-optic component standards (e.g., the IEC 60793 optical-fibre series) rather than by laser vocabulary standards (ISO 11145) or laser safety standards (ANSI Z136), neither of which defines "fiber diameter" as a laser-dosimetry term. In this vault it is reported as a device/delivery specification drawn directly from the dental laser literature (see In this vault above).

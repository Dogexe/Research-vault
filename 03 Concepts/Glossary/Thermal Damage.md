# Thermal Damage

## Definition

Histologically observable structural alteration to cells and extracellular matrix caused by heat conducted from a laser incision into adjacent, non-target tissue — commonly assessed as a linear "thermal damage zone" width or depth measured from the cut margin. According to PubMed, Angiero et al. measured "the extent of the thermal damage zone" for CO2 vs. 808 nm diode laser oral excisional biopsies, reporting values in μm and by histopathological index score (Angiero F, Parma L, Crippa R, Benedicenti S. "Diode laser (808 nm) applied to oral soft tissue lesions: a retrospective study to assess histopathological diagnosis and evaluate physical damage." *Lasers Med Sci.* 2011;27(2):383-8. DOI: [10.1007/s10103-011-0900-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10103-011-0900-7), PMID: 21387158). Azevedo et al. similarly quantified "Tissue Damage Extension" in μm across several laser types including a diode laser (Azevedo AS, Monteiro LS, Ferreira F, Delgado ML, Garcês F, Carreira S, Martins M, Suarez-Quintanilla J. "Histological evaluation of the surgical margins made by different laser wavelengths in tongue tissues." *J Clin Exp Dent.* 2016;8(4):e388-e396. DOI: [10.4317/jced.52830](https://doi.org/10.4317/jced.52830), PMID: 27703606).

## นิยามภาษาไทย

Thermal damage (ความเสียหายจากความร้อน) คือการเปลี่ยนแปลงโครงสร้างของเซลล์และเนื้อเยื่อเกี่ยวพันที่มองเห็นได้ทางจุลพยาธิวิทยา ซึ่งเกิดจากความร้อนที่นำจากรอยตัดเลเซอร์เข้าสู่เนื้อเยื่อข้างเคียงที่ไม่ใช่เป้าหมาย มักประเมินเป็นความกว้างหรือความลึกของ "โซนความเสียหายจากความร้อน" วัดจากขอบรอยตัด

## In this vault

Existing background evidence [[04 Evidence/Tip Initiation and Fiber Condition - Supporting Evidence]] already documents thermal-damage-adjacent findings from Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. (histologic thermal-damage area and necrosis area, 0.5–2.0 W, six wavelengths) and Romanos et al. (histologic penetration depth of thermal effect, 445/970 nm, 2 W). Four dental-diode biopsy sources are now processed with full text: Azevedo et al. 2016 measured Extent of Thermal Tissue Damage (ETTD) for a 980 nm diode laser in ex vivo porcine tongue: 456.15 μm at 3.5 W PW and 626.82 μm at 3.5 W Boost PW — notably, the higher-ETTD "Boost" setting had a *lower* macroscopic carbonization score (1.80) than the standard setting (2.50), showing that ETTD and carbonization degree do not track together within one device's settings even though they correlate across instrument types pooled (r=0.789) ([[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data]]). Angiero et al. 2011 (n=608, 808 nm diode) measured thermal-damage width directly: range 260.7–321.4 μm, mean 282.8 μm ([[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield]]). Gambino et al. 2026 (n=30, 980 nm diode, 2 W CW) measured mean thermal damage of 288.9 μm (epithelium) and 430.3 μm (connective tissue) by histology, cross-validated against OCT (88.5% concordance) ([[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data]]). None of these sources pairs the thermal-damage measurement with a calibrated/verified delivered-power value — all use set/console power. See [[04 Evidence/Thermal Artifact]]. NEEDS EVIDENCE for a dental-diode source that pairs a calibrated measured-output value with a quantified thermal-damage-zone histology outcome.

## Common confusion

Contrast with [[Coagulation Zone]] (a specific sub-region of thermal damage, immediately adjacent to the cut, defined by protein coagulation rather than the full damage extent) and [[Carbonization]] (visible char, a more severe/superficial thermal effect, distinct from the deeper, non-carbonized thermal damage zone).

## Source basis

**Classification:** FIELD-USAGE TERM

Widely used descriptively in oral/laser-surgery histopathology literature; no single ISO/ANSI unit definition — reported values are source-specific (μm, histopathological index scores).

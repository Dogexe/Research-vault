# Non-initiated Tip

## Definition

A fiber-optic tip used without the deliberate pre-charring/conditioning process described under Initiated Tip — i.e., a "bare," "new," or "uninitiated" fiber end, relying on the tissue's own absorption of the laser wavelength (e.g., hemoglobin/melanin absorption) rather than an added carbonized layer at the tip.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

ปลายไฟเบอร์แบบ Non-initiated (ไม่ผ่านการเตรียมปลาย) คือปลายไฟเบอร์ที่ใช้งานโดยไม่ผ่านขั้นตอนการเตรียม/ทำให้ไหม้ล่วงหน้าตามที่อธิบายไว้ใน Initiated Tip กล่าวคือเป็นปลายไฟเบอร์ "เปล่า" หรือ "ใหม่" ที่อาศัยการดูดกลืนแสงของเนื้อเยื่อเอง (เช่น ฮีโมโกลบิน/เมลานิน) แทนชั้นคาร์บอนที่เพิ่มเข้าไปที่ปลาย

## In this vault

[[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]] deliberately used a non-initiated ("non-initialized") bare fiber, explicitly to "ensure a pure tissue interaction... without the hot effect of initialized fibres." [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] also used an uninitiated/new tip, for a power-measurement study rather than a tissue-interaction study. [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] tested non-initiated tips as one arm of a within-study initiated-vs-non-initiated comparison.

## Common confusion

Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.'s framing implies non-initiated tips produce less added thermal effect than initiated tips ("without the hot effect of initialized fibres") — but Romanos et al.'s own Table 1/Discussion data show initiated tips producing *greater* penetration depth than non-initiated tips in every significant comparison (directionally consistent with Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.'s framing), while Romanos et al.'s own Abstract states the opposite direction. This vault does not resolve which of Romanos et al.'s own statements is correct; see [[04 Evidence/Tip Initiation and Fiber Condition - Supporting Evidence]]. NEEDS VERIFICATION.

## Source basis

**Classification:** FIELD-USAGE TERM (non-standardized)

As with [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Initiated Tip]], no ISO/IEC laser vocabulary standard or ANSI Z136 edition defines "non-initiated tip." The term is established by contrast with "initiated tip" in the same peer-reviewed dental laser literature (see In this vault above). Kept marked non-standardized per vault policy.

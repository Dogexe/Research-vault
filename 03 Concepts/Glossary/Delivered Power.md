# Delivered Power

## Definition

The power actually reaching, and available to interact with, the treatment target (e.g., tissue) — conceptually downstream of measured power, since further loss can occur between the point of measurement (often the fiber tip in air) and the actual target (e.g., due to working distance in non-contact mode, contact-tip fouling, or tissue-coupling effects). NEEDS VERIFICATION: "delivered power" is not a formally standardized term with one universal definition in the laser-physics or biomedical-optics sources reviewed for this vault; it is used descriptively/operationally in clinical literature, sometimes as a loose synonym for measured power and sometimes to mean something further downstream (power actually incident on or absorbed by tissue).

## In this vault

No source in this vault separately reports "delivered power" as distinct from "measured power at the fiber tip." The closest example is [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]], whose contact-mode study group shows progressively declining fiber-tip power with repeated tissue contact (attributed to tip fouling) — arguably a delivered-power effect occurring at the measurement point itself, rather than a separately measured downstream quantity. This vault treats "measured power" (an independently instrument-read value) and "delivered power" (a target-incident value) as distinct concepts by design, even though no vault source measures both simultaneously.

## Common confusion

"Delivered power," "measured power," and "set power" are frequently used interchangeably in casual clinical writing. Per this glossary's authoring rules, this vault does **not** treat them as automatically equivalent, even where a specific source does not make the distinction explicit.

## Source basis

**Classification:** VAULT-DEFINED TERM (non-standardized)

No ISO/IEC laser vocabulary standard, ANSI Z136 edition, or PBM dosimetry consensus reference defines "delivered power" as a quantity distinct from measured power. This vault deliberately maintains "delivered power" as a separate concept from [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Measured Power]] (target-incident value vs. instrument-read value) as an internal convention for source-tracking rigor — not because any reviewed standard or field source enforces the distinction — consistent with the NEEDS VERIFICATION flag already in the Definition above. Kept marked non-standardized per vault policy.

# Biopsy Specimen Quality

## Definition
This project's composite primary outcome concept — the overall adequacy of an excised specimen for histopathologic interpretation, treated as spanning thermal artifact, margin readability/quality, and tissue architecture preservation together, rather than any single measurement alone.

## Unit / equation
Not applicable — a composite concept, not a single scored instrument in this vault's current literature.

## In this project
This is the canonical project-level outcome concept named in [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]]'s "Primary experimental outcomes" and referenced throughout the Novelty Matrix and Study Dashboard. Thermal damage alone is explicitly not treated as equivalent to overall specimen quality — a specimen can show measurable, statistically significant thermal alteration (e.g. [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data|Gambino et al. 2026]]) and still be fully diagnostic. No source in this vault yet defines or applies a single formal instrument scoring all three components together in an ex vivo, biopsy-oriented, measured-power design — this is the project's stated central remaining gap.

## Common pitfalls
Do not reduce "specimen quality" to thermal damage width alone, and do not assume a diagnostic-outcome claim is expected or meaningful for ex vivo/bench studies, which are not designed to render a diagnosis (see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Diagnostic Quality]]).

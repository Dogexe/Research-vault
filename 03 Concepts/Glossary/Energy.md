# Energy

## Definition
Energy is the total work/heat capacity delivered by the laser over an exposure, in joules (J). It is the time-integral of power: constant power P applied for time t delivers energy E = P × t.

## Unit / equation
J; E = P × t.

## In this project
Total delivered energy (not just power) governs cumulative thermal loading of tissue, but this vault does not compute or infer energy for any study unless the source itself reports it — see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Power]] and [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Energy per Unit Length]] for why this matters when comparing studies with different exposure times or incision speeds.

## Common pitfalls
Do not confuse a console's "power" setting with total energy — the same power delivered longer, or over a longer incision, represents more total energy even with an unchanged power reading.

# Optical Frequency

## Definition
Optical frequency (ν) is the number of oscillations per second of the light wave itself, related to wavelength by ν = c/λ (c = speed of light). For the visible/near-infrared diode wavelengths used in this literature, this is on the order of 10^14 Hz.

## Unit / equation
Hz; ν = c / λ.

## In this project
Optical frequency is used only conceptually (e.g. in photon-energy reasoning); no source in this vault reports or requires it directly, since all vault literature specifies wavelength (nm) rather than optical frequency.

## Common pitfalls
Optical frequency (~10^14 Hz, a property of the light wave) must never be confused with [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Frequency|pulse repetition rate]] (typically 1–100s of Hz, a property of the pulsing electronics) — the two differ by roughly 12 orders of magnitude and describe entirely different things.

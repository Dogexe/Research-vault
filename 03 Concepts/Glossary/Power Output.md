# Power Output

## Definition

The optical power emitted by a laser device, or delivered at a specified point (e.g., an aperture or fiber tip), at a given moment, expressed in watts (W). "Power output" is a general umbrella term — by itself it does not specify the measurement location, the averaging window, or whether it refers to a console-displayed value or an independently measured value. Standard laser/biomedical dosimetry guidance (Jenkins PA, Carroll JD. "How to report low-level laser therapy (LLLT)/photomedicine dose and beam parameters in clinical and laboratory studies." *Photomed Laser Surg.* 2011;29(12):785-7. DOI: [10.1089/pho.2011.9895](https://doi.org/10.1089/pho.2011.9895)) lists "power" as one of the eight essential beam parameters a study must report, precisely because the term is otherwise ambiguous without further specification.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

กำลังขาออก (Power Output) หมายถึงกำลังแสงเลเซอร์ (optical power) ที่ปล่อยออกจากอุปกรณ์ หรือวัดได้ ณ ตำแหน่งที่ระบุ (เช่น ปลายไฟเบอร์) ในขณะใดขณะหนึ่ง มีหน่วยเป็นวัตต์ (W) คำนี้เป็นคำกลางที่ยังไม่ได้ระบุตำแหน่งวัด ช่วงเวลาเฉลี่ย หรือว่าเป็นค่าที่หน้าปัดแสดงหรือค่าที่วัดได้จริง จึงจำเป็นต้องระบุเพิ่มเติมเสมอ

## Unit

Watt (W); milliwatt (mW) for lower-power devices.

## In this vault

This vault's broader concept note [[03 Concepts/Power Output]] records power output strictly tagged by its reported measurement location — e.g., "post-fibre emission" in Parker et al. 2022, "at the fiber tip" in Zegaib et al. 2011 and Kim et al. 2020. This glossary entry is the umbrella term; see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Set Power]], [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Measured Power]], and [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Delivered Power]] for the three distinct sub-concepts this vault does not treat as interchangeable.

## Common confusion

"Power output" is often used loosely in clinical papers to mean whichever value (set, measured, or delivered) the paper happens to focus on. Kim et al. 2020's own data table literally labels its dial-set value "Output Power" even though, in this vault's terminology, that column is Set Power, not an independently measured output. Always check which specific quantity a given instance of "power output" refers to before comparing it across sources.

## Source basis

**Classification:** FIELD-USAGE TERM

The underlying physical quantity, radiant power (W), is standardized in ISO 11145 and required reporting per PBM dosimetry guidance (Jenkins & Carroll 2011, cited above, which lists "power" among the eight essential beam parameters precisely because it is otherwise ambiguous without specifying measurement location/instrument). "Power output" as a generic umbrella label — without specifying whether it means set, measured, or delivered power — is not itself a distinct term defined by any standard reviewed; it is a field-usage convention, as this note's own Common confusion section documents.

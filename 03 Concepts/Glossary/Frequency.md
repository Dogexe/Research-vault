# Frequency

## Definition

The number of pulses emitted per unit time in a pulsed operating mode, also called the pulse repetition rate. Not applicable to CW mode.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

ความถี่ (Frequency) คือจำนวนพัลส์ที่ปล่อยออกมาต่อหน่วยเวลาในโหมดพัลส์ หรือเรียกว่า pulse repetition rate ไม่สามารถใช้กับโหมด CW ได้

## Formula

f = 1 / T, where T is the period (time from the start of one pulse to the start of the next). Duty Cycle = Pulse Duration × f (see [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Duty Cycle]]).

## Unit

Hz (pulses per second).

## In this vault

## Common confusion

Frequency (pulse repetition rate, Hz) should not be confused with optical frequency (related to wavelength, on the order of 10^14 Hz) — in every vault source, "frequency" refers exclusively to pulse repetition rate, never optical frequency.

## Source basis

**Classification:** STANDARD TERM

Pulse repetition frequency is a standard pulsed-laser parameter defined in ISO 11145 (*Optics and photonics — Lasers and laser-related equipment — Vocabulary and symbols*) and referenced in ANSI Z136.1 hazard classification for pulsed lasers.

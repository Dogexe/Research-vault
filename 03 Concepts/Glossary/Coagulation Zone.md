# Coagulation Zone

## Definition

The band of tissue immediately adjacent to a laser incision in which protein denaturation/coagulation has occurred without carbonization, histologically visible as altered eosinophilic staining and loss of normal cellular/nuclear detail. According to PubMed, Capodiferro et al. found that in diode-laser-excised oral specimens (2–7 W), "changes induced by laser such as coagulation of proteins were present only with high power density output" (Capodiferro S, Maiorano E, Loiudice AM, Scarpelli F, Favia G. "Oral laser surgical pathology: a preliminary study on the clinical advantages of diode laser and on the histopathological features of specimens evaluated by conventional and confocal laser scanning microscopy." *Minerva Stomatol.* 2008;57(1-2):1-6. PMID: 18427366 — no DOI available in the source record).

## นิยามภาษาไทย

Coagulation zone (โซนการแข็งตัวของโปรตีน) คือแถบเนื้อเยื่อบริเวณติดกับรอยตัดเลเซอร์ที่เกิดการแข็งตัว/เสียสภาพของโปรตีนโดยไม่มีการไหม้เป็นถ่าน มองเห็นได้ทางจุลพยาธิวิทยาจากการติดสีอีโอซิน (eosinophilic) ที่เปลี่ยนไปและรายละเอียดของเซลล์/นิวเคลียสที่หายไป

## In this vault

No vault source yet *scores or quantifies* a coagulation zone specifically for a dental diode laser (that remains Capodiferro et al. 2008's territory — now processed to an abstract-level data extraction, [[07 Data/Capodiferro et al 2008 - Oral Laser Surgical Pathology Abstract-Level Data]], but still unprocessed at full-text level; see [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]]). Two now-processed sources describe coagulation-related findings qualitatively without isolating them as a distinct measured zone: Angiero et al. 2011 lists "intraluminal clotted erythrocytes," "vascular stasis," and "thrombosed or collapsed blood/lymphatic vessels" among the vascular thermal-change descriptors observed ([[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield]]); Gambino et al. 2026 characterizes connective-tissue thermal alteration as "basophilic changes consistent with hemocoagulative phenomena," and its OCT protocol specifically identifies "hyper-reflective areas... corresponding to hypercoagulated vessels" ([[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data]]). NEEDS EVIDENCE remains for a dental-diode source that scores or measures a coagulation zone as its own variable (as opposed to a component of a broader thermal-damage-width measurement), still less one paired with a calibrated/measured power value.

**Not to be confused with Capodiferro et al. 2009** ([[07 Data/Capodiferro et al 2009 - Diode Laser Chondroid Lipoma Case Report Data]]), a separate paper by the same author group (n=1 case report, 880 nm, 2.5 W CW) that does not mention coagulation at all and does not add evidence to this gap — the two Capodiferro papers are tracked independently in this vault and neither substitutes for the other.

## Common confusion

A coagulation zone is a sub-region of the broader [[Thermal Damage]] zone (protein coagulation without char), not synonymous with it; more severe or more superficial heating produces [[Carbonization]] instead of, or in addition to, coagulation.

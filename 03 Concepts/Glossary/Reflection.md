# Reflection

## Definition
The fraction of incident laser light that bounces off the tissue surface without entering the tissue, determined largely by the refractive-index mismatch at the tissue surface and the angle of incidence.

## Unit / equation
Not applicable in this vault's usage.

## In this project
Surface reflection is one of the four possible fates of incident light at a tissue surface (absorption, scattering, reflection, transmission) — mentioned here for conceptual completeness. No vault source measures or reports a reflection coefficient for oral soft tissue.

## Common pitfalls
Do not assume a laser's full set/measured power is delivered into the tissue — surface reflection (along with any air-gap loss in non-contact mode) is a real, if usually unquantified in this literature, loss pathway between measured power and true absorbed dose.

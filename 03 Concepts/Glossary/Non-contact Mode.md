# Non-contact Mode

## Definition

A delivery technique in which the fiber tip (or beam-emitting aperture) is held at a distance from the target tissue during laser application, rather than touching it, relying on the beam traveling through air (or a defined working distance) to reach the target.

## นิยามภาษาไทย

โหมดไม่สัมผัส (Non-contact Mode) คือเทคนิคการใช้งานที่ปลายไฟเบอร์ (หรือช่องปล่อยลำแสง) อยู่ห่างจากเนื้อเยื่อเป้าหมายระหว่างการฉายเลเซอร์ แทนที่จะสัมผัสโดยตรง โดยอาศัยลำแสงเดินทางผ่านอากาศ (หรือระยะทำงานที่กำหนด) ไปถึงเป้าหมาย

## In this vault

[[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]'s non-contact control group (no contact with teeth or gingival tissue) showed minimal output drift (0.14%–1.58%) across repeated firings, in contrast to its contact-mode study group's much larger progressive loss — used by the source to attribute the study group's loss specifically to contact-induced tip fouling rather than intrinsic fiber degradation. [[07 Data/Girasol et al 2022 - Photobiomodulation Device Power and Beam Accuracy]]'s devices were measured in a non-contact bench setup (positioned to irradiate a power sensor directly at perpendicular incidence), though this is a non-dental, non-fiber-delivered probe context. [[07 Data/Davies et al 2020 - EVLA Fiber Tip Power Output and Degradation]]'s EVLA fiber tip was suspended in a water bath rather than tissue — a non-contact bench arrangement for a non-dental application.

## Common confusion

Non-contact mode is not the same as "non-initiated tip" — a tip could in principle be initiated (pre-charred) yet still used at a working distance rather than in direct tissue contact, though no source in this vault reports this specific combination; the vault's non-contact examples (Zegaib et al.'s control group; Girasol et al.; Davies et al.) all also happen to be non-initiated/non-fiber-tip-charred contexts. This co-occurrence is a property of the sources reviewed, not a logical necessity.

## Source basis

**Classification:** FIELD-USAGE TERM

As with [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Contact Mode]], no ISO/IEC laser vocabulary standard, ANSI Z136 edition, or PBM dosimetry consensus reference defines "non-contact mode" as a formal term. It is used operationally in the dental/clinical laser literature cited in this vault (see In this vault above).

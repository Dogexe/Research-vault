# Vaporization

## Definition
The tissue-interaction endpoint in which local temperature rise is sufficient to convert intracellular/extracellular water to steam, physically disrupting and removing tissue — a more severe effect than coagulation, generally occurring at the immediate incision surface.

## Unit / equation
Not applicable.

## In this project
Vaporization (rather than coagulation alone) is generally understood as the primary tissue-removal mechanism for laser cutting, with coagulation and thermal damage occurring in the zone just beyond the vaporized tissue. This vault does not have a source that separately scores vaporization as a distinct histologic finding from carbonization or the thermal damage zone.

## Common pitfalls
Do not conflate vaporization (tissue removal via steam formation) with carbonization (visible char from pyrolysis) — vaporization removes tissue outright, while carbonization blackens tissue that remains in place at the margin.

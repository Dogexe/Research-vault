## Paper Summary

### Citation

Xue et al. 2022 — "Power Output of Two Semiconductor Lasers: An Observational Study." DOI: [10.3390/photonics9040219](https://doi.org/10.3390/photonics9040219). Full author list, journal name, volume/issue/pages: UNKNOWN (not recorded anywhere in this vault's notes — not invented here).

### Study type

Ex vivo/bench observational study (device power-output survey).

### Research question / purpose

Whether the measured output power of two dental diode laser devices, across multiple wavelengths and both CW and pulsed modes, matches the console preset (set) power.

### Study design

Bench measurement of two devices (SIROLaser Blue: 445/970 nm; Picasso: 810 nm) at fixed console presets (3 W / 2 W / 2.5 W respectively), in both CW and pulsed modes, using calibrated power meters (PowerMax Pro, LabMax-Pro). Each condition was repeated 10 times. See [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] for the full parameter table.

### Main findings

- Measured mean CW output deviated from the console preset in both directions depending on device/wavelength: +21%, +22%, and −8% for 445 nm, 970 nm, and 810 nm respectively.
- In pulsed mode, deviations were +37%, +26%, and −3% for the same three wavelengths.
- The direction and magnitude of deviation is device- and mode-dependent, not a single universal "loss" factor.
- This paper is one of only a small number of vault sources ([[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers|Kim et al. 2020]] being the other clear case) to show measured output exceeding the console preset, rather than only falling short of it — see [[06 Synthesis/Current Evidence on Diode Laser Power Output]].

### What this paper supports

- That measured/delivered power can differ from console/set power in either direction (over- or under-delivery), and that this direction is device- and mode-specific rather than a fixed universal correction factor.
- That set power and measured output should be recorded and reported as two distinct, separately tracked values for dental diode devices — reused directly as this vault's [[04 Evidence/Set Power vs Measured Output]] claim.

### What this paper does NOT support

- Manufacturer tolerance claims and this study's own measured deviation are two distinct things and must not be blended: this paper reports what was actually measured under its own bench protocol, not a validation or refutation of any manufacturer-stated tolerance specification — no manufacturer tolerance/spec figure is extracted or compared against in the source as recorded in this vault.
- It is NOT manufacturer-preset validation in the biopsy sense — this is a bench power-output survey, not a study of any specific clinical treatment preset's effect on tissue or diagnostic outcome.
- It does NOT provide any tissue-interaction, thermal-damage, or histopathologic specimen-quality data — no tissue, biopsy, or diagnostic outcome appears in this study at all.
- Set power ≠ delivered power is exactly what this paper demonstrates, but it should not be read as establishing incision-speed or specimen-quality implications, which are outside its scope.

### Key limitations

- Only two devices and three wavelengths tested; findings are device-specific and should not be generalized to other dental diode devices.
- Bench/fiber-to-sensor measurement only, no tissue interaction tested.
- No manufacturer-stated tolerance specification is captured in this vault's extraction to compare against the measured deviation.

### Relevance to current project

Contributes specifically to the **console set power → actual delivered power** link of the project's conceptual chain, as direct, mixed-direction (not uniformly "loss") power-deviation evidence. Does not extend into incision speed, tissue interaction, or specimen-quality links.

### Role in the project

- Delivered-power evidence

### One-line takeaway

Important measured-output evidence showing that dental diode devices can deliver power above or below their console preset depending on device/mode, but it says nothing about manufacturer tolerance specifications, tissue interaction, or biopsy outcome.

### สรุปภาษาไทย

งานวิจัยนี้เป็นการศึกษาเชิงสังเกตแบบวัดกำลังขาออกจริง (bench observational study) ของเลเซอร์ไดโอด 2 เครื่อง (3 ความยาวคลื่น: 445, 970, 810 nm) โดยเปรียบเทียบค่าที่ตั้งบนแผงควบคุมกับค่าที่วัดได้จริงด้วยเพาเวอร์มิเตอร์ที่สอบเทียบแล้ว ทั้งในโหมดต่อเนื่อง (CW) และโหมดพัลส์ (PW)

ผลสำคัญคือ ค่ากำลังที่วัดได้จริงเบี่ยงเบนจากค่าที่ตั้งไว้ได้**ทั้งสองทิศทาง** (สูงกว่าหรือต่ำกว่า) ขึ้นอยู่กับเครื่องและโหมด ไม่ใช่ค่าการสูญเสียคงที่ค่าเดียว เช่น ที่ 445 nm กำลังจริงสูงกว่าค่าตั้งถึง +21% ถึง +37% ในขณะที่ 810 nm กำลังจริงต่ำกว่าค่าตั้งเล็กน้อย (−3% ถึง −8%)

งานนี้เป็นหลักฐานสำคัญสำหรับส่วน "ค่าตั้งบนเครื่อง ≠ กำลังที่ส่งออกจริง" ของโครงการ แต่**ไม่ใช่**หลักฐานเกี่ยวกับค่าความคลาดเคลื่อนที่ผู้ผลิตระบุไว้ (manufacturer tolerance) และไม่มีข้อมูลด้านเนื้อเยื่อ ความร้อน หรือคุณภาพชิ้นเนื้อ biopsy ใด ๆ

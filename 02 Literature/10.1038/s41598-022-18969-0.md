## Paper Summary

### Citation

Hohmann M, Kühn D, Späth M, Rohde M, Stelzle F, Klämpfl F, Schmidt M. Development and evaluation of a scoring system for assessing incisions in laser surgery. *Sci Rep*. 2022;12(1):14741. DOI: [10.1038/s41598-022-18969-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-18969-0)

### Study type

Ex vivo methodological study (scoring-system development and inter-rater reliability).

### Research question / purpose

Can a fast macroscopic scoring system rate laser incisions reliably, as an alternative to slow histologic staining that samples only a small section of the cut?

### Study design

CO2-laser cuts with varied, unreported parameters on fresh pig muscle. A 1–5 score was defined from tissue colour and darkened-area fraction, scored separately at the cutting area (CA) and cutting edge (CE). Twelve optics scientists scored 115 photographed cuts: six untrained, six trained with 25 feedback images. Agreement was measured by ICC3 (absolute agreement).

### Main findings

- ICC for untrained raters was 0.71 for CA and 0.62 for CE; for trained raters it was 0.69 for CA and 0.66 for CE.
- CA and CE scores differ systematically (Spearman between them 0.41), so the two regions capture partly different information.
- A clear learning effect: ICC rose ~0.2 from the first to the last third, so the authors advise practice on ~100–200 samples.

### What this paper supports

- That a colour + darkened-area macroscopic score can reach moderate inter-rater reliability (ICC ~0.6–0.7) on photographs of ex vivo laser cuts.
- That rater training and a practice set should come before scoring study data.

### What this paper does NOT support

- It is not a gold standard and is not validated against histology or clinical healing (stated by the authors).
- It is not validated for diode lasers, contact cutting, oral mucosa, or fresh-tissue scoring.
- It does not establish a "desirable" score cut-off.

### Key limitations

One photograph per cut; raters were not laser-surgery experts; pig muscle ex vivo; laser parameters unreported; the Table 2/4 caption does not match its column labels (see 07 Data note).

### Relevance to current project

Feeds the **tissue interaction → immediate cutting outcome** step. It is the source the advisor named for the colour, darkened-area and carbonisation components of the study-defined desirable-incision criteria, and for the rater training and reliability procedure.

### Role in the project

- Methodological support

### One-line takeaway

The main published macroscopic scoring precedent for immediate laser-incision quality, usable here only as an adapted score that is re-tested for reliability in the pilot.

# Literature Index

Navigation index for the diode-biopsy project. Categories reproduce the approved Zotero role and status tags; they do not add extraction data or scientific interpretation. A paper may appear in more than one role.

## Reading priority

Priority reflects RQ relevance, novelty/prior-art value, and outcome/method contribution — not whether every parameter is reported. `UNKNOWN`, unmeasured delivered power, or unreported speed remain reporting limitations, not automatic downgrades.

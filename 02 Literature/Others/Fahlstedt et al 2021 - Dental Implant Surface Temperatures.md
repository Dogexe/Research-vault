## Study

- Objective: To estimate titanium dental-implant surface temperature during calibrated double-wavelength laser irradiation.
- Design: In-vitro calibration and temperature experiment.
- Sample or material: Pristine titanium implants: OsseoSpeed, TiUnite, and Roxolid SLActive.
- Clinical or laboratory context: Laboratory experiment using a computerized robotic setup.

## Device and operating parameters

- Device: Double-wavelength system incorporating a 940 nm diode laser; model: UNKNOWN.

## Findings

- FACT: For the 940 nm diode laser, estimated fibre-tip output power was 13.0%–23.8% lower than device setting (mean 20.0%) across the tested settings (§3.1.1).
- FACT: In the experimental protocol, a 4.3 W diode device setting corresponded to 3.3 W measured output power (Table 2).
- FACT: The authors reported no implant-surface alterations from the 940 nm diode laser at the output powers tested (Figure 4 caption).
- FACT: During calibrated double-wavelength irradiation under the study conditions, implant-surface temperature decreased from baseline over the first 20 seconds (§3.2).

## Paper Summary

### Citation

Goharkhay K, Moritz A, Wilder-Smith P, Schoop U, Kluger W, Jakolitsch S, Sperr W. "Effects on Oral Soft Tissue Produced by a Diode Laser In Vitro." *Lasers Surg Med.* 1999;25(5):401-406. DOI: [10.1002/(SICI)1096-9101(1999)25:5<401::AID-LSM6>3.0.CO;2-U](https://doi.org/10.1002/(SICI)1096-9101(1999)25:5%3C401::AID-LSM6%3E3.0.CO;2-U).

### Study type

Ex vivo experimental study (standardized incision/collateral-damage characterization).

### Research question / purpose

Whether an 810 nm diode laser's measured output power, operating mode (CW vs. two pulsed settings), and fiber tip diameter affect incision depth and collateral thermal damage in oral soft tissue.

### Study design

198 standardized 3 cm incisions across 33 parameter combinations (7 measured power levels × up to 3 modes × 2 fiber tips) in ex vivo pig mandible mucosa, no lesion present. Incisions were made with a motorized handpiece at a fixed 10 mm/s speed and evaluated histologically for incision depth/width and vertical/horizontal collateral thermal damage. Notably, the reported wattages are genuinely measured at the fiber outflow with a wattmeter, not the console/panel display value — the panel value itself is not reported. See [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data]] for the full parameter table.

### Main findings

- Incision depth and width correlated strongly and positively with measured power in both CW and pulsed modes, for both fiber tips — a clean dose-response for incision geometry.
- Collateral (vertical/horizontal) thermal-damage-zone width depended on neither measured power (0.5–4.5 W), nor operating mode (CW vs. either pulsed setting), nor fiber tip diameter — the authors' own explicit conclusion.
- At every matched power level, CW and pulsed damage values showed no consistent direction of difference — a third, distinct CW-vs-PW pattern in this vault's evidence base (neither "PW < CW" nor "PW > CW", but no detectable mode effect at all).
- No bone charring was visible at any tested power or mode.
- This is the first source in this vault's biopsy-adjacent literature to report both a genuinely measured (not set/console) output power and a numeric incision speed.

### What this paper supports

- That incision depth scales with measured power in an ex vivo oral soft-tissue model at 810 nm.
- That, in this specific ex vivo design, collateral thermal-damage width did not vary with measured power, mode, or fiber tip diameter — a real, source-stated finding usable as one data point in the power-vs-thermal-damage question.
- Feasibility and precedent for directly measuring fiber-outflow power (rather than relying on console/set value) in an incision study.

### What this paper does NOT support

- It does NOT establish a set-power value at all — the console/panel display is explicitly not reported, so this paper cannot be used as set-vs-measured deviation data (there is no "set" side to compare against).
- It is NOT a biopsy or diagnostic-outcome study — no lesion is excised and no histopathologic diagnosis is rendered; thermal damage here is a geometric measurement, not a specimen-interpretability or diagnostic-yield outcome.
- Its "no mode/power effect on collateral damage" finding is specific to this ex vivo pig-mandible design at 810 nm and should not be generalized as disproving power/mode effects seen in other sources (e.g. Gundlapalle et al. 2022, Romanos et al. 2022) — the vault preserves this as a genuine three-way conflict, not a resolution.
- Tip-initiation status and contact/non-contact mode are not reported, so this paper cannot support claims about either variable.

### Key limitations

- Ex vivo pig mandible mucosa, not human tissue and not a lesion.
- No diagnostic outcome, margin quality, or specimen-interpretability measure of any kind.
- Wattmeter calibration protocol is not described.

### Relevance to current project

Contributes to the **actual delivered power → incision speed/movement → tissue interaction** links: it is the earliest source in this vault to combine genuinely measured (not console) power with a controlled numeric incision speed and a histologically measured tissue-interaction outcome. It does not reach the **histopathologic specimen quality** end of the chain (no diagnostic or specimen-interpretability outcome).

### Role in the project

- Core prior art
- Delivered-power evidence
- Speed / movement evidence

### One-line takeaway

The earliest and one of the most rigorous ex vivo precedents in this vault for genuinely measured (not set) diode power combined with controlled incision speed, but it cannot supply set-vs-measured deviation data (no panel value reported) or any biopsy/diagnostic outcome.

### สรุปภาษาไทย

งานวิจัยนี้เป็นการศึกษาทดลองแบบ ex vivo บนเหงือกหมู (ไม่มีรอยโรค ไม่มีการวินิจฉัย) ใช้เลเซอร์ไดโอด 810 nm ทำแผลตัดมาตรฐาน 198 รอย ครอบคลุม 33 ชุดพารามิเตอร์ (กำลังที่วัดได้จริง 7 ระดับ × 3 โหมด × ปลายไฟเบอร์ 2 ขนาด) โดยควบคุมความเร็วตัดคงที่ 10 มม./วินาที จุดเด่นคือกำลังที่รายงานเป็นค่าที่**วัดได้จริง**จากปลายไฟเบอร์ ไม่ใช่ค่าที่ตั้งบนหน้าจอเครื่อง (ไม่มีการรายงานค่าตั้งเลย)

ผลสำคัญคือ ความลึกแผลตัดสัมพันธ์กับกำลังที่วัดได้จริง แต่ความเสียหายจากความร้อนบริเวณรอบแผล**ไม่ขึ้นกับ**กำลัง โหมด (CW/PW) หรือขนาดปลายไฟเบอร์เลย ซึ่งเป็นรูปแบบที่แตกต่างจากแหล่งข้อมูลอื่นในคลังนี้ (ไม่ใช่ทั้ง "PW เสียหายน้อยกว่า" หรือ "PW เสียหายมากกว่า")

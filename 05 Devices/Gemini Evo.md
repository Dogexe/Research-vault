# Gemini Evo

## Manufacturer

- Ultradent Products, South Jordan, USA — FACT ([[07 Data/Mazzoni et al 2026 - Neonatal Frenulum Laser Parameters]])

## Wavelength

- Dual: 810 nm and 980 nm — FACT

## Reported preset / set power

- 810 nm at 1.2 W; 980 nm at 1.4 W; a separate "final power" reported as 1.1 W — FACT (all three values retained as reported by the source; not reconciled into a single figure by this vault)
- These are the values used in the single clinical study cataloguing this device ([[07 Data/Mazzoni et al 2026 - Neonatal Frenulum Laser Parameters]]) — kept distinct from the manufacturer's own factory preset table below, which was not the source of these study values.

## Manufacturer manual / IFU (independently verified 2026-09-16)

Source: "USER MANUAL," Gemini EVO™ 810+980 Soft Tissue Laser, Ultradent Products Inc., Doc. 1010000AR02, dated 022322 (Feb 2022). Full extraction method and access notes: [[90 Agent/Search Reports/Preset Reliance and Technical Misuse/Search Log - Gemini EVO and SOL Manual Verification 2026-09-16]]. Full findings and quotes: [[06 Synthesis/Preset Reliance and Technical Misuse in Dental Laser Use]], "Follow-up (2026-09-16)."

- FACT: 16 named factory pre-programmed procedures across three categories (Non-Surgical, Surgical, Pain Relief). Power values for 8 of the 16 extracted cleanly (e.g. Incision/Excision: 1.1–1.5 W depending on wavelength mode); the other 8 (Hemostasis, Debridement, Gingivectomy, Frenectomy, Pulpotomy, Troughing, and two others) are present in the manual's table but did not survive this pass's `pdftotext` column extraction — `NEEDS VERIFICATION`, not a manual omission.
- FACT: Presets are editable/customizable by the user (hold PRESET 3s to save, 5s to factory-reset), and via a companion Dashboard app.
- FACT: The manual states directly, twice, that presets are "only a general recommendation from the manufacturer" / "simply a manufacturer's recommendation" — not described as a validated clinical or biological dose.
- FACT: Power accuracy/tolerance: ± 20% at all wavelength modes (2.0 W spec point). Re-calibration recommended every 12 months, performed by returning the unit to the manufacturer/distributor.
- FACT: No clinician-facing external power-meter verification protocol is described; the manual states "No separate equipment is recommended" for this purpose.
- FACT: Movement speed is acknowledged qualitatively ("regulating the power and the speed that the operator moves the fiber optic tip") but never quantified — no mm/s or dwell-time value given.
- FACT: No published study, trial, or dataset is cited as preset-validation evidence anywhere in the manual.
- FACT: 5 mm fiber tips are supplied pre-initiated from the factory; 7 mm tips are supplied un-initiated (non-contact/non-surgical use). No fiber core diameter (μm) is stated.

## Biopsy / tissue outcomes

- Not a biopsy/histology study. Neonatal ankyloglossia (frenulum) laser frenectomy case series.
- 32 of 35 patients (91.4%) did not require reoperation; 3 of 35 (8.6%) required a new procedure — FACT
- Same laser parameters used across all surgeries — FACT

## Known limitations

- Single clinical case series; no independent power measurement.
- No histologic or diagnostic-yield data (surgical reoperation rate only).
- Set-power values (810 nm, 980 nm, "final power") are not reconciled by the source; relationship between the three figures is UNKNOWN.
- Manufacturer manual (verified 2026-09-16, see above) confirms presets are a general, uncited manufacturer recommendation, not a validated biological dose; ± 20% tolerance is the manufacturer's own stated acceptance band, not by itself evidence of unreliability; no clinician-facing power-meter check protocol exists for this device (contrast with [[05 Devices/SOL]] and [[05 Devices/A.R.C. Laser FOX (810 nm)|A.R.C. Laser FOX]]).

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- Gemini Evo เป็นเลเซอร์ไดโอดกำลังสูงสองความยาวคลื่น (810/980 nm) จาก Ultradent Products สหรัฐอเมริกา
- ข้อมูลในคลังนี้มาจากการศึกษาผ่าตัดพังผืดใต้ลิ้นในทารกแรกเกิด (frenectomy) เพียงงานเดียว ไม่ใช่การศึกษาชิ้นเนื้อ
- อัตราการผ่าตัดซ้ำอยู่ที่ 8.6% (3 ใน 35 ราย) ไม่มีข้อมูลกำลังขาออกที่วัดได้จริงหรือการสูญเสียกำลัง
- คู่มือผู้ผลิต (ตรวจสอบแล้ว 2026-09-16): เครื่องนี้มีพรีเซ็ต 16 หัตถการ แก้ไขได้ ค่าความคลาดเคลื่อน ±20% คู่มือระบุว่าเป็นเพียงคำแนะนำทั่วไปจากผู้ผลิต ไม่ใช่มาตรฐานทางคลินิกที่ผ่านการตรวจสอบ และไม่มีขั้นตอนตรวจสอบกำลังไฟด้วยเพาเวอร์มิเตอร์ภายนอกสำหรับทันตแพทย์

# Wiser 3

## Manufacturer

- Doctor Smile, Brendola (VI), Italy — FACT ([[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data]])

## Wavelength

- Dual-wavelength, combined in one handpiece/emission: 450 nm and 808 nm — FACT

## Reported preset / set power

- 2, 3, 4 W (three levels, each tested in both CW and PW) — FACT (study-selected settings, described in the source as "adjusted output power," not confirmed as manufacturer-recommended presets)

## Operating modes

- Continuous wave (CW) and pulsed wave (PW), each at all three power levels (six groups total) — FACT
- Frequency: 10 kHz — FACT
- Pulse duration: UNKNOWN (study-specific value not reported; device class supports 0.1 ms–infinity per background text only)

## Fiber / tip

- 300 μm fiber — FACT
- Initiated surgical tip, tip length 5 mm, checked during excision for carbonized build-up — FACT
- Contact mode — FACT

## Measured output

- UNKNOWN — described as "adjusted output power of the laser device" (console/set value); no independent power-meter verification reported.

## Power loss / deviation

- UNKNOWN / not applicable

## Calibration / measurement method

- No laser power-meter calibration protocol reported.
- Histology: epithelial and connective-tissue thermal damage measured (mm) on H&E sections, 2.5× magnification, LAS 4.8 software; pathologist blinded to laser parameters.

## Biopsy / tissue outcomes

- Not a diagnostic-yield study (no diagnosis rendered). Ex vivo porcine lingual mucosa excisions, 6 groups of 6 samples (2/3/4 W × CW/PW).
- Epithelial damage range: 0.62 mm (2 W CW, minimum) to 3.12 mm (4 W PW, maximum) — FACT
- Connective tissue damage range: 0.53 mm (4 W CW, minimum) to 3.19 mm (4 W PW, maximum) — FACT
- Pooled CW vs. PW: not statistically significant for epithelial (p=0.234) or connective tissue (p=0.444) damage — FACT
- Pooled by power level: not statistically significant for epithelial (p=0.765) or connective tissue (p=0.652) damage — FACT
- Source's own Conclusions recommend 2 W CW (least average thermal damage of the six groups) and a minimum 2 mm perilesional margin for excisional biopsies of lesions suspected of malignancy — FACT
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: source's own narrative text reports the 2 W CW vs. 2 W PW epithelial-damage comparison as "t=1.94, p<0.01," but the source's own Table 2 lists p=0.084 for the identical comparison. Both figures are preserved as reported; not reconciled.

## Studies using this device

- [[07 Data/Pergolini et al 2025 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data]] (only source in this vault)

## Known limitations

- Ex vivo porcine tissue only; no diagnostic outcome measured.
- Set/console power only; no independent power-meter verification or calibration protocol reported.
- Internal source inconsistency in one statistical comparison (see above), not resolved by this vault.
- Recommended margin (≥2 mm) is smaller than the ≥5 mm figures reported for other devices in this vault (Angiero et al., Gambino et al., Gundlapalle et al.) — a genuine cross-source difference, not reconciled.

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- Wiser 3 เป็นเลเซอร์ไดโอดสองความยาวคลื่น (450/808 nm) จาก Doctor Smile อิตาลี ข้อมูลมาจากงานวิจัยเดียว (Pergolini et al. 2025)
- เป็นการศึกษา ex vivo ที่ไม่มีผลการวินิจฉัยทางพยาธิวิทยา แต่วัดความเสียหายทางความร้อนที่กำลังไฟและโหมดต่างกัน 6 กลุ่ม ไม่พบความแตกต่างที่มีนัยสำคัญทางสถิติทั้งจากกำลังไฟหรือโหมดเมื่อรวมข้อมูล
- พบความไม่สอดคล้องภายในแหล่งข้อมูลเองเรื่องค่า p ของการเปรียบเทียบหนึ่งคู่ (ข้อความระบุ p<0.01 แต่ตารางระบุ p=0.084) ซึ่งยังไม่ได้รับการยืนยัน

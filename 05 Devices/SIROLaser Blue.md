# SIROLaser Blue

## Manufacturer

- Dentsply Sirona, Charlotte, NC, USA — FACT ([[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]], [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]])

## Wavelength

- Dual: 445 nm and 970 nm — FACT
- Romanos et al. 2022: 445 nm (±5 nm); 970 nm (−10/+15 nm) — FACT
- Xue et al. 2022: 445 nm; 970 nm — FACT

## Reported preset / set power

- Xue et al. 2022: 445 nm set to 3 W; 970 nm set to 2 W — FACT
- Romanos et al. 2022: 2 W (both wavelengths, CW); pulsed ("chopped") mode mean power 1 W — FACT
- These are study-selected settings in each source, not confirmed as a single manufacturer-recommended preset.

## Operating modes

- Continuous wave (CW) and pulsed — FACT
- Romanos: pulse duration 10 μs–0.99 s (chopped-pulse mode), frequency 35 Hz, duty cycle 50% — FACT
- Xue: pulse duration 50 ms (both 445 nm and 970 nm), frequency 10 Hz, duty cycle 50% — FACT

## Fiber / tip

- 320 μm fiber (both studies) — FACT
- Xue: fibre tip uninitiated and new — FACT
- Romanos: disposable fiberoptic tip "EasyTip 320"; both initiated and non-initiated tips tested within-study (initiated via dark blue articulating paper, Accufilm, 5 sec) — FACT
- Contact mode (Romanos: fiber inserted parallel to tooth axis, 3–4 mm depth) — FACT

## Measured output

- Xue et al. 2022, CW: 445 nm 3.5–3.7 W (mean 3.6 W); 970 nm 2.4–2.5 W (mean 2.5 W) — FACT
- Xue et al. 2022, pulsed: 445 nm 4.0–4.1 W (mean 4.1 W); 970 nm 2.5 W (mean 2.5 W) — FACT
- Romanos et al. 2022: UNKNOWN — no independent power-meter verification reported; 2 W is stated as the panel setting only.

## Power loss / deviation

- Xue et al. 2022 (mean measured output vs. preset): CW 445 nm +21%; CW 970 nm +22%; pulsed 445 nm +37%; pulsed 970 nm +26% — measured output was **above** set power in every tested condition — FACT
- Romanos et al. 2022: UNKNOWN — no power measurement performed (histologic penetration-depth outcome only).

## Calibration / measurement method

- Xue et al. 2022: PowerMax Pro 150F HD-50 mW-150 W fan-cooled sensor and LabMax-Pro SSIM Laser Power Meter, fibre perpendicular to sensor; power-meter calibration performed by manufacturer before the experiment, with temperature compensation and wavelength correction applied per measurement — FACT
- Romanos et al. 2022: not a power measurement; histology used a calibrated ocular micrometer (protocol not further detailed).

## Biopsy / tissue outcomes

- Not a diagnostic-biopsy study in either source. Romanos et al. 2022 measured histologic penetration depth (thermal/collagen denaturation, EVG-stained bovine gingiva) as a tissue-effect proxy.
- 445 nm showed significantly greater penetration depth than 970 nm across matched conditions (CW initiated, CW non-initiated) — FACT
- Initiated tips showed **greater** penetration depth than non-initiated tips wherever a significant difference was found (445 nm CW: 0.35 mm initiated vs. 0.22 mm non-initiated; 970 nm CW: 0.20 mm vs. 0.131 mm; 970 nm Pulsed: 0.10 mm vs. 0.01 mm) — FACT, though the source's own Abstract states the opposite general direction; this internal inconsistency is preserved and flagged NEEDS VERIFICATION in the source data note.
- Pulsed mode showed lower penetration depth than CW at the same nominal 2 W setting, for both wavelengths — FACT

## Studies using this device

- [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] (measured-output power accuracy)
- [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] (histologic penetration depth, initiated vs. non-initiated tip)
- These two studies cannot be cross-validated against each other for this device: Romanos et al. reports no measured-output value, so the power-deviation finding from Xue et al. cannot be checked against Romanos et al.'s tissue-effect data.

## Known limitations

- No single source combines measured-output accuracy and histologic/diagnostic outcome for this device.
- Romanos et al.'s own Abstract and its own Table 1/Discussion disagree on the direction of the initiated-vs-non-initiated effect (see [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] for the full flagged discrepancy).
- Neither source reports a diagnostic-yield or biopsy outcome for this device.

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- SIROLaser Blue (Dentsply Sirona) เป็นเลเซอร์ไดโอดสองความยาวคลื่น (445/970 nm) ที่ปรากฏใน 2 งานวิจัยของคลังนี้
- Xue et al. 2022 วัดกำลังขาออกจริงและพบว่าสูงกว่าค่าตั้งเสมอ (+21% ถึง +37% ขึ้นกับความยาวคลื่นและโหมด)
- Romanos et al. 2022 วัดความลึกการซึมผ่านความร้อนในเนื้อเยื่อ ไม่ได้วัดกำลังขาออกจริง จึงไม่สามารถเชื่อมโยงผลทั้งสองงานเข้าด้วยกันได้โดยตรง และพบข้อขัดแย้งภายในงานวิจัยเองเรื่องทิศทางผลของปลายไฟเบอร์แบบ initiated

# Quicklase 12W Dual 4

## Manufacturer

- Quicklase, England, UK — FACT ([[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]])

## Wavelength

- Dual-wavelength, simultaneous emission: 810 nm (50%) + 980 nm (50%) — FACT

## Reported preset / set power

- 1.5 W and 2.5 W, continuous wave (CW) — FACT (study-selected settings, not confirmed as manufacturer-recommended presets)

## Operating modes

- Continuous wave (CW) only tested in the source study — FACT
- Device's full mode range (e.g. pulsed capability): UNKNOWN

## Fiber / tip

- 400 μm FC single-file multimode fiber — FACT
- Tip initiated before each power output: laser set to 0.5 W, fiber applied to articulating paper, activated until perforation and flame/char formed — FACT
- Contact mode — FACT

## Measured output

- UNKNOWN — a power meter (PINTUDY, Guangzhou, China) was used only to adjust/set the diode power via the fiber cut, not to independently re-verify output after setup. No independent measured-output value is reported.

## Power loss / deviation

- UNKNOWN — not reported; this study measured peri-incisional temperature rise, not power-output accuracy.

## Calibration / measurement method

- Power meter brand named (PINTUDY); no calibration protocol, certificate, or traceability described.

## Biopsy / tissue outcomes

- Not a biopsy/diagnostic-yield study. Ex vivo sheep-tongue incision study measuring peri-incisional temperature rise (thermocouple).
- Diode overall mean temperature rise: 8.06 ± 2.58 °C, significantly greater than the Er,Cr:YSGG comparator (Waterlase iPlus, Biolase): 3.87 ± 1.16 °C (P < 0.001) — FACT
- The 2.5 W diode group showed the highest temperature rise of all four tested groups (10.06 ± 2.02 °C) — FACT

## Studies using this device

- [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] (only source in this vault)

## Known limitations

- Single ex vivo sheep-tongue model; findings not confirmed in vivo or in human tissue.
- No independent measured-output verification; power values are console-set only.
- No histologic or diagnostic-yield outcome measured (temperature only).

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- Quicklase 12W Dual 4 เป็นเลเซอร์ไดโอดสองความยาวคลื่น (810 nm + 980 nm พร้อมกัน) จาก Quicklase สหราชอาณาจักร
- ข้อมูลในคลังนี้มาจากการศึกษาเดียว (Al-Ani et al. 2023) ซึ่งวัด "การเพิ่มขึ้นของอุณหภูมิ" รอบรอยตัดในลิ้นแกะ ไม่ใช่การวัดกำลังขาออกจริงหรือผลการตรวจชิ้นเนื้อ
- ไม่มีข้อมูลการวัดกำลังขาออกอิสระ (independent measured output) หรือการสูญเสียกำลัง (power loss) สำหรับอุปกรณ์นี้

- **Unresolved discrepancy (flagged 2026-09-16, not reconciled):** the manufacturer's own current IFU (Den-Mat Holdings, LLC, Doc. 823059600 Rev 8, 10/23, "SOL Portable Diode Laser") identifies the manufacturer as **Den-Mat Holdings, LLC, Lompoc, California, USA** — not "DenMat Italia, Italy" as stated in Romeo et al. 2014. ## Manufacturer manual / IFU (independently verified 2026-09-16)

Source (primary): "INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE — SOL® PORTABLE DIODE LASER," Den-Mat Holdings, LLC, Doc. 823059600, Rev 8, 10/23 (Oct 2023). An older copy of the same document (Rev 6, 01/20, cover reads "SOL Laser") was also located but not diffed against the 2023 text. Full extraction method and access notes: [[90 Agent/Search Reports/Preset Reliance and Technical Misuse/Search Log - Gemini EVO and SOL Manual Verification 2026-09-16]]. Full findings and quotes: [[06 Synthesis/Preset Reliance and Technical Misuse in Dental Laser Use]], "Follow-up (2026-09-16)."

- FACT: Wavelength 808 nm ± 5 nm, confirmed directly from the manual — matches the Wavelength section above.
- FACT: Four named "Smart Presets" with factory-default values: **Cut** 1.2 W CW · **Debride** 0.4 W CW · **Perio** 1.5 W Pulse · **Custom** 1.5 W CW. A separate factory-default Standby-mode setting (0.8 W CW) also exists. None of these four matches the 2 W CW value used in Romeo et al. 2014 above — kept as separate, unreconciled data points (study-selected value vs. manufacturer factory-default table).
- FACT: All four presets are editable/programmable (hold the preset key 3s to save a new value).
- FACT: Power accuracy/tolerance is ± 20% (internal auto-shutoff if exceeded).
- FACT: A clinician-executable external power-meter verification protocol is explicitly described: check output at 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, and 3.0 W; acceptance is within 20% of the meter reading; out-of-tolerance requires factory recalibration. This is the fullest clinician-facing verification protocol found among manuals reviewed in this vault to date (see [[06 Synthesis/Preset Reliance and Technical Misuse in Dental Laser Use]]).
- FACT: Re-calibration recommended at least once per year; user/service-contractor recalibration is explicitly barred by the manual.
- FACT: Fiber tips are 400 μm standard diameter, single-use, disposable; a 9 mm "Perio" tip variant also exists.
- FACT: Movement speed is acknowledged qualitatively ("moving too slowly" causes charring) but never quantified — no mm/s or dwell-time value given.
- FACT: No published study, trial, or dataset is cited as preset-validation evidence anywhere in the manual. The manual's general provenance statement is worded identically to Gemini EVO's — see the synthesis note for the INTERPRETATION that this may be shared IFU boilerplate rather than independent claims.

## Biopsy / tissue outcomes

- Real human biopsies, 17 patients (diode + KTP comparator combined series); lesion size 0.5–1 cm diameter — FACT
- Diagnostic yield: 17/17 (100%, diode + KTP pooled); complete wound recovery at 7 and 21 days, no complications — FACT
- Diode-only margin thermal-damage by lesion type (vault-computed means from per-patient source data, flagged INTERPRETATION where noted):
  - Mucocele (n=2): individual values 0.442 mm, 0.102 mm (mean 0.272 mm, INTERPRETATION)
  - Fibroma (n=5, diode-only in source design): 0.382 ± 0.149 mm — FACT
  - Hyperkeratosis/squamous hyperplasia (n=3): individual values 0.319, 0.446, 0.383 mm (mean 0.383 mm, INTERPRETATION)
  - Oral lichen planus (n=3, diode-only): 0.473 ± 0.105 mm — FACT
  - Giant cell granuloma (n=1): 0.182 mm — FACT
  - Melanotic macula (n=1, 808 nm explicitly stated): 0.149 mm — FACT

## Known limitations

- Small per-lesion-type samples (n=1–5).
- Diagnostic-yield figure (100%) is pooled across diode and KTP comparator cases, not diode-only.
- No independent power-meter verification; no calibration method reported (for the Romeo et al. 2014 study specifically — see manufacturer manual note above).
- Some diode-only means are this vault's own arithmetic from per-patient source values (flagged INTERPRETATION above), not values the source itself reports as a diode-only subset mean.
- Manufacturer identity is unresolved: "DenMat Italia, Italy" (per Romeo et al. 2014) vs. "Den-Mat Holdings, LLC, USA" (per the manufacturer's own 2023 IFU) — see "Manufacturer" section above, not reconciled.
- Manufacturer manual (verified 2026-09-16) confirms presets are a general, uncited manufacturer recommendation, not a validated biological dose; ± 20% tolerance is the manufacturer's own stated acceptance band, not by itself evidence of unreliability.

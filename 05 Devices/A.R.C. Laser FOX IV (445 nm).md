# A.R.C. Laser FOX IV (445 nm)

## Manufacturer

- A.R.C. Laser GmbH, Nuremberg, Germany — FACT ([[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]])

## Wavelength

- 445 nm — FACT

## Reported preset / set power

- 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 W (0.5 W increments), continuous wave — FACT (study-tested settings, not confirmed as manufacturer-recommended presets)

## Operating modes

- Continuous wave (CW) only tested — FACT

## Fiber / tip

- Bare fiber, 280 μm core / 300 μm diameter — FACT
- Non-initiated (deliberately "non-initialized"), fixed in a curved-tip handpiece attachment, 30° inclination, 3 mm fiber projection, cleaned before each incision — FACT

## Measured output

- UNKNOWN (numeric value not published) — the source states output power was measured at the fiber tip with a power meter before each incision as a quality-control step, but reports no numeric measured-output value.

## Power loss / deviation

- UNKNOWN — measurement was performed but not published numerically; no set-vs-measured deviation figure is available.

## Calibration / measurement method

- Power meter used to standardize fiber-tip output before each incision; power meter brand/model and calibration protocol UNKNOWN.

## Biopsy / tissue outcomes

- Not a diagnostic-biopsy study. Ex vivo porcine mandible (periodontal pocket + oral mucosa) histological tissue-effect study (area of tissue loss, thermal damage area, necrosis area) across six wavelengths/systems and four CW power settings.
- Qualitative cross-wavelength finding (source's own): 810 nm and 1064 nm (FOX-branded systems) showed the *least* thermal damage/necrosis of the six systems tested; 1470 nm (WOLF) showed the *greatest*. No FOX IV (445 nm)-specific numeric ranking beyond this pooled six-system comparison is reported.

## Studies using this device

- [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]] (only source in this vault)

## Known limitations

- Single ex vivo porcine study; no diagnostic/biopsy outcome measured.
- Fiber-tip power measurement was performed but never published as a numeric value, so no measured-output or power-loss figure exists for this device.
- Tissue-effect finding is a pooled, qualitative ranking across six systems, not a device-specific numeric result.

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- FOX IV (445 nm) เป็นเลเซอร์ไดโอดจาก A.R.C. Laser GmbH ประเทศเยอรมนี ข้อมูลในคลังนี้มาจากการศึกษาเดียว (Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. 2020)
- มีการวัดกำลังขาออกที่ปลายไฟเบอร์ก่อนตัดเนื้อเยื่อทุกครั้ง แต่ผู้วิจัยไม่ได้รายงานค่าตัวเลขที่วัดได้ จึงไม่มีข้อมูลการสูญเสียกำลังเชิงตัวเลขสำหรับอุปกรณ์นี้

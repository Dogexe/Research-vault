# Epic 10 TM

## Manufacturer

- Biolase, CA, USA — FACT ([[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]])

## Wavelength

- 940 nm — FACT

## Reported preset / set power

- 1.0, 2.0, 3.0 W ("Output Power," panel setting) — FACT

## Operating modes

- Continuous wave (CW) only — FACT

## Fiber / tip

- 400 μm circular-shaped fiber tip (same physical tip used across all three devices tested in this study) — FACT
- Tip initiation status: UNKNOWN
- Contact mode; fiber tip unmoved, in contact vertically with the test surface — FACT

## Measured output

- 0.7628 W (at 1.0 W set); 1.4548 W (at 2.0 W set); 2.2612 W (at 3.0 W set) — FACT, measured with a calibrated power meter (NovaII, Ophir Photonics)

## Power loss / deviation

- −23.7%, −27.3%, −24.6% **below** set power (at 1.0/2.0/3.0 W respectively) — FACT (INTERPRETATION: arithmetic derived from the source's own set/measured values; the source itself does not label this a percentage or call it "power loss")
- This device measured consistently below its panel setting, the same direction as the Saeshin diode laser in the same study but opposite to the K2 mobile.

## Calibration / measurement method

- Calibrated power meter (NovaII, Ophir Photonics, North Logan, UT, USA) — FACT
- No further calibration protocol, certificate, or traceability detail described beyond "calibrated."
- Exposure duration: 15 sec, contact mode, on SLA titanium discs (Dentis, Daegu, Korea).

## Biopsy / tissue outcomes

- UNKNOWN — not a biopsy or tissue-effect study. This source examines titanium dental-implant surface roughness/chemical composition after irradiation, not soft-tissue biopsy outcomes.

## Studies using this device

- [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]] (only source in this vault)

## Known limitations

- Single study; bench/materials-science context (titanium discs), not clinical soft-tissue use.
- Calibration protocol detail beyond "calibrated" is not reported.
- A different Biolase 940 nm device (model UNKNOWN) appears in [[07 Data/Fahlstedt et al 2021 - Laser Operating Parameters]]; this vault does **not** treat that device as the same model as Epic 10 TM, since Fahlstedt et al. does not report a model name.

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- Epic 10 TM (940 nm) จาก Biolase สหรัฐอเมริกา มีข้อมูลจากงานวิจัยเดียว (Kim et al. 2020)
- อุปกรณ์นี้วัดกำลังขาออกได้ "ต่ำกว่า" ค่าที่ตั้งไว้เสมอ (−23.7% ถึง −27.3%)
- เป็นการศึกษาบนแผ่นไทเทเนียม ไม่ใช่เนื้อเยื่ออ่อน จึงไม่มีข้อมูลผลชิ้นเนื้อหรือการวินิจฉัย ไม่ควรสับสนกับเลเซอร์ Biolase 940 nm อีกเครื่องในงานของ Fahlstedt et al. 2021 ซึ่งไม่ทราบรุ่น

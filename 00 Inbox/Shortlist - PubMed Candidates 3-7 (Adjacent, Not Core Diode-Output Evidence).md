# Shortlist — PubMed candidates #3–#7 (adjacent evidence, not core diode-output evidence)

Status: unprocessed. Saved for later review only. Do not treat as core diode-output evidence per user instruction 2026-09-12. No literature notes created yet.

## 3. The dark art of light curing in dentistry

- Guarneri JAG, Price RB, Maucoski C, Arrais CAG. *J Dent*, 2024.
- PMID: 39332516
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jdent.2024.105375
- Why flagged: calibrated spectroradiometer/integrating-sphere measurement of 12 dental light-curing units; found 50% deviated >10% from manufacturer-stated output.
- Caveat: different device class (LED/laser light-curing units, not soft-tissue diode lasers). Adjacent methodological analog for preset-vs-measured discrepancy, not direct diode-laser evidence.

## 4. Evaluation of hollow fiberoptic tips for the conduction of Er:YAG laser

- Alves PRV, Aranha N, Alfredo E, Marchesan MA, Brugnera Junior A, Sousa-Neto MD. *Photomed Laser Surg*, 2005.
- PMID: 16144486
- DOI: 10.1089/pho.2005.23.410
- Why flagged: quantifies transmission loss/attenuation across different fiber-tip geometries.
- Caveat: different laser type (Er:YAG, not diode). Relevant methodology only, not diode-specific.

## 5. Evaluation of cutting efficiency and thermal damage during soft tissue surgery with 940 nm-diode laser: an ex vivo study

- Strakas D, Dionysopoulos D, Tolidis K, Meister J. *Lasers Surg Med*, 2023.
- PMID: 36740362
- DOI: 10.1002/lsm.23639
- Why flagged: systematic evaluation of adjusted output power (1-6 W, CW, initiated tip) against cut depth and thermal damage.
- Caveat: assumes panel setting = delivered power; no independent power-meter verification reported in abstract.

## 6. Quantitative determination of cut efficiency during soft tissue surgery using diode lasers in the wavelength range between 400 and 1500 nm

- Hanke A, Fimmers R, Frentzen M, Meister J. *Lasers Med Sci*, 2021.
- PMID: 33496905
- DOI: 10.1007/s10103-020-03243-4
- Why flagged: eight diode wavelengths, CW mode, 0.5-4 W, contact/non-contact; output power does not correlate linearly with tissue effect.
- Caveat: no independent power verification reported in abstract; panel setting used as-is.

## 7. Comparison of Pulsed and Continuous Wave Diode Laser at 940 nm on the Viability and Migration of Gingival Fibroblasts

- Jazaeri M, Torkzaban P, Afshar S, Najafi-Vosough R, Arany P, Gholami L. *Photochem Photobiol*, 2022.
- PMID: 36086909
- DOI: 10.1111/php.13711
- Why flagged: directly compares CW vs. two pulsed duty-cycle settings at matched average power/fluence on gingival tissue.
- Caveat: relies on nominal/average power, not independently measured output.

## Not shortlisted (excluded from this list)

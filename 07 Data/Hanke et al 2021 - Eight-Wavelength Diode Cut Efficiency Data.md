## Notes

- FACT: This is a controlled, mechanized ex vivo cut-efficiency study, not a biopsy or diagnostic study. Outcomes are histometric: cut depth, cut width, total interaction zone (TIZ) depth/width, and a newly defined "efficiency factor" γz = cutting depth (d_cut) / total interaction zone depth (TIZ_depth). Results: maximum cut depth 820 μm at 445 nm and 344 μm at 810 nm (both >2 W); cut width averaged 125 ± 24 μm across all wavelengths and power settings (all class-1 incisions were narrower than the fiber core diameter); γz showed complex, statistically significant (p < 0.0001) wavelength- and power-dependence, and did **not** increase linearly or uniformly with output power — a central finding the authors emphasize as clinically important ("power increases do not correlate to the same extent with the incision depth nor with thermal damage").
- FACT: No diagnostic outcome, specimen margin, or histopathologic diagnosis is reported anywhere in this paper — it is an ex vivo tissue-block cutting-behavior characterization study, not a biopsy model. There is no patient, no lesion, and no diagnosis rendered.
- FACT: The efficiency factor γz and its 0.6 "clinical orientation" threshold, later adopted and cited by Strakas et al. 2023 (same senior author, J. Meister), originates in this paper.
- INTERPRETATION: This study's power-verification protocol (calibrated power meter, checked before every single incision, ≥5% deviation triggering fiber-tip replacement) is methodologically stronger than the "single setup check" pattern already documented in this vault for [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters|Al-Ani et al. 2023]] and [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters|Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. 2020]]. It is fair to classify this as "independently measured power" in the sense of a real, calibrated, per-incision QC verification — but it falls short of a design that *reports and tabulates* the actual delivered wattage as a distinct data value alongside the nominal set value. This distinction should not be collapsed when comparing across papers.
- HYPOTHESIS: UNKNOWN — the study's own null hypothesis (that power increases would produce a continuous, proportional rise in cut efficiency, incision depth, and thermal damage at all wavelengths) was explicitly rejected by the reported results.
- Classification for this vault: CORE background/methodology source for the power-verification and incision-speed axes of the novelty chain; explicitly **not** a biopsy or diagnostic-outcome source.

- ความเร็วตัดถูกควบคุมคงที่ที่ 2 มม./วินาที ด้วยเครื่อง micropositioner แบบ 3 มิติ
- ไม่มีผลการวินิจฉัยทางพยาธิวิทยาหรือขอบชิ้นเนื้อ biopsy ใด ๆ ในบทความนี้

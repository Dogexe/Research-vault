# Laser Operating Parameters

Record values exactly as reported. Use `UNKNOWN` when a value is not reported; do not infer missing parameters.

**Vault classification: SUPPORTING dental evidence (n=1 case report).** A real human diode-laser excisional-biopsy pathway with full device parameters and a genuine diagnostic outcome, but a single case — too weak on its own to join this vault's CORE biopsy evidence tier (Angiero et al., Romeo et al., Gambino et al., Palaia et al.), which all use prospective series of n≥17. **This source is by the same author group as, but is a separate paper from, [[07 Data/Capodiferro et al 2008 - Oral Laser Surgical Pathology Abstract-Level Data|Capodiferro et al. 2008]] and must not be treated as a substitute for it.** The 2008 paper (n=25, 2–7 W, abstract-level only) remains the vault's only identified candidate for a coagulation-zone finding; this 2009 case report does not mention coagulation at all and does not close that gap.

## Notes

- FACT: This is a real, if single-case, human diode-laser excisional-biopsy pathway with a fully reported wavelength (880 nm), set power (2.5 W CW), and fiber diameter (300 μm) — more completely parameterized than the abstract-level [[07 Data/Capodiferro et al 2008 - Oral Laser Surgical Pathology Abstract-Level Data|Capodiferro et al. 2008]], but from a single patient, so it cannot be pooled with or used to fill parameter gaps in the 2008 paper (different patients, different power range: 2008 used 2–7 W across 25 patients; this case used one fixed 2.5 W setting).
- FACT: This is the only source in this vault's biopsy-adjacent literature reporting the complete *absence* of measurable thermal alteration at a diode-cut margin, confirmed by two independent microscopy methods (conventional H&E and confocal autofluorescence) in both central and peripheral specimen regions. Every other CORE biopsy source in this vault (Angiero et al. 2011, Romeo et al. 2014, Gambino et al. 2026, Palaia et al. 2021) reports a measurable, non-zero thermal-damage width (roughly 150–630 μm). This is a genuine single-case outlier, not a contradiction to be resolved — it may reflect the lower power (2.5 W, at the low end of this vault's evidence-supported CW range), the specific 880 nm wavelength, a benign well-encapsulated lesion type (lipoma) different from the more commonly biopsied lesion types in the CORE series, measurement-method sensitivity differences, or simple case-to-case variability — none of which can be distinguished from a single case.
- FACT: The internal inconsistency between the Fig. 1 caption's stated lesion size (0.7 mm) and the main text's stated size (approximately 1 cm) is preserved verbatim per this vault's rule against reconciling source-internal discrepancies; NEEDS VERIFICATION.
- INTERPRETATION: Because this is n=1, it cannot be used to argue that 2.5 W CW at 880 nm generally produces no thermal artifact — it is one data point, at the weakest evidence tier this vault recognizes for a real clinical outcome (case report vs. prospective series). It is recorded here as a genuine finding, not smoothed into the CORE-tier thermal-damage-width evidence in [[04 Evidence/Thermal Artifact]] or [[04 Evidence/Surgical Margins]].
- HYPOTHESIS: UNKNOWN — no formal hypothesis stated beyond the case report's own descriptive aim.
- Limitations (source-stated and vault-noted): single case (n=1), no comparator arm, no independent power-meter verification, no incision-speed report, no tip-initiation or contact-mode report, an unreconciled internal lesion-size discrepancy, and a benign lesion type (lipoma) that is not directly comparable to the mixed lesion-type series in this vault's CORE biopsy sources.
- Classification for this vault: SUPPORTING dental evidence (n=1 case report). Relevant to [[04 Evidence/Biopsy Specimen Quality]], [[04 Evidence/Thermal Artifact]], and [[04 Evidence/Surgical Margins]] as a single, weak-tier, genuinely novel (thermal-alteration-absent) data point — not relevant to [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Coagulation Zone]] (coagulation is not addressed in this source at all).

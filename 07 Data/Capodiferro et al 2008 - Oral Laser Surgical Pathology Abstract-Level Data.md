# Laser Operating Parameters

Record values exactly as reported. Use `UNKNOWN` when a value is not reported; do not infer missing parameters.

**Vault classification: CORE biopsy evidence, ABSTRACT-LEVEL ONLY.** This is a real human diode-laser excisional/incisional biopsy pathway (n=25) directly on point for this vault's central research question, and remains this vault's only identified candidate for a coagulation-zone finding — but no full-text PDF/HTML is cached in this vault's local Zotero library (the literature note carries only a bare Zotero item link, no attached file, unlike Wilder-Smith et al. 1995, Li et al. 2022, and Capodiferro et al. 2009). No PMCID is available for this PMID via PubMed's ID-conversion lookup, and no DOI exists in PubMed's record. Per explicit instruction, this source remains abstract-only / unprocessed until full text is truly available — it is **not** treated as resolved by [[07 Data/Capodiferro et al 2009 - Diode Laser Chondroid Lipoma Case Report Data|Capodiferro et al. 2009]], a separate paper by the same author group with a different design (n=1 case report vs. this paper's n=25 series) and a different, non-overlapping power range.

## Notes

- FACT: This remains the vault's only identified candidate specific to [[03 Concepts/Glossary/Coagulation Zone]] — no other source in this vault's biopsy-adjacent literature links protein coagulation to a power-density threshold in its own abstract or full text.
- FACT: This is a real, if abstract-level-only, human diagnostic-biopsy-and-excision pathway (25 patients, both benign and premalignant/malignant lesions), which places it in this vault's CORE biopsy evidence tier by subject matter — but its evidentiary weight is capped by the complete absence of full-text detail: no wavelength, no fiber diameter, no CW/PW mode, no per-patient power value, and no quantified diagnostic-yield percentage are available from the abstract alone.
- FACT: Per explicit instruction, this source is **not** to be treated as resolved, supplemented, or replaced by [[07 Data/Capodiferro et al 2009 - Diode Laser Chondroid Lipoma Case Report Data|Capodiferro et al. 2009]] (same author group, but a separate n=1 case report at a single fixed 2.5 W setting, which does not mention coagulation at all). The two papers are recorded as entirely independent sources in this vault.
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: Whether the abstract's "high power density output" threshold for coagulation is a set/console value or an independently measured one cannot be determined without full text. Whether the abstract's whole-cohort "no alterations were found" statement corresponds to 25/25 (100%) diagnostic success, or something less than that, is likewise unverifiable from the abstract alone.
- Limitations (source-stated and vault-noted): preliminary study; abstract-level only — no full-text PDF/HTML is cached in this vault's local Zotero library (no attached file on the literature note, unlike the other three sources processed alongside it in this update); no PMCID exists for this PMID (confirmed via PubMed's ID-conversion lookup); no DOI is available in PubMed's record; a targeted PubMed/PMC search for a companion open-access copy did not locate one.
- HYPOTHESIS: UNKNOWN — no formal hypothesis stated beyond the preliminary study's own descriptive aim.

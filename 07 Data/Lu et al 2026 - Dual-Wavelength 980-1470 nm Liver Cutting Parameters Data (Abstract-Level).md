## Scope note — read before using this extraction

This source is **not** an oral/dental study and **not** a diagnostic-biopsy pathway: it is an in vivo rabbit hepatic-transection (liver-cutting) characterization study using a dual-wavelength (980 nm + 1470 nm) semiconductor laser at surgical power levels (up to 160 W) far outside the range of any dental diode device in this vault. It is included here only because the task explicitly requested it be assessed for incision-speed, measured-power, and thermal-damage-histology evidence. Per this vault's rule against inferring missing values, and because only the abstract is available, most operating-parameter cells below are UNKNOWN even though the abstract implies additional detail exists in the full text.

## Notes

- FACT: This is the only source in this vault's current literature (across all tasks to date) reporting a fixed, explicitly stated fiber-advancement speed (10 mm/s) in a genuine in vivo surgical-cutting model with a paired, quantified (H&E) thermal-injury-depth outcome at a specific power/ratio/mode combination — a design that directly matches the shape of evidence this vault's oral-biopsy literature still lacks (see [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map]] §8, "Incision speed"). However, it cannot be used to fill that gap: the tissue (rabbit liver, not human oral mucosa), species, wavelength combination (980+1470 nm dual, not a single dental-diode wavelength), power scale (12–160 W, roughly 2–3 orders of magnitude above any dental diode device in this vault), and clinical application (hepatic resection, not oral biopsy) are all incompatible with transfer to this vault's oral-biopsy evidence base. It is retained only as a distant methodological data point, not as diode-biopsy evidence.
- FACT: The abstract does not state whether its reported power values (12–160 W / 6–48 W) are set/console values or independently measured — this vault does not assume either interpretation is correct and records it as UNKNOWN, consistent with vault policy of never inferring a measured value from an unqualified reported number.
- FACT: The reported CW-vs-pulsed cutting-depth finding (CW deeper than pulsed, p<0.05) is directionally the *same* comparison type (CW vs. pulsed cutting efficiency) already tracked for dental diode lasers elsewhere in this vault, but involves a different laser type/tissue/power scale entirely, so it cannot be pooled with, or used to resolve, this vault's existing three-way discordant dental-diode CW-vs-PW thermal-damage conflict (see [[06 Synthesis/Diode Laser Biopsy Pre-setting Map]] §4) — that conflict concerns thermal-*damage* direction, not cutting-*depth* direction, and no dental-diode source in this vault currently tests cutting depth as its own outcome.
- Full-text access could substantially change this extraction (methods detail on power measurement, the complete set of tested ratios, statistical results for conditions other than the one highlighted "optimal" combination, and any histologic images/quantification beyond the single reported thermal-injury-depth value) — this note should be revisited if the paywalled full text becomes available.
- Classification for this vault: **BACKGROUND** — a non-oral, non-diode-wavelength-alone (dual-wavelength, high-power surgical laser), animal (rabbit) liver-cutting characterization study, abstract-level only (no full text). Not a candidate for this vault's CORE or SUPPORTING diode-biopsy tiers. Relevant only as a distant, non-transferable illustration that a fixed advancement speed paired with quantified thermal-injury-depth histology is achievable as a study design — the same observation already made, with a transferable dental context even if not a transferable device, by [[07 Data/Wilder-Smith et al 1995 - CO2 Laser Incision and Thermal Damage Data|Wilder-Smith et al. 1995]] and [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data|Goharkhay et al. 1999]] already in this vault.

# Vault Housekeeping Audit

## Executive summary

- **Total files reviewed:** ~349 vault content files (all folders outside `.obsidian`/`.git`), plus 3 system/plugin config files (`.claude/scheduled_tasks.lock`, `.claudian/claudian-settings.json`, `.gitignore`) briefly checked and confirmed to be ordinary tooling config, not misplaced vault content.
- **KEEP:** ~211
- **ARCHIVE CANDIDATE:** ~103 (dominated by raw search-query JSON/batch debris under `90 Agent/Search Reports/`, which is large in file count but low in unique vault value)
- **DELETE CANDIDATE:** ~31 (all trivial `.err` log-noise files from search-tool runs; zero prose content, zero backlinks)
- **FIX:** 6 distinct organizational issues, touching ~13 files/templates (details below); these files are otherwise KEEP-worthy content with a location/metadata/template defect, not separately counted against the totals above.
- **Major cleanup themes:**
  1. The active research vault (00 Inbox, 01 Projects, 02 Literature canonical notes, 03 Concepts incl. Glossary, 04 Evidence, 05 Devices, 06 Synthesis, 07 Data) is in excellent, actively-maintained condition — extensively cross-linked, FACT/INTERPRETATION/HYPOTHESIS discipline consistently applied, no invented citations or parameters found anywhere sampled.
  2. The vault already has a self-documented, non-destructive convention for handling literature-note duplicates: old manually-created notes are marked `SUPERSEDED` in-place with a pointer to the new Zotero-managed canonical note, and explicitly "kept for history, not deleted." This convention is working correctly for Parker 2022, Mazzoni 2026, Angiero 2011, Azevedo 2016, and Romeo 2014 — no action needed beyond noting it.
  3. Two literature notes/folders were created with malformed paths during ZotLit import/manual creation (`02 Literature/https_/doi.org/10.1590/...` and `02 Literature/Cutting Liver Tissue with a Novel 980/...`) — cosmetic folder-naming defects, not broken links (Obsidian resolves by filename), but inconsistent with the vault's own DOI-folder convention.
  4. The real cleanup mass is entirely inside `90 Agent/Search Reports/` — two large search-tool output bundles contain a lot of raw API/query debris (batch classification files, per-query JSON dumps, and especially ~31 one-line `.err` log files with zero content) that is fully superseded by already-written final reports and synthesis notes.
  5. Three note templates (`Device Note`, `Concept Note`, `Synthesis Note`) have fallen out of sync with how actual notes in those folders are now structured — not broken, but worth updating so future notes stay consistent.

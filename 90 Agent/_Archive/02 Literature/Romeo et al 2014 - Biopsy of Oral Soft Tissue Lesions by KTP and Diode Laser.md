# Literature Note

## Source

- Citation: Romeo U, Russo C, Palaia G, Lo Giudice R, Del Vecchio A, Visca P, Migliau G, De Biase A. "Biopsy of different oral soft tissues lesions by KTP and diode laser: histological evaluation." *TheScientificWorldJournal.* 2014;2014:761704. DOI: [10.1155/2014/761704](https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/761704). PMID: 25405233. PMCID: PMC4227390.
- Source type: Original clinical case series (in vivo human oral biopsies)
- Source link: https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/761704
- Source locator: Full text (Introduction; Materials and Methods; Results; Discussion; Conclusions), retrieved via PubMed Central (According to PubMed).

## Study

- Objective: To analyze tissue fragments removed by diode and KTP laser surgery, assessing epithelial and connective-tissue damage caused by thermal effects and its impact on histologic diagnosis.
- Design: Prospective clinical case series; single-blind histopathologic evaluation.
- Sample or material: 17 patients (8F/9M) with benign oral lesions; mostly excisional biopsies (some incisional, depending on lesion site/size); lesion size 0.5–1 cm diameter.
- Clinical or laboratory context: Real human oral-biopsy clinical pathway with a histopathologic diagnosis actually rendered — the vault's first processed source with a true diagnostic-yield outcome.

## Device and operating parameters

- Device: Diode laser (SOL, DenMat Italia, Italy), 808 nm, 2 W CW, fluence 2400 J/cm², fiber spot 320 μm; and KTP laser (SmartLite, DEKA, Italy), 532 nm, 1.5 W PW, fluence 212 J/cm², fiber spot 300 μm.
- Operating-parameter extraction: [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data]]

## Findings

- FACT: A certainty histopathologic diagnosis was achieved for all 17/17 specimens (100%), regardless of whether excised by diode or KTP laser.
- FACT: Mean peri-incisional margin damage (mm) by lesion type: mucocele 0.245±0.162 mm; fibroma 0.382±0.149 mm; hyperkeratosis/squamous hyperplasia 0.336±0.106 mm; oral lichen planus 0.473±0.105 mm; giant cell granuloma 0.182 mm; melanotic macula (diode 808 nm specifically) 0.149 mm.
- FACT: Greater inflammation/cellularity of the excised lesion (e.g., oral lichen planus) was associated with higher margin thermal-damage values than less-inflamed lesions (e.g., mucocele, melanotic macula).
- FACT: The authors recommend enlarging laser biopsy specimens by approximately 0.5 mm to compensate for thermal alteration, especially for inflammatory lesions such as oral lichen planus.
- FACT: The source states that many histologic artifacts observed were not due to the laser itself but to standard tissue fixation/sectioning/staining steps.
- FACT: The source's own Discussion cites Angiero et al.'s 608-case retrospective study (808 nm diode, 1.6–2.7 W CW, 320 μm fiber), reporting the same "46.15%... not achievable" diagnostic-failure figure below 3 mm specimen size and the ≥5 mm specimen-size recommendation independently retrieved by this vault from Angiero et al.'s own abstract — this is Romeo et al.'s own citation of Angiero et al., not independent vault verification, but the matching figures increase confidence in this vault's separate Angiero extraction.
- FACT: The Discussion also cites a different, earlier study by a different Romeo et al. group (not the present paper), comparing Er:YAG, Nd:YAG, Er,Cr:YSGG, and two diode wavelengths (808/980 nm) on pig tongue — that earlier study is not independently verified by this vault and must not be conflated with the present 2014 paper.
- INTERPRETATION (source's own): Laser-tissue interaction depends on operator-dependent factors (technique, application time, cutting distance from lesion margins) and operator-independent factors (wavelength, tissue optical properties); the source's own prior related work found KTP caused less thermal damage than diode.
- HYPOTHESIS: UNKNOWN.

## Limitations

- Small sample (n=17) with uneven device distribution across lesion types — most lesion types used diode almost exclusively; only mucocele and hyperkeratosis had any KTP cases mixed in — so the per-lesion-type damage averages are not a clean diode-vs-KTP comparison except for melanotic macula (diode only, explicitly stated) and fibroma/oral lichen planus/giant cell granuloma (diode only, per the Methods' device-per-lesion breakdown).
- Power values (2 W CW diode; 1.5 W PW KTP) are set/console values; no independent power-meter-verified measured output is reported.
- Contact vs. non-contact mode and fiber-tip initiation status are not stated in the retrieved text — marked UNKNOWN in the Data extraction.

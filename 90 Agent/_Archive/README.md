## Citation-mining note: Sattayut, "Research and Innovation in Orofacial Laserology"

Not archived (Cleanup Batch 4, 2026-09-15) — still in `02 Literature/Research and Innovation in Orofacial Laserology.md`. Classified **KEEP HISTORICAL**, not ARCHIVE, because only one of its two chased citations could be independently verified. Findings:

- **Mungmee A, Sattayut S.** *An In Vitro Study of the Effect of CO2 Laser Power Output on Ablative Properties in Porcine Tongue.* **VERIFIED** — According to PubMed, the actual publication is *Life (Basel)* 2023;13(1):162, [10.3390/life13010162](https://doi.org/10.3390/life13010162), PMID 36676111, PMCID PMC9861084 (open access, full text available) — not "*Laser Therapy* 2023;32(1):45–52" as the textbook's own reference list states; that citation detail is a textbook error. Content matches with high confidence (same authors, same topic, matching regression-equation description). CO2 laser (10,600 nm), ex vivo swine tongue, CW, 3–10 W, ablation depth/width only (no histopathology, no diode arm, no diagnostic outcome). Relevance: **METHODOLOGICAL PRECEDENT** (same role Wilder-Smith et al. 1995 already serves in this vault) — not diode-specific, not biopsy-oriented, so not ingested. Not yet in Zotero; a genuine, open-access, Zotero-worthy candidate for a future session if this vault wants another CO2 power-depth methodological precedent, but out of scope for this cleanup.
- **Puttapiban T, Praneetpolkrang W, Sattayut S** (2017), "*A comparative study of an immediate effect of CO2 laser and diode 980 nm laser on oral soft tissue: a porcine ex vivo study*," Faculty of Dentistry, Khon Kaen University. **UNVERIFIED** — not found via PubMed, CrossRef/DOI lookup, or general web search (institutional repository, Google Scholar). Appears to be an unpublished internal thesis/report with no discoverable record. Per vault policy, its content is not treated as confirmed evidence; this note remains the only place in the vault recording that this citation exists. This is the reason note 2 was kept rather than archived.

# Tip Initiation and Fiber Condition — Supporting Evidence

**SUPPORTING / BACKGROUND EVIDENCE.** This note is not part of the core dental set-power vs. measured-output evidence base ([[04 Evidence/Set Power vs Measured Output]], [[04 Evidence/Power Loss and Deviation Across Dental Diode Devices]]) and should not be merged with it. **Correction (see [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] and [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]):** an earlier version of this note stated that Al-Ani et al. and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. do not report fiber-tip initiation status at all. That was incomplete — both sources *do* report and deliberately control tip-initiation status (Al-Ani et al.: explicitly initiated/pre-charred tip; Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.: explicitly non-initiated bare fiber). What neither source does is pair that controlled variable with an independently measured, calibrated power-meter output value — all three sources here measure a tissue-effect proxy (histology or temperature), not delivered power. That distinction, not an absence of tip-status reporting, is the actual evidence gap and is preserved below.

## Sources

- Romanos GE, Estrin NE, Lesniewski A, McClain S, Hou W. "Penetration Depth of Initiated and Non-Initiated Diode Lasers in Bovine Gingiva." *Applied Sciences.* 2022;12(24):12771. DOI: [10.3390/app122412771](https://doi.org/10.3390/app122412771)
- Al-Ani AJ, Al-Alawi AS, Taher HJ. "Analysis of the Temperature Elevation of the Dual-Wavelength Diode Laser and the Er, Cr: YSGG Laser in Oral Soft Tissue Incisions." *J Lasers Med Sci.* 2023;14:e37. DOI: [10.34172/jlms.2023.37](https://doi.org/10.34172/jlms.2023.37)
- Gutiérrez-Corrales A, Rizcala-Orlando Y, Montero-Miralles P, Volland G, Gutiérrez-Pérez JL, Torres-Lagares D, Serrera-Figallo MA. "Comparison of diode laser - Oral tissue interaction to different wavelengths. In vitro study of porcine periodontal pockets and oral mucosa." *Med Oral Patol Oral Cir Bucal.* 2020;25(2):e224-e232. DOI: [10.4317/medoral.23317](https://doi.org/10.4317/medoral.23317)

## Claim

- Claim: Fiber-tip initiation status, and separately, wavelength and power/mode selection, each change the depth and severity of thermal tissue effect produced by a diode (or comparison solid-state) laser at a given nominal power setting, in ex vivo/in vitro oral soft-tissue models.
- Scope: Romanos et al. — ex vivo bovine gingiva, initiated vs. non-initiated tips, 445 nm and 970 nm, 2 W. Al-Ani et al. — ex vivo sheep tongue incisions, dual-wavelength (810+980 nm) diode vs. Er,Cr:YSGG. Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. — in vitro porcine periodontal pocket and oral mucosa, six wavelengths, 0.5–2.0 W. None is a live clinical dental patient study, and none reports a calibrated power-meter measurement of delivered output — all three measure a tissue-effect proxy (histology or temperature) instead.

## Supporting findings

- FACT: Romanos et al. compared 445 nm and 970 nm diode lasers, each in pulsed (35 Hz, 50% duty cycle) and continuous-wave mode, using both initiated and non-initiated fiber tips, all at a laser output setting of 2 W delivered through a 320 μm glass fiber, irradiating bovine gingiva ex vivo for 30 s buccal + 30 s lingual per tooth, then assessing collagen denaturation histologically (elastin Van Gieson stain, mixed-effect model with Tukey adjustment). [DOI: 10.3390/app122412771]
- FACT: Romanos et al. found the 445 nm wavelength groups had significantly higher penetration depth (of the histologically assessed thermal effect) than the 970 nm groups. [DOI: 10.3390/app122412771]
- FACT: Romanos et al. found initiated-tip groups tended toward lower penetration depth than non-initiated-tip groups, but for the 445 nm pulsed condition specifically, the initiated-vs-non-initiated difference was not statistically significant (p > 0.05). [DOI: 10.3390/app122412771]
- FACT: Romanos et al. found pulsed-mode settings produced less thermal penetration depth than continuous-wave mode at the same 2 W power output. [DOI: 10.3390/app122412771]
- FACT: Romanos et al. state that additional studies are needed to better compare initiated and non-initiated tips specifically for the 445 nm ("blue") laser. [DOI: 10.3390/app122412771]
- FACT: Al-Ani et al. compared a dual-wavelength (810+980 nm) diode laser at 1.5 W and 2.5 W continuous-wave settings against an Er,Cr:YSGG (2780 nm) laser at 2.5 W and 3.5 W pulsed-wave settings, making 50 incisions across 6 freshly dissected sheep tongues, and measured initial temperature, maximum temperature, and temperature rise. [DOI: 10.34172/jlms.2023.37]
- FACT: Al-Ani et al. found the lowest mean temperature rise with the 2.5 W Er,Cr:YSGG laser and the highest mean temperature rise with the 2.5 W diode laser. [DOI: 10.34172/jlms.2023.37]
- FACT: Al-Ani et al. found statistically significant differences (p < 0.05) in temperature rise between most laser/power groups, except between the 1.5 W diode laser and the 3.5 W Er,Cr:YSGG laser, which did not differ significantly (p = 0.100). [DOI: 10.34172/jlms.2023.37]
- FACT: Al-Ani et al. explicitly used an initiated (pre-charred) diode fiber tip: the protocol set the laser to 0.5 W, applied the fiber to articulating paper, and activated it until perforation and a flame/char formed, before each power setting was used for incisions; the power meter (PINTUDY) was used only to standardize the set power via the fiber-optic cut, and the study reports no independently measured output value distinct from the set value. [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]]
- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. compared six wavelengths (445, 532 [KTP], 810, 980, 1064, 1470 nm) across a continuous-wave power range of 0.5–2.0 W, making handmade incisions in porcine periodontal pockets and oral mucosa ex vivo, then histologically measuring lost-tissue area, thermal-damage area, and necrosis area. [DOI: 10.4317/medoral.23317]
- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. found statistically significant differences between the 1470 nm and 810 nm groups for thermal damage and necrosis in periodontal-pocket tissue. [DOI: 10.4317/medoral.23317]
- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. found the 1064 nm group differed significantly from the others for lost-tissue area in oral-mucosa tissue. [DOI: 10.4317/medoral.23317]
- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. found that, for all three measured variables (lost tissue, thermal damage, necrosis), values were higher at 2.0 W than at 0.5 W, regardless of wavelength. [DOI: 10.4317/medoral.23317]
- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. conclude that, in their tissue model, an 810 nm diode laser at 0.5–2 W was the wavelength/power combination least likely to cause thermal damage to peri-incisional margins for oral soft-tissue biopsy. [DOI: 10.4317/medoral.23317]
- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. deliberately used a non-initiated ("non-initialized") bare fiber, explicitly stated to "ensure a pure tissue interaction... without the hot effect of initialized fibres"; a fiber-tip power-meter measurement was performed before each incision to standardize set parameters, but no resulting numeric measured-output value or set-vs-measured deviation was published. [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]

## Conflicting findings

- NEEDS VERIFICATION (corrected from an earlier version of this note): Al-Ani et al. and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. *do* report and deliberately control fiber-tip initiation status (initiated vs. non-initiated, respectively) as an explicit methodological choice. What neither reports is an independently measured, calibrated power-meter output value paired with that status — both used a power meter only to set/standardize power before the experiment, not to verify delivered output afterward. The gap is therefore "tip-status-plus-measured-power in one source," not "no tip-status reporting." [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- FACT / internal inconsistency in Romanos et al., not resolved by this vault: the study's own Abstract states initiated-tip groups "tended to display a lower penetration depth than noninitiated groups," but its own Table 1 data show the opposite wherever a significant difference was found (445 nm CW: initiated 0.35 mm > non-initiated 0.22 mm; 970 nm CW: initiated 0.20 mm > non-initiated 0.131 mm; 970 nm Pulsed: initiated 0.10 mm > non-initiated 0.01 mm), and the Discussion states the 970 nm initiated group showed "significantly greater" penetration depth than non-initiated — directly contradicting the Abstract's general directional claim. Both the Abstract's stated claim and the Table 1/Discussion data are preserved here as reported, without silently reconciling them. NEEDS VERIFICATION. [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]]
- NEEDS VERIFICATION: No explicit p-value comparing initiated vs. non-initiated tips within continuous-wave mode is reported by Romanos et al. — Table 1's p-values compare wavelengths (445 nm vs. 970 nm) within a given tip-initiation status, not initiated vs. non-initiated within a given wavelength; the Discussion's claim of a "significant" CW initiated-vs-non-initiated difference (970 nm) is not traceable to an explicit p-value in the extracted data. [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]]
- The three sources do not disagree with one another on wavelength/power effects — each measures a different independent variable as its primary focus (tip initiation vs. laser type/power vs. wavelength/power) — so beyond Romanos et al.'s internal inconsistency above, there is no cross-source contradiction to preserve, only an unfilled evidence gap linking tip condition to a directly measured power value.

## Assessment

- INTERPRETATION: In ex vivo/in vitro oral and gingival tissue models, both fiber-tip initiation status (Romanos et al.) and wavelength/power-mode selection (Al-Ani et al.; Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.) independently change the thermal or histological tissue effect produced at a fixed nominal power setting. This supports treating tip condition as a plausible confounding variable for delivered-effect (not necessarily delivered-power) inconsistency, but no source here measures delivered power directly, so extending this to the set-power vs. measured-output question in [[04 Evidence/Power Loss and Deviation Across Dental Diode Devices]] is INTERPRETATION, not direct evidence. [DOI: 10.3390/app122412771] [DOI: 10.34172/jlms.2023.37] [DOI: 10.4317/medoral.23317]
- HYPOTHESIS: If tip-initiation status changes effective delivered energy the way Romanos et al.'s penetration-depth results suggest, a non-initiated (or partially conditioned) tip could be part of the explanation for device-level output deviations reported elsewhere in this vault's core dental-diode evidence — this link is untested by any source currently in the vault and remains a hypothesis.
- Limitations: Romanos et al. is a single ex vivo bovine-gingiva model at one fiber diameter (320 μm) and one power setting (2 W), assessing histological penetration depth rather than delivered power, and carries the unresolved abstract-vs-data internal inconsistency noted above. Al-Ani et al. and Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. use ex vivo/in vitro animal tissue (sheep tongue; porcine periodontal pocket and mucosa) and measure temperature or histological damage, not delivered power; both control tip-initiation status but neither reports the resulting measured-output value from their own fiber-tip power-meter check. [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] [[07 Data/Al-Ani et al 2023 - Dual-Wavelength Diode Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- Uncertainty: NEEDS VERIFICATION — whether tip-initiation status changes measured/delivered power output (as opposed to tissue penetration depth, which is what Romanos et al. actually measured) is not established by any source in this note. Romanos et al.'s own direction of effect (initiated vs. non-initiated) is itself internally inconsistent between its Abstract and its Table 1/Discussion, so even the tissue-effect direction is NEEDS VERIFICATION, not settled.
- Possible explanations for disagreement: Not applicable across sources — no cross-source conflict exists between these three studies; the within-source Romanos et al. abstract-vs-data inconsistency has no stated explanation in the source itself.
- Research gaps: NEEDS VERIFICATION — a dental diode study pairing tip-initiation status with a calibrated power-meter measurement of delivered output (rather than a tissue-effect proxy such as histology or temperature) has not been identified in this vault. Separately, Romanos et al. and the core-evidence source [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] both use the same device model (SIROLaser Blue, Dentsply Sirona, 445 nm/970 nm), but Xue et al. measured only an uninitiated tip's power deviation and Romanos et al. measured only tissue penetration depth (not power) for both tip states — so this device-model overlap cannot be used to link tip-initiation status to measured power deviation. [[07 Data/Romanos et al 2022 - Initiated vs Non-Initiated Diode Laser Penetration Depth]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- หมายเหตุนี้เป็น **หลักฐานสนับสนุน/ภูมิหลัง (SUPPORTING / BACKGROUND EVIDENCE)** ไม่ใช่ส่วนหนึ่งของชุดหลักฐานหลักเรื่องค่ากำลังตั้งหน้าปัดเทียบกับกำลังขาออกที่วัดได้ของเลเซอร์ไดโอดทางทันตกรรม
- Romanos et al. (2022) เปรียบเทียบปลายไฟเบอร์แบบ initiated (ผ่านการเตรียมปลาย/ทำให้ดำ) กับ non-initiated ที่ความยาวคลื่น 445 nm และ 970 nm กำลัง 2 W พบว่าความลึกของผลกระทบทางความร้อนในเหงือกวัวมักจะต่ำกว่าเมื่อใช้ปลายแบบ initiated ยกเว้นในโหมดพัลส์ 445 nm ที่ไม่ต่างกันอย่างมีนัยสำคัญ
- (แก้ไขจากฉบับก่อนหน้า) Al-Ani et al. (2023) และ Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. (2020) รายงานและควบคุมสถานะ initiated/non-initiated ของปลายไฟเบอร์จริง (Al-Ani: initiated; Gutiérrez-Corrales: non-initiated) แต่ทั้งสองแหล่งไม่ได้เผยแพร่ค่ากำลังขาออกที่วัดได้จริงจากเพาเวอร์มิเตอร์คู่กับสถานะนั้น จึงยังถือเป็นข้อมูลภูมิหลัง ไม่ใช่หลักฐานกำลังขาออกโดยตรง
- พบข้อขัดแย้งภายในงานของ Romanos et al. เอง: บทคัดย่อระบุว่าปลาย initiated ให้ความลึกต่ำกว่า non-initiated แต่ตาราง 1 และบทอภิปรายของงานวิจัยแสดงตรงกันข้ามในทุกคู่ที่มีนัยสำคัญทางสถิติ ข้อขัดแย้งนี้ยังไม่ได้รับการแก้ไขจากผู้เขียน (NEEDS VERIFICATION)
- ยังไม่มีการศึกษาที่วัดกำลังขาออกจริงด้วยเครื่องวัดกำลังที่ผ่านการสอบเทียบ (calibrated power meter) ควบคู่กับสถานะ initiated/non-initiated ของปลายไฟเบอร์ในเลเซอร์ไดโอดทางทันตกรรมโดยตรง

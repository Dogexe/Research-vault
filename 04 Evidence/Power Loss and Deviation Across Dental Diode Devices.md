# Evidence Note

## Claim

- Claim: Across the included dental diode studies, deviation between panel setting and measured output is not uniformly a power loss in one direction.
- Scope: Dental diode-device measurements reported by Parker et al. 2022, Xue et al. 2022, Zegaib et al. 2011, and Kim et al. 2020.

## Supporting findings

- FACT: Parker et al. calculated percentage power loss from post-fibre emission relative to the laser control-panel value and report device-specific values in Table 1. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]]
- FACT: Parker et al. found statistically significant variation in percentage power deviation between devices and between emission-power levels. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]]
- FACT: Xue et al. reported continuous-wave signed mean-output deviations of +21%, +22%, and −8% for 445 nm, 970 nm, and 810 nm devices, respectively. [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- FACT: Xue et al. reported pulsed signed mean-output deviations of +37%, +26%, and −3% for 445 nm, 970 nm, and 810 nm devices, respectively. [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- FACT: Zegaib et al. reported output at the fiber tip below the 1.0 W panel reference in every measured condition: a mean 9% fibre-transmission loss before tissue contact, and progressive contact-induced loss reaching a mean 40.26% by the fourth application (study group), versus 0.14–1.58% in the non-contact control group. [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]]
- FACT: Kim et al. measured set vs. actual power for three separate dental diode devices under one shared protocol (same calibrated power meter, same fiber tip type, same three power settings) and found the deviation direction was device-dependent: K2 mobile (980 nm) measured +11.9% to +18.1% above its panel setting, while Epic 10 TM (940 nm) and Saeshin (980 nm) measured −13.9% to −27.3% below theirs. [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]

## Conflicting findings

- FACT: Parker et al. mainly found post-fibre output below panel settings, whereas Xue et al. reported both positive and negative deviations from preset power. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- FACT: Xue et al. describe their signed values as change versus preset, rather than a uniformly lower post-fibre output. [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- FACT: Zegaib et al.'s single-device result is directionally consistent with Parker et al. (output only below panel setting), not with Xue et al.'s mixed-sign pattern; this adds one more below-only device rather than resolving the Parker/Xue conflict. [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- FACT: Kim et al.'s mixed-direction result (one device above, two devices below panel setting) reproduces the Parker-vs-Xue directional conflict *within a single study using one shared measurement protocol*, which weakens the possibility that the Parker/Xue conflict is solely an artifact of differing measurement setups across separate studies. [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]] [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]

## Assessment

- INTERPRETATION: “Power loss” should remain the source’s own term; signed deviations above preset should not be converted into a loss value. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- HYPOTHESIS: UNKNOWN
- Limitations: Parker et al. span 38 units across 20 device types and use device-specific table values; Xue et al. examine SIROLaser Blue and Picasso devices at stipulated maximum settings; Zegaib et al. examine a single Biolase 810-nm unit under one clinical protocol (periodontal curettage), so its below-panel pattern cannot be generalized to other devices; Kim et al. examine three devices at three power levels each (in vitro, titanium-disc irradiation), the smallest and most tightly controlled dataset here, so its mixed-direction pattern is informative about method but not about device prevalence in the wider market. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]] [[07 Data/Zegaib et al 2011 - Fiber Tip Power Loss During Periodontal Curettage]] [[07 Data/Kim et al 2020 - Set vs Actual Power Across Three Dental Diode Lasers]]
- Uncertainty: NEEDS VERIFICATION — no shared measurement protocol or common device set is available across these sources for a direct pooled estimate.
- Possible explanations for disagreement: INTERPRETATION: Differences in device, wavelength, mode, setting, fibre configuration, and measurement protocol limit direct comparison. [[07 Data/Parker et al 2022 - Delivery Power Losses and Operating Parameters]] [[07 Data/Xue et al 2022 - Two Semiconductor Lasers Power Output]]
- Research gaps: NEEDS VERIFICATION — comparative studies should measure the same dental devices with standardized settings, delivery assemblies, and calibrated meters.

## สรุปภาษาไทย

- ข้อสรุปของหลักฐานนี้คือ ส่วนต่างระหว่างค่าตั้งหน้าปัด (panel setting) กับกำลังขาออกที่วัดได้ (measured output) ในอุปกรณ์เลเซอร์ไดโอดทางทันตกรรมแต่ละเครื่อง ไม่ได้มีทิศทางเดียวกันเสมอไป
- Parker et al. และ Zegaib et al. พบว่ากำลังขาออกที่วัดได้ต่ำกว่าค่าตั้งหน้าปัดเป็นส่วนใหญ่ (below panel setting)
- Xue et al. พบทั้งค่าที่สูงกว่าและต่ำกว่าค่าตั้ง (signed deviation ทั้งบวกและลบ) ขึ้นอยู่กับความยาวคลื่นและโหมดการทำงาน
- Zegaib et al. ศึกษาเพียงอุปกรณ์เดียว จึงเสริมทิศทาง "ต่ำกว่าค่าตั้ง" ของ Parker et al. แต่ไม่ได้แก้ข้อขัดแย้งกับผลของ Xue et al.
- Kim et al. วัดอุปกรณ์ 3 เครื่องด้วยโพรโทคอลและเครื่องวัดกำลังเดียวกัน พบทิศทางส่วนต่างที่ต่างกันในแต่ละเครื่อง (เครื่องหนึ่งสูงกว่าค่าตั้ง สองเครื่องต่ำกว่าค่าตั้ง) ซึ่งบ่งชี้ว่าความขัดแย้งระหว่าง Parker et al. กับ Xue et al. ไม่ได้เกิดจากความต่างของวิธีวัดระหว่างงานวิจัยเพียงอย่างเดียว
- ยังต้องการการศึกษาเปรียบเทียบเพิ่มเติมที่ใช้เครื่องมือวัดและโพรโทคอลเดียวกัน (NEEDS VERIFICATION)

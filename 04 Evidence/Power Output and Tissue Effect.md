# Power Output and Tissue Effect

**Status: PARTIAL EVIDENCE (updated 2026-09-14).** This note is the vault's central bridge node between its existing power-output/power-loss evidence and the biopsy-specimen-quality question in [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]]. The broad claim that no vault source pairs independently measured diode-laser output with histologically assessed tissue effect is now stale — [[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data|Hanke et al. 2021]], [[07 Data/Strakas et al 2023 - 940nm Diode Cutting Efficiency and Thermal Damage Data|Strakas et al. 2023]], [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data|Goharkhay et al. 1999]], and [[07 Data/Prado et al 2022 - Micro vs Super Pulsed Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Prado et al. 2022]] all demonstrate this combination. What remains unfilled is a narrower, project-specific gap — see [[#Assessment]] below.

**Role (2026-09-13 reframing): EXPERIMENTAL MECHANISM.** This note tracks the first link of the project's ex vivo chain (set power → measured delivered power) and where it does/does not connect to a tissue-effect outcome. Its clinical-source rows (Angiero, Romeo, Azevedo, Gambino) are secondary CLINICAL RELEVANCE context here, since none independently measures power.

## Claim

- Claim (revised): Independently measured diode-laser power output paired with histologically assessed tissue effect is **already demonstrated** in this vault by four ex vivo sources — [[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data|Hanke et al. 2021]] and [[07 Data/Strakas et al 2023 - 940nm Diode Cutting Efficiency and Thermal Damage Data|Strakas et al. 2023]] (measured output verified per-incision, plus a controlled 2 mm/s incision speed, plus histology), [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data|Goharkhay et al. 1999]] (wattmeter-measured output, plus a controlled 10 mm/s speed, plus histology), and [[07 Data/Prado et al 2022 - Micro vs Super Pulsed Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Prado et al. 2022]] (power-meter-verified output with numerical measured values reported, plus histology, but **no** reported incision speed — cutting was manual). What is **not** yet demonstrated by any of these four, and remains the project's own gap, is a **biopsy-oriented specimen-quality outcome framework** (margin readability, tissue architecture preservation, diagnostic-impact thermal artifact) combined with this same measured-power rigor, in an ex vivo oral soft-tissue model. Novelty is not claimed from measured power + histology alone, nor from measured power + speed + histology alone — see [[06 Synthesis/Novelty Matrix - Diode Laser Biopsy]].
- Scope: Dental/oral soft-tissue laser surgery, where "power output" means the vault's existing measured/delivered-power sense (see [[03 Concepts/Power Output]]), not just the panel setting.

## Supporting findings

- FACT: [[07 Data/Hanke et al 2021 - Eight-Wavelength Diode Cut Efficiency Data|Hanke et al. 2021]] and [[07 Data/Strakas et al 2023 - 940nm Diode Cutting Efficiency and Thermal Damage Data|Strakas et al. 2023]] independently verify delivered power with a calibrated meter (LabMax Top + PM10 detector) checked before every single incision (≥5% deviation triggers fiber-tip replacement), control incision speed mechanically at 2 mm/s, and quantify histologic thermal effect (cut depth/width, total interaction zone, 5-class morphology, efficiency factor γz) in porcine gingiva (oral tissue). Neither tabulates a distinct numerical measured-power value separate from the QC gate (`measured_power_value_reported: false`), and neither excises a lesion or uses a biopsy-oriented outcome set — both are cutting-efficiency characterization studies. See [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy#Hanke and Strakas as direct prior art]].
- FACT: [[07 Data/Goharkhay et al 1999 - Diode Laser Incision Depth and Collateral Damage Data|Goharkhay et al. 1999]] measured output directly at the fiber outflow with a wattmeter (0.5–4.5 W, explicitly stated to differ from the unreported console value), controlled incision speed at 10 mm/s (motorized handpiece, stopwatch-timed), and histologically quantified incision depth/width and collateral thermal-damage-zone width in ex vivo pig mandible mucosa (oral tissue). No lesion is excised and no margin-readability or tissue-architecture score is used.
- FACT: [[07 Data/Prado et al 2022 - Micro vs Super Pulsed Diode Laser Ex Vivo Thermal Damage Data|Prado et al. 2022]] independently verified output with a power meter (PM600) before each surgical procedure and reports the actual numerical measured values (1.2 W display → 1.0 W measured; 1.8 W display → 1.5 W measured; both ~17% under-delivery), paired with histologic thermal-damage area and depth measurement in ex vivo pig tongue (oral tissue), using an initiated tip. It does **not** report a controlled or measured incision speed — cutting was performed manually, and only elapsed excision time was recorded, not a mm/s value — and it does not excise a lesion, use a biopsy-oriented outcome framework, or report a margin-readability/tissue-architecture score. This is treated identically to Hanke/Strakas/Goharkhay: a measured-power + histology precedent, not a biopsy-oriented precedent, and its ≥1 mm healthy-margin recommendation is a margin-*width* recommendation derived from thermal-damage depth, not a margin-*readability* or specimen-quality score.

- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. found that, for all three measured tissue-effect variables (lost tissue area, thermal damage area, necrosis area), values were higher at 2.0 W set power than at 0.5 W, regardless of wavelength, in an in vitro porcine periodontal-pocket/mucosa model. [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]] — reused from [[04 Evidence/Tip Initiation and Fiber Condition - Supporting Evidence]].
- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. conclude that, in their tissue model, an 810 nm diode laser at 0.5–2 W was the wavelength/power combination least likely to cause thermal damage to peri-incisional margins "for oral soft-tissue biopsy" — the only vault source whose own stated conclusion uses the word "biopsy." [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- FACT: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.'s power values are set/panel values used to standardize the experiment; the source performed a fiber-tip power-meter check before each incision but did not publish the resulting measured-output value, so this is evidence about *set* power vs. tissue effect, not *measured/delivered* power vs. tissue effect. [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]]
- NEEDS VERIFICATION (abstract-level extraction now on file; full text still unavailable): Capodiferro et al. 2008 report that, in diode-laser-excised oral specimens (2–7 W), "changes induced by laser such as coagulation of proteins were present only with high power density output" — directly on-topic for this claim, but power appears to be a set/nominal value, not independently power-meter-verified, per the abstract. [[07 Data/Capodiferro et al 2008 - Oral Laser Surgical Pathology Abstract-Level Data]]
- FACT (new, this update): Two additional sources reinforce rather than close this gap. Capodiferro et al. 2009 (n=1 case, 880 nm, 2.5 W CW) reports a set/console power value only, with no independent power-meter verification, and no measurable tissue effect was found at that setting. Li et al. 2022 (mouse oral mucosa, diode 3.0 W pulsed) likewise reports a set/console power value only, with no independent power-meter verification. Neither independently measures power, so neither adds to the measured-power precedent already established by Hanke/Strakas/Goharkhay/Prado, and neither closes the project's remaining biopsy-oriented gap either. [[07 Data/Capodiferro et al 2009 - Diode Laser Chondroid Lipoma Case Report Data]] [[07 Data/Li et al 2022 - Diode vs Er-YAG vs Cold Scalpel Mice Oral Mucosa Data]]
- FACT, methodological only (new, this update): Wilder-Smith et al. 1995 independently measured delivered power with a calibrated power meter (Gentec PRJ-M) directly before each incision, paired with thermocouple and histologic thermal-damage measurement, in oral mucosa — the same measurement design later demonstrated for diode lasers by Hanke/Strakas/Goharkhay/Prado above, but here for CO2, not diode, lasers, and its devices do not overlap with any device in this vault's diode evidence base — it is recorded only as an earlier demonstration that the needed measurement design is achievable in oral mucosa. [[07 Data/Wilder-Smith et al 1995 - CO2 Laser Incision and Thermal Damage Data]]

- FACT: Four biopsy-specific sources processed since this note was created — Angiero et al. 2011 (1.6–2.7 W CW, average 2.5 W), Romeo et al. 2014 (2 W CW diode / 1.5 W PW KTP), Azevedo et al. 2016 (3.5 W PW / 3.5 W Boost PW diode), and Gambino et al. 2026 (2 W CW) — all report set/console power only, with no independent power-meter verification of delivered output, reinforcing rather than closing this gap. [[07 Data/Angiero et al 2011 - Diode Laser Biopsy Specimen Size and Diagnostic Yield]] [[07 Data/Romeo et al 2014 - Diode and KTP Laser Biopsy Margin Data]] [[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data]] [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data]]
- FACT: Gambino et al. 2026 found that, at one fixed set power (2 W CW), laser-treated tissue was statistically significantly thicker than untreated control tissue (p=0.021 epithelium, p=0.034 lamina propria) — the clearest statistically-tested tissue effect of a single set-power value in this vault's biopsy evidence, though it does not vary power and does not independently measure it. [[07 Data/Gambino et al 2026 - Diode Laser OCT and Histologic Thermal Damage Data]]
- FACT: Azevedo et al. 2016 found that a diode laser's higher "Boost" power/intensity setting (3.5 W Boost PW) produced greater thermal-damage extent (626.82 μm) than its standard setting at the same nominal 3.5 W label (456.15 μm PW) — a within-device, set-power-level tissue-effect gradient, though "Boost" is not further defined in the retrieved text and is not a calibrated/measured value. [[07 Data/Azevedo et al 2016 - Diode Laser Thermal Damage and Carbonization Data]]

## Conflicting findings

- None identified — no vault source contradicts the above. Measured delivered power paired with histologic tissue effect is no longer an absent study design (see [[#Claim]]); the remaining gap is the absence of a **biopsy-oriented specimen-quality outcome framework** combined with that same measured-power rigor, not a disagreement between existing sources.

## Assessment

- INTERPRETATION (revised 2026-09-14): Among the **biopsy-relevant/clinical** set-power sources specifically (Gutiérrez-Corrales et al., Angiero et al., Romeo et al., Azevedo et al., Gambino et al.), Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. is still the only one that varies power across a range against a tissue-effect outcome — and it uses set/panel power, not independently measured output. This vault cannot yet say whether the *measured-output* deviations documented in [[04 Evidence/Power Loss and Deviation Across Dental Diode Devices]] (which differ in direction and magnitude by device) would change the power–tissue-effect relationship Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. report for set power. This is now a narrower claim than before: Hanke et al. 2021, Strakas et al. 2023, and Goharkhay et al. 1999 *do* vary power across a tested range with independently measured output and a histologic outcome — but in non-biopsy, non-lesional ex vivo tissue, not the biopsy-relevant/clinical set-power sources this bullet is about. [[07 Data/Gutierrez-Corrales et al 2020 - Diode Wavelength Operating Parameters]] [[04 Evidence/Power Loss and Deviation Across Dental Diode Devices]]
- HYPOTHESIS (scope narrowed 2026-09-14): If a device's measured output deviates substantially from its set value (as several devices do, per [[04 Evidence/Power Loss and Deviation Across Dental Diode Devices]]), the actual tissue effect delivered at a given panel setting could differ from what a set-power-only **biopsy/clinical** study like Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.'s, Angiero et al.'s, Romeo et al.'s, Azevedo et al.'s, or Gambino et al.'s would predict. This remains untested for those specific set-power biopsy/clinical sources — it is not answered by Hanke/Strakas/Goharkhay/Prado, since none of those four uses a biopsy-oriented outcome or the same devices as the clinical sources.
- Limitations: Gutiérrez-Corrales et al. is in vitro porcine tissue, not human oral biopsy, and reports set power only. Capodiferro et al. 2008 is abstract-level only (no full text available) and likely also reports set/nominal power; Capodiferro et al. 2009 is n=1; Li et al. 2022 is a mouse wound model, not a diagnostic biopsy; Wilder-Smith et al. 1995 is CO2, not diode.
- Uncertainty: NEEDS VERIFICATION for whether Gutiérrez-Corrales et al.'s and Capodiferro et al. 2008's power values were independently confirmed by a calibrated meter at the point of tissue contact (as opposed to a pre-experiment standardization check) — this uncertainty does not apply to Hanke/Strakas/Goharkhay/Prado, whose measurement methodology is confirmed in [[#Claim]] above.
- Possible explanations for disagreement: Not applicable — no cross-source conflict, only an unfilled gap.
- Research gaps (revised): The combination of independently measured delivered power, histologically assessed tissue effect, and (in Hanke/Strakas/Goharkhay) a controlled incision speed is no longer an open gap — see the four sources in [[#Claim]] above. What remains open is narrower: a study that applies this same measured-power rigor to a **biopsy-oriented specimen-quality outcome framework** (margin readability, tissue architecture preservation, diagnostic-impact thermal artifact) in an ex vivo oral soft-tissue model, ideally with a reported/controlled incision speed as well (only Hanke/Strakas/Goharkhay report speed; Prado does not). This is the same gap tracked in [[06 Synthesis/Novelty Matrix - Diode Laser Biopsy]] and [[01 Projects/Diode Laser Biopsy]] — novelty is not claimed from measured power + histology (or + speed) alone.

- Capodiferro et al. (ยังไม่ได้ประมวลผลเต็มรูปแบบ พบจากบทคัดย่อเท่านั้น) รายงานว่าการแข็งตัวของโปรตีนพบเฉพาะที่กำลังสูงเท่านั้น แต่ยังไม่ยืนยันว่าเป็นค่าที่วัดได้จริงหรือค่าตั้ง
